# Auto Interview AI Hiring Index – July 2026

Every quarter we turn anonymized platform signals into a field report for job seekers. This July 2026 edition distills **14,287 ATS scans**, **6,412 job-matching sessions**, and **1,903 cover-letter drafts** completed on Auto Interview AI between April 1 and June 30, 2026.

> **TL;DR:** Product talent is still under-keyworded for AI initiatives, secondary U.S. metros are out-hiring the Bay Area, and candidates who follow up the ATS report with a resume refresh see a 21-point gain.

---

## Key Findings at a Glance

- **Average ATS score = 63/100**. PM resumes that explicitly mention _AI safety_ or _responsible deployment_ jump to 78+.
- **Top missing keywords:** “Experiment design,” “AI roadmap,” “cross-functional pods,” “LTV/CAC,” and “go-to-market feedback loops” appear in \<30% of scanned resumes.
- **Fastest-growing AI job hubs:** Austin, Toronto, Bengaluru, Salt Lake City, and Raleigh outpaced SF Bay Area by posted roles per capita.
- **Follow-through matters:** Users who clicked from the ATS report into the resume builder and downloaded a refreshed version were **3.4×** more likely to return for a second scan, indicating higher engagement (and recruiter replies).
