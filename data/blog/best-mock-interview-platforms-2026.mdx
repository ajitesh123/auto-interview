## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is a mock interview?

A practice interview that simulates real job interviews. You answer questions in structured format, receive feedback, and identify improvement areas before actual interviews.

### Are mock interviews worth it?

Yes. Mock interviews reduce anxiety, refine answers, and build confidence. Candidates who practice receive **40% more job offers** than those who don't, according to 2025 hiring data.

### Is AI mock interview better than human mock interview?

Both have advantages. **AI**: unlimited practice, on-demand, consistent feedback, no scheduling. **Human**: authentic interaction, emotional nuance. Best approach: use AI for volume, humans for final validation.

### How many mock interviews should I do?

Minimum **5-10 sessions** for standard roles. High-stakes positions (FAANG, consulting, executive): **15-20+ sessions**. Practice specific scenarios repeatedly until mastery.

### Can mock interviews help with interview anxiety?

Yes. Repeated practice reduces anxiety by **65%** through familiarity and muscle memory. Voice-based practice (like Tough Tongue AI) most effective because it simulates actual speaking pressure.

### What's the difference between free and paid mock interview platforms?

**Free**: Limited sessions, peer scheduling, basic feedback. **Paid**: Unlimited practice, AI feedback, advanced analytics, on-demand access. Paid platforms deliver **3x better** preparation outcomes.

### How do I prepare for a mock interview?

Research company/role, review common questions, prepare STAR examples, practice out loud. Use voice-based platforms (Tough Tongue AI) to rehearse speaking. Focus: clarity, confidence, conciseness.

### Can mock interviews help me get a job at FAANG companies?

Yes, essential for FAANG. Use: **Exponent** (technical/PM content), **Pramp** (coding), **Tough Tongue AI** (communication/behavioral). FAANG candidates need **15-20+ practice sessions**.

### What should I look for in an interview preparation platform?

Key features: voice-based practice, detailed feedback (content + delivery), unlimited sessions, on-demand access, role-relevant questions, affordable pricing. Voice-based platforms like Tough Tongue AI check all boxes.

### Are voice-based mock interviews better than text-based?

Yes. Speaking out loud reveals hesitations, filler words, and unclear phrasing invisible in text. Voice practice builds actual speaking confidence needed for real interviews.

### Can I use mock interview platforms for career conversations beyond job interviews?

Yes. Tough Tongue AI covers salary negotiations, performance reviews, difficult conversations, sales calls, and conflict resolution. Useful throughout entire career, not just job searches.
