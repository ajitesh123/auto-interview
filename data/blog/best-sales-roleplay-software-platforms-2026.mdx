## What is AI sales roleplay software?

It is software where a rep has a practice conversation, usually by voice, with an AI playing a buyer: a cold-call prospect, a sceptical CFO, a customer about to churn. When the call ends, the software scores it against criteria a manager sets (did the rep ask about budget, handle the price objection, book a next step) and keeps a record a manager can review.

It replaces the role-play a manager or peer would otherwise run, not the manager. Salesforce's survey of 4,050 sales professionals (August–September 2025) found 47% lack roleplay opportunities and 46% "rarely get feedback on their sales conversations". A tool gives every rep as many practice calls as they want; what it cannot do is decide what good looks like for your market. That part is still your rubric.

## Which features actually matter when you compare tools?

Five, in this order. Most lists compare latency and avatar quality; those matter less than whether reps will practise your real calls and whether a manager can trust the score.

1. **Scenarios from your own material.** A buyer built from your real objections beats a generic "busy CFO". Check whether the tool builds from call transcripts and recordings, or only from a typed description.
2. **Your rubric, weighted.** You should set what is scored and how much each item counts. Second Nature, for example, defaults to 70% knowledge and 30% style and lets managers change it; Showpad allows up to 10 weighted categories.
3. **Real-call scoring with the same scorecard.** If practice is scored one way and real calls another, you cannot see whether practice changed anything. 5 of 15 state they do both.
4. **Voice that fits your calls.** Phone-style voice for cold calling; video avatars for in-person or demo meetings. Ten of the 15 confirm the AI buyer speaks; four imply it; Highspot's page does not say.
5. **The paperwork:** SOC 2 or ISO 27001, where recordings are stored, and whether your data trains their models. Most practice platforms state SOC 2 or ISO 27001; Allego, Highspot and Showpad name no certification on their roleplay pages.

## Does AI roleplay practice actually work?

Practice with feedback works; that is well established. Whether a specific AI tool improves your team's win rate is not yet shown by independent research, so treat vendor case studies as claims and run your own pilot.

<SalesRoleplayEvidenceFigure />

The strongest evidence comes from fields that have used simulation for decades. A JAMA meta-analysis of 609 studies and 35,226 trainees found simulation-based training had large effects on knowledge and skills and smaller but clear effects on behaviour at work. A meta-analysis of behaviour modelling training (show the skill, then practise it with feedback, Taylor, Russ-Eft and Chan, 2005) found large effects on knowledge and skills and "modest" effects on job behaviour ([summary](https://qic-wd.org/node/682)). The pattern is the same in both: practice changes what people can do in a test more than what they do on the job, so the score inside the tool is not the result you are buying.

There is a counterweight. A 2014 meta-analysis found that deliberate practice explained "less than 1 percent of individual differences in performance in professions" ([Princeton](https://www.princeton.edu/news/2014/07/03/becoming-expert-takes-more-practice)). Practice helps; talent, territory and product still decide most of a quota.

On AI roleplay specifically, the first controlled study we found is in healthcare: practising with an AI patient improved communication skills in a randomised study ([Haut et al., 2025, preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02694)). In sales, Forrester's Peter Ostrow wrote in July 2026 that "high-performing reps are four times more likely to use AI role-play" ([Forrester](https://www.forrester.com/blogs/stop-running-batting-practice-in-sales-ai-role-play-is-the-new-pitching-machine/)); the post does not name the survey behind the figure.

## How do you run a two-week AI roleplay pilot?

Pick two tools from the finder, give each the same three real calls, and decide on what the reps do on real calls, not on the score inside the tool.

Copy this scorecard into a sheet before you start. Fill one column per tool.

| Pilot question                         | How to measure                                                       | Pass mark (set yours)                     |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Did reps use it without being chased?  | Practice minutes per rep per week, from the tool's report            | Most reps hit the weekly target           |
| Does the AI score match your managers? | A manager scores 5 sessions blind; compare with the tool             | Agrees on most items                      |
| Did scores rise on the 3 scenarios?    | First vs last attempt per rep                                        | Clear rise for most reps                  |
| Did anything change on real calls?     | One metric you already track: e.g. conversations that book a meeting | Moves the right way, or too early to tell |
| Can you build a new scenario alone?    | Time for a manager to build the 4th scenario                         | Under an hour                             |
| What will year one cost?               | The quote, at your seats, in writing                                 | Within budget                             |

Ask every vendor these five questions, in writing, before you compare quotes:

- [ ] What is the price per seat per year at our team size, and what is the minimum contract?
- [ ] Is practice metered (sessions, minutes) and what happens when we run out?
- [ ] Can you build a scenario from our call recordings, and who owns those recordings?
- [ ] Do you score real customer calls with the same rubric, on which plan?
- [ ] Is our data used to train your models, and where is it stored?

Before you upload real calls, tell your team and your customers how recordings are used. The UK regulator says: "You must make sure you inform workers of any call monitoring in your privacy information" ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/employment/monitoring-workers/specific-data-protection-considerations-for-different-ways-or-methods-of-monitoring-workers/)). In India, the DPDP Act lists employment purposes as a legitimate use without consent ([Section 7(i)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)), but your customers' voices on those recordings need their own basis.

### When should you not buy AI roleplay software?

- **You have no talk track or rubric yet.** The tool will score reps against something; write what good looks like first.
- **The problem is leads, not skills.** If reps are good on calls but have too few, fix pipeline first.
- **No manager will look at the sessions.** Reps practise more when someone notices; budget an hour a week of manager review.
- **Two or three reps and a manager with time.** A weekly live roleplay costs nothing but an hour.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AI sales roleplay software?

There is no neutral ranking: most "best" lists are published by a vendor that ranks itself first. Choose by situation: a practice platform such as Hyperbound, Outdoo, Exec or Tough Tongue AI (our product) for most teams without a suite; your suite's module if you already use Mindtickle, Allego, Highspot or Showpad; Quantified for pharma; Zenarate for contact centres.

### How much does AI sales roleplay software cost?

Only 4 of 15 tools publish a paid price (13 Nov 2026): Exec \$20 or \$39 per seat a month billed annually; Yoodli \$8 or \$20 a month for individuals; Trellus \$149.99 per seat a month with its dialer; Tough Tongue AI from \$16.67 a month billed yearly, teams \$299–\$999 a month. For 15 reps that is \$3,588 to \$26,998.20 a year at list price. The rest quote.

### Is there a free AI sales roleplay tool?

Five tools let you start free without a sales call: Hyperbound (free plan with 45 prebuilt roleplays), Outdoo (60 minutes), Exec (5 sessions per user a year), Yoodli (5 lifetime sessions) and Tough Tongue AI (25 minutes, our product). Free plans limit custom scenarios, scorecards or integrations.

### Can I use ChatGPT or Claude for sales roleplay?

Yes, for one rep rehearsing one call: give it the buyer's role and objections and talk to it in voice mode. What it does not do is score every rep against the same rubric, keep a record a manager can review, or build buyers from your call recordings. That is what the paid tools add.

### What are the best Hyperbound alternatives?

Tools that, like Hyperbound, state both practice and real-call scoring on their own pages: Outdoo, PitchMonster, Exec and Tough Tongue AI (our product). For avatars and LMS delivery, Second Nature; for EU data residency, PitchMonster or Retorio. Hyperbound publishes no paid price, so get its quote alongside one of these.
