### What GST do you pay on sales training?

18%. The GST Council lists "commercial training and coaching services" under SAC 999293, in the education services heading taxed at 18%, and its education flyer says training by private coaching institutes is not covered by the education exemption. The exemption that does exist (Notification 12/2017, entry 69) covers NSDC, Sector Skill Councils and their approved training partners only when the training runs under an NSDC scheme. The GST rate changes that took effect on 22 September 2025 did not list training. A registered business can usually claim the GST back as input tax credit; ask your accountant. Software billed to you from outside India is taxed under reverse charge: you pay the 18% yourself.

## Does sales training stick? What the forgetting research really says

Some of it fades within days, so practice after a workshop matters. But two numbers you will see on almost every sales training page do not come from a study you can open.

- **"84% of training is forgotten within 90 days."** We traced it to an [ATD blog post from 5 March 2014](https://www.td.org/content/atd-blog/five-ways-to-improve-the-roi-of-your-sales-training-program), which attributes it to "one study" without naming it. We found no study behind it.
- **"Sales training returns 353%."** This traces to a secondhand claim about an Accenture study of training in general (\$4.53 back for each \$1), [repeated by Cornerstone](https://www.cornerstoneondemand.com/resources/article/5-steps-proving-roi-your-training-investment/), not to research on sales training. We could not find the original.

The real forgetting research is Hermann Ebbinghaus's 1885 experiments, [replicated in 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4492928) by Jaap Murre and Joeri Dros. Ebbinghaus measured "savings": "the relative amount of time saved on the second learning trial as a result of having had the first." He learned lists of nonsense syllables, not sales skills, so treat it as a direction, not a forecast.

The practical reading: most of the drop happens in the first day, and what is not used again fades further. Plan the weeks after a workshop (short practice sessions on the calls your reps actually make) before you sign for the workshop.

## Is there free sales training in India?

Yes, for entry-level roles. India's skills system has government-approved sales qualifications, run by Sector Skill Councils and delivered by approved training partners. Training under the Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) is described by the government as "free short duration skill training".

**Copy these 10 RFP questions:**

- [ ] What will my reps be able to do after the programme that they cannot do now? Show one example call.
- [ ] Who delivers it: name, city, and how many sales teams in my industry they have trained?
- [ ] Can you deliver in Hindi or [our language], in person in [our city]?
- [ ] Will you use our product, our buyers and our real objections, or a generic script?
- [ ] What is the price per participant or per batch, and is GST included?
- [ ] What happens in the 90 days after the workshop: practice, coaching, refreshers?
- [ ] How do you measure the change, and what would count as failure?
- [ ] Can we run a pilot with one team before rolling out?
- [ ] Which of your clients can we call as a reference?
- [ ] Who owns the materials and recordings after the programme?

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is the best sales training company in India?

There is no independent ranking; every ranked list we found was published by a company that ranks itself first. Choose by what you need: a trainer for a new method (Dale Carnegie India, Korn Ferry, Edstellar, Pinvista and others), enablement software for large field teams (Sharpsell.ai, Mindtickle), or AI roleplay for daily practice (Awarathon, Tough Tongue AI, our product).

### How much does sales training cost in India?

Most companies quote only. Published prices on 12 Nov 2026: Pinvista ₹1,00,000–₹5,00,000+ per team programme; The Knowledge Academy ₹1,54,995 for a one-day online course plus 18% GST; Tough Tongue AI (our product) from \$20 a month for AI roleplay. Add 18% GST to any commercial training.

### Is GST charged on corporate sales training in India?

Yes, 18%. Commercial training and coaching (SAC 999293) is not covered by the education exemption. Training by NSDC-approved partners is exempt only under NSDC schemes. Registered businesses can usually claim input tax credit.

### Which sales training companies teach in Hindi?

Of the 17 we checked, five say so on their pages: Edstellar (10+ languages including Hindi), Pinvista (Hindi and English), Sharpsell.ai (Hindi, English, Hinglish), Awarathon (22+ languages including Hindi) and SmartWinnr (Indian languages). Ask any trainer directly; many deliver in Hindi without saying so online.

### Is online sales training as good as in-person training in India?

It depends on what you need. A method can be taught well live online; practice needs each rep to speak, which online roleplay and AI practice do at scale. In-person works best for a kick-off or a team that has never trained together.
