## Virtual vs. In-Person Training: Making the Right Choice

The debate between virtual and in-person training continues, but research suggests that **blended approaches deliver optimal results**:

### Virtual Training Advantages:

- Cost efficiency (reduced travel and venue expenses)
- Flexibility and accessibility for global teams
- Scalability for large organizations
- Easy content customization

### Virtual Training Challenges:

- Lower engagement levels without proper design
- Technology barriers and connectivity issues
- Limited hands-on learning opportunities
- Reduced interpersonal connections

### In-Person Training Advantages:

- Higher engagement and participation
- Superior for hands-on learning and role-playing
- Stronger social interaction and team building
- Immediate feedback and real-time adaptability

### In-Person Training Challenges:

- Higher costs for logistics and travel
- Limited flexibility for distributed teams
- Scheduling complexity

**Best Practice:** Most effective organizations adopt a **blended approach** that combines in-person kickoffs for relationship building, virtual modules for knowledge transfer, and ongoing digital reinforcement for skill application.

### What is the difference between sales training and sales enablement?

While related, they serve different purposes:

**Sales Training:**

- Focuses on skill development and knowledge transfer
- Event-based (courses, workshops, bootcamps)
- Teaches techniques, methodologies, and best practices
- Typically conducted periodically
- Example: 3-day negotiation skills workshop

**Sales Enablement:**

- Provides ongoing resources, tools, and content
- Continuous process supporting the entire sales cycle
- Includes CRM systems, sales playbooks, content libraries, battle cards
- Always accessible and integrated into daily work
- Example: Sharpsell.ai platform with real-time product illustrations

**Best Results**: Combine both - training builds foundational skills, enablement ensures those skills are applied consistently with the \right tools and resources.
