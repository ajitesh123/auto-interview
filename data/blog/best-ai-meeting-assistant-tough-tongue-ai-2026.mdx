## The Core Capabilities That Make Tough Tongue AI the Best

### 1. Real-Time Note Creation During the Call

Most AI meeting tools process audio after the meeting ends. Tough Tongue AI creates structured notes as the meeting unfolds. It identifies decisions, action items, key discussion points, and unresolved questions live. These appear in real time in a shared panel that all participants can see. The meeting is being documented while it is happening.

### 2. The Confirmation Loop — "Is This What You Meant?"

After capturing a decision or key point, Tough Tongue AI **pauses and checks with participants:**

> *"I captured: 'We will ship the MVP to beta customers by June 15.' Does this match what everyone understood?"*

Participants can confirm, correct, or clarify. The result: everyone leaves the meeting with the same understanding.

### 3. Built-In AI Whiteboard

Words fail during complex discussions. Tough Tongue AI includes a built-in AI whiteboard that activates during meetings. If someone describes a process, the AI creates a visual flowchart in real time. Participants can annotate, modify, and save whiteboard outputs as part of the meeting record. No other AI meeting assistant has this capability.

### 4. AI Image Generation for Instant Clarification

Sometimes a participant is stuck trying to explain a concept. Tough Tongue AI generates explanatory images on the spot. Whether it's a UI mockup, an architecture diagram, or a data funnel, the AI creates visuals instantly. This transforms meetings into visual collaborations.

### 5. Slide Recall from Previous Sessions

When someone references a past meeting, Tough Tongue AI can instantly retrieve the relevant visual or slide. Tough Tongue AI indexes every visual artifact from every meeting, eliminating the need to search through old recordings.
