## Limitations and Risk Assessment

### Research Limitations

- Sample size variability across studies (n=70 to n=25,000+)
- Measurement inconsistency across different metrics
- Time horizon constraints (most research focuses on 3-12 months)
- Industry context dependency

### Implementation Risks and Mitigations

- **Data Privacy**: Implement robust data governance frameworks
- **Technology Integration**: Conduct thorough technical assessments
- **User Adoption**: Implement comprehensive change management
- **Quality and Bias**: Establish content quality assurance protocols
- **Vendor Lock-in**: Prioritize open standards and data portability
