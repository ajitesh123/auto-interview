## #2: Gong (Best for Revenue Intelligence at Scale)

**Website:** [gong.io](https://www.gong.io/)

[Gong](https://www.gong.io/) is the largest and most recognized conversation intelligence platform, used by over 4,000 companies worldwide. Their strength lies in massive data processing power, deal intelligence and integration depth with enterprise CRM and communication stacks.

### Strengths

- **Largest conversation dataset.** Gong processes billions of customer interactions, giving their AI models a training advantage for pattern recognition and benchmarking.
- **Revenue intelligence.** Goes beyond call analysis to provide deal risk scoring, pipeline intelligence and forecasting powered by conversation data.
- **Deep CRM integration.** Native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics and virtually every enterprise CRM.
- **Market leadership.** As the category leader, Gong benefits from the broadest ecosystem of integrations, partners and implementation resources.
- **Multi-channel capture.** Analyzes calls, video meetings, emails and web conferences in one platform.

### Limitations

- **No practice or roleplay capability.** Gong tells you what went wrong but does not provide a built-in way for reps to practice and fix the identified weaknesses.
- **Premium enterprise pricing.** Typically \$100 to \$200+ per user per month with annual contracts and implementation costs. Not accessible for SMEs or growing teams.
- **Scorecard customization requires support.** Custom evaluation criteria often need Professional Services or dedicated admin resources to configure and maintain.
- **Analysis focus, not action focus.** Excellent at identifying problems but relies on external tools and manual coaching to solve them.

### Best For

Large enterprise sales organizations with 200+ reps that need comprehensive revenue intelligence, deal analytics and conversation insights at scale, and have the budget for premium tooling plus separate coaching and training platforms.

## #3: Chorus by ZoomInfo (Best for CRM-Integrated Call Analysis)

**Website:** [chorus.ai](https://www.chorus.ai/)

[Chorus](https://www.chorus.ai/), acquired by ZoomInfo in 2021, combines conversation intelligence with ZoomInfo's massive B2B contact and company database. This integration gives sales teams call analysis enriched with buyer intent data and company insights.

### Strengths

- **ZoomInfo data enrichment.** Calls are automatically enriched with firmographic, technographic and intent data from ZoomInfo's database, providing context that standalone auditing platforms lack.
- **Competitive conversation intelligence.** Strong tracking of competitor mentions, market trends and buyer sentiment across conversations.
- **Meeting intelligence.** Captures and analyzes video meetings alongside calls, providing a complete view of customer interactions.
- **Coaching playlists.** Managers can curate collections of call moments for team coaching sessions and onboarding.
- **CRM auto-population.** Automatically writes call insights, action items and deal updates to CRM records.

### Limitations

- **Tied to ZoomInfo ecosystem.** Maximum value requires a ZoomInfo subscription, adding significant cost for teams not already using the platform.
- **No practice or roleplay tools.** Like Gong, Chorus identifies coaching needs but does not provide a practice environment for reps to improve.
- **Interface complexity.** The breadth of features can overwhelm smaller teams or those without a dedicated RevOps admin.
- **Post-acquisition uncertainty.** ZoomInfo's strategic direction for Chorus has evolved since acquisition, with some feature consolidation into the broader ZoomInfo platform.

### Best For

Sales teams already invested in the ZoomInfo ecosystem that want conversation intelligence enriched with buyer data, competitive tracking and CRM automation.

## Why the Audit-to-Practice Loop Changes Everything

Every call auditing platform on this list can tell you what went wrong on a sales call. Only one can help your rep fix it immediately.

Consider the typical flow with an audit-only platform:

1. **Monday:** Rep has a bad call. AI scores it poorly on objection handling.
2. **Wednesday:** Manager sees the score in their dashboard. Schedules coaching for Friday.
3. **Friday:** Manager coaches the rep. Suggests they practice the objection.
4. **Next week:** Rep tries to remember the coaching on their next live call.
5. **Two weeks later:** Maybe the behavior improves. Maybe it does not.

Now consider the Tough Tongue AI flow:

1. **Monday, 2:15 PM:** Rep finishes a call. AI scores it poorly on pricing objection handling.
2. **Monday, 2:17 PM:** Rep receives the score and a targeted AI roleplay scenario for pricing objections.
3. **Monday, 2:20 PM:** Rep practices the pricing objection three times against the AI buyer.
4. **Monday, 2:35 PM:** Rep's next live call includes the improved pricing response.

The time from diagnosis to improvement goes from **two weeks to twenty minutes.** This is why the audit-to-practice loop is the single most important feature in AI call auditing. It is the difference between generating reports and generating results.

## How to Choose the Right AI Call Auditing Platform

### Ask These Five Questions

1. **Does auditing connect to practice?** If the platform identifies weaknesses but relies on external tools for coaching and practice, you lose the feedback speed advantage.
2. **Can I customize scorecards myself?** Platforms requiring Professional Services for every scorecard change will slow your iteration speed dramatically.
3. **What is the real total cost?** Enterprise platforms may quote per-user pricing but add implementation, training, support and integration fees that double the cost.
4. **Does the platform match my selling motion?** Contact center tools do not serve B2B SaaS sales teams well. Revenue intelligence platforms may be overkill for SMEs.
5. **How fast can I deploy?** Platforms requiring months of implementation delay your time-to-value. Look for same-day or same-week deployment capability.
