## Before You Start: Choose Your First Call Type

The biggest mistake teams make is trying to automate everything at once. Start with **one call type** and nail it before expanding.

### The Best First Call Type for Each Goal

| Your Goal            | Start With This Call Type     | Why                                   |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| More meetings        | **Inbound lead follow-up**    | High-intent prospects, fastest ROI    |
| Faster qualification | **Demo request confirmation** | Clear intent, structured conversation |
| Pipeline recovery    | **No-show re-engagement**     | Existing pipeline, warm contacts      |
| Outbound at scale    | **Cold intro calls**          | Volume play, clear success metric     |
| Customer retention   | **Renewal check-ins**         | Known contacts, retention-focused     |

**Our recommendation:** Start with **inbound lead follow-up.** These prospects have already raised their hand. The AI's job is simple: respond fast, qualify interest and book the next step. This is the fastest path to measurable results.

## Step 1: Define Your Call Objective (3 Minutes)

Every AI call needs a single, clear objective. Not "engage the prospect." Not "build rapport." A specific, measurable outcome.

**Fill in this template:**

> **Call type:** [Inbound lead follow-up / Demo confirmation / Cold intro / etc.]
>
> **Primary objective:** [Book a meeting / Qualify budget / Confirm attendance / etc.]
>
> **Success metric:** [Meeting booked / Qualified lead forwarded / Confirmation received]
>
> **Escalation trigger:** [When to transfer to a human rep]
>
> **Data to collect:** [Name, company, budget range, timeline, use case]

**Example for inbound lead follow-up:**

> **Call type:** Inbound lead follow-up
>
> **Primary objective:** Book a demo within 48 hours
>
> **Success metric:** Demo scheduled and calendar invite sent
>
> **Escalation trigger:** Deal size above \$50K, competitor mention, or explicit human request
>
> **Data to collect:** Company size, current solution, timeline, decision-making process

This takes 3 minutes and prevents the most common setup mistake: building a conversation without a clear destination.

## Step 2: Write Your Call Script (10 Minutes)

Your AI calling script needs four components. Writing them takes 10 minutes if you already know your sales process.

### Component 1: The Opener (30 Seconds)

The opener determines whether the prospect stays or hangs up. AI callers must be transparent, concise and value-leading.

**Template:**

> "Hi [Name], this is the AI assistant from [Company]. You [visited our website / requested a demo / downloaded our guide] recently. I am calling to help you get the information you need. This will take about two minutes. Is now a good time?"

**Rules for AI openers:**

- Identify as an AI assistant immediately (transparency builds trust and is legally required in many jurisdictions)
- Reference the prospect's specific action to establish relevance
- State the time commitment (under 3 minutes for initial calls)
- Ask for permission to continue

### Component 2: Qualifying Questions (60 to 90 Seconds)

Keep it to 3 to 4 questions maximum. Every question should advance toward the call objective.

**Template for demo booking:**

1. "What prompted your interest in [product category]? Are you actively evaluating solutions?"
2. "How is your team currently handling [problem your product solves]?"
3. "What would success look like for you in the next 90 days?"
4. "Who else on your team would be involved in evaluating a solution like this?"

### Component 3: Objection Responses (Prepare 3 to 5)

Script responses for the most common objections your team encounters:

| Objection                              | AI Response                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Just send me an email"                | "Absolutely. I will send you all the details. Would it be helpful if I also booked a 15-minute slot so [sales rep name] can walk you through the most relevant features for your situation?"                        |
| "We are not looking \right now"        | "Understood. When would be a better time to revisit this? I can schedule a follow-up so you do not have to remember."                                                                                               |
| "How much does it cost?"               | "Pricing depends on your team size and use case. A quick 15-minute call with our team will give you an exact number. Would you prefer morning or afternoon?"                                                        |
| "I am happy with our current solution" | "That makes sense. Many of our customers were satisfied with their previous solution until they saw how [specific differentiator] could save them [specific benefit]. Would you be open to a 15-minute comparison?" |

### Component 4: Close and Next Step (30 Seconds)

Always end with a specific, concrete next step:

> "Great. I am going to book a 30-minute demo with [rep name] for [proposed time]. You will receive a calendar invite and a brief preparation guide by email in the next 5 minutes. Is there anything specific you would like the team to cover in the demo?"

## Step 3: Build in Scenario Studio (10 Minutes)

Log in to [Tough Tongue AI](https://app.toughtongueai.com/) and open Scenario Studio. The visual interface lets you build the entire conversation flow without code.

### Building Blocks

**1. Create a New Scenario**

Name it descriptively: "Inbound Lead Follow-up - Demo Booking" so your team can find it easily.

**2. Set the Opening Node**

Paste your opener script. Configure the AI voice tone (professional, friendly, consultative). Set the initial greeting and permission check.

**3. Add Qualifying Question Nodes**

Create branching nodes for each qualifying question. Set the AI to listen for key signals in the prospect's response and branch accordingly:

- **High intent:** Prospect confirms active evaluation. Route to demo booking.
- **Medium intent:** Prospect is researching. Route to resource sharing plus future follow-up.
- **Low intent:** Prospect was browsing casually. Route to email nurture.

**4. Add Objection Handling Nodes**

Create response nodes for each scripted objection. The AI matches objection patterns and responds with your prepared scripts.

**5. Set the Closing Node**

Configure the demo booking flow: propose time, confirm details, trigger calendar invite and CRM update.

**6. Configure Escalation Rules**

Set the triggers that transfer the conversation to a human rep:

- Prospect explicitly requests a human ("Let me talk to a real person")
- Deal size exceeds threshold (mention of team size &gt; 50, or enterprise keywords)
- Competitor name is mentioned (route to competitive specialist)
- Prospect shows frustration or escalating dissatisfaction

### Pro Tips for Scenario Studio

- **Keep scenarios under 8 nodes.** Complex branching confuses the conversation flow. Simple, direct paths convert better.
- **Test your script by reading it aloud.** If it sounds robotic or unnatural, the AI will deliver it that way. Write conversationally.
- **Add fallback responses.** For anything the AI cannot handle, have a graceful fallback: "That is a great question. Let me connect you with someone on our team who can give you the best answer."

## Step 5: Test and Launch (3 Minutes)

### Pre-Launch Checklist

Run through this checklist before going live:

- [ ] Call the AI yourself 3 \times and complete the full flow
- [ ] Test each objection response by voicing the objection naturally
- [ ] Verify CRM data appears correctly after test calls
- [ ] Confirm calendar invites are sent with correct details
- [ ] Test the escalation trigger by requesting a human transfer
- [ ] Verify notifications reach the \right team members
- [ ] Check that transcript and call summary generate accurately

### Soft Launch Strategy

Do not go from 0 to 100% overnight.

**Week 1:** Route 20% of inbound leads to AI calling. Monitor every call.
**Week 2:** If quality is good, increase to 50%. Review call data daily.
**Week 3:** If metrics confirm success, scale to 100%. Switch to weekly reviews.

### Your First 48 Hours

After launching, focus on these three signals:

1. **Connection rate:** Are prospects picking up and staying on the call past the opener? If not, refine the opener.
2. **Qualification completion rate:** Are prospects answering all qualifying questions? If drop-off is high at a specific question, simplify or reorder.
3. **Meeting booking rate:** Is the AI successfully closing for meetings? If not, examine the closing script and calendar integration.

## After Setup: Optimizing for Maximum Impact

### Week 1 Optimization: Script Refinement

Review AI call transcripts daily for the first week. Look for:

- Moments where prospects seem confused (simplify the AI's language)
- Questions prospects ask that the AI cannot handle (add new response nodes)
- Common objections you did not script (add new objection handling)
- Points where prospects disengage (shorten or restructure the flow)

### Month 1 Optimization: Scenario Expansion

Once your first call type is performing well, expand to additional scenarios:

- **Add a no-show re-engagement scenario** for prospects who miss scheduled demos
- **Add a post-demo follow-up scenario** to re-engage prospects who attended but did not convert
- **Add a cold outbound scenario** for new prospect lists

### Ongoing Optimization: Data-Driven Iteration

Use [Tough Tongue AI](https://app.toughtongueai.com/) analytics to continuously improve:

- A/B test different openers and measure connection rate impact
- Track which qualifying questions correlate with higher close rates
- Identify the optimal call time for your specific audience
- Monitor seasonal trends in objection types and update scripts accordingly
