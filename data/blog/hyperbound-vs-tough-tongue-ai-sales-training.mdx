## The Core Difference: Specialized Simulator vs Practice Plus Live Agents

### Hyperbound: The Cold-Call Drill Platform

Hyperbound builds AI versions of B2B buyers. It lets sales managers pick target buyer titles (such as VP of Finance or Director of IT) and lets reps pitch them over simulated voice calls.

**Where Hyperbound fits well:**

- Simulating specific enterprise buyer objections on outbound discovery calls.
- Standardized SDR certification programs in larger sales organizations.

**What to check:**

- **Response pacing:** Record a few roleplays and check whether pauses feel like a real cold call.
- **Buying process:** Confirm pricing, contract terms and trial access with their sales team.
- **Scope:** It is a training tool; if you also want AI agents to make live calls, you will need a separate product.

---

### Tough Tongue AI: Sales Roleplay Practice and AI Voice Agents

[Tough Tongue AI](https://app.toughtongueai.com/) runs AI voice agents for calls and meetings and AI sales roleplay practice. It streams speech in and out so the AI buyer can be interrupted mid-sentence, and can push back on a rep in the same way.

**How teams use it:**

1. **Train your reps:** Reps practice with AI buyer personas that raise objections, and each session is scored against the rubric you set (for example MEDDPICC, BANT, or Challenger).
2. **Review what works:** Session reports show where reps handled objections well and where they struggled.
3. **Run live conversations:** The same kind of conversation can be run by an AI voice agent on phone calls or in meetings.

## Head-to-Head: 5 Evaluation Areas

### 1. Voice Response Speed and Conversational Flow

In sales simulations, long pauses break the illusion of a real call. If the AI takes two seconds to respond, SDRs can develop a leisurely cadence that gets them hung up on during real cold calls.

Neither vendor publishes independently verified end-to-end latency. To compare them:

- Run the same scenario on both platforms and record the sessions.
- Time the gap between the end of your sentence and the start of the AI buyer's reply across many turns.
- Interrupt the AI buyer mid-sentence and check whether it stops and responds.

---

### 2. Scenario Setup

How easily can a VP of Sales create a simulation for an unexpected competitor announcement or a brand-new product launch?

- **Hyperbound:** Offers configurable buyer personas; ask how much of the setup your team can do without vendor help.
- **Tough Tongue AI:** Sales leaders write their own scenarios in the web app, describing things like:
  - **Buyer profile:** Title, company size, temperament (Analytical, Aggressive, Skeptical, Friendly).
  - **Hidden agendas:** E.g., _"Secretly evaluates your competitor and will only concede if you mention SOC 2 Type II compliance."_
  - **Pushback rules:** If the rep stumbles, the AI pushes back harder; if the rep makes the right case, the AI opens up.

---

### 3. Feedback Depth

A sales roleplay tool must tell you _why_ a conversation failed and _how_ to fix it. Look for feedback on both **what** the rep said (methodology, objection handling) and **how** they said it (pacing, talk-to-listen ratio, filler words).

- **Hyperbound:** Ask for a sample report and check whether it covers delivery as well as objection handling.
- **Tough Tongue AI:** Each session produces a scored report based on the rubric you define for the scenario, with strengths, weaknesses and a transcript.

---

### 4. Training vs Execution

The traditional sales stack separates training tools from calling tools. A team practices in one application and dials out of another.

- **Hyperbound:** Built for practice. It does not run live outbound calls.
- **Tough Tongue AI:** Runs both practice roleplays and AI voice agents for phone calls and meetings, so you can practice a renewal objection with reps and also have a voice agent handle similar conversations.
