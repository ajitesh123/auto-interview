## What is an AI LMS?

A learning management system (LMS) is the software a company or school uses to deliver courses, track completions and run assessments. [Training Industry](https://trainingindustry.com/glossary/learning-management-system) defines it as a computer-based system training organisations use to log and oversee their training activity.

An **AI LMS** adds AI on top of those jobs. In 2026 the vendors we checked use AI in six ways:

1. **Authoring:** turn a PDF, slide deck or prompt into a course and quiz.
2. **Tutor or assistant:** a chat box that answers learners' questions from the course content.
3. **Roleplay:** the learner talks or types to an AI customer, buyer or colleague and gets scored.
4. **Skills:** AI tags content and people with skills and recommends what to learn next.
5. **Translation:** one course, many languages, without a translation agency.
6. **Agents:** AI that does admin work inside the LMS, such as building learning paths or reports.

Most "AI LMS" claims are about the first two. Roleplay, skills and agents are where platforms really differ, so that is what to test in a demo.

## Why practice is the feature to test

AI that writes courses faster does not, on its own, change what people can do at work. In a 2026 vendor survey for Docebo of 2,000 people in six countries, ["85% of employees say the training they receive does not help them use AI in their role"](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260407928752/en/Docebo-Releases-The-AI-Readiness-Gap-The-2026-Enterprise-Learning-Wake-Up-Call-Report), while 79% of learning leaders already use AI for content, assessments and recommendations. Only 9% said AI had fully redefined their workflows.

The gap is practice. A sales rep, support agent or new manager learns a hard conversation by having it, getting feedback and having it again. That is why roleplay is the AI feature to test hardest in a demo:

- [ ] Can a learner **speak**, not just type, to the AI character?
- [ ] Does the AI push back with a realistic objection?
- [ ] Is every session scored against a rubric you can edit?
- [ ] Are sessions capped, metered or sold as an add-on?
- [ ] Can scores flow back into the LMS or your CRM?

If your LMS passes on everything else but fails here, you don't have to switch. ## FAQ

**What is the best AI LMS in 2026?** For small teams, 360Learning covers all six AI types we checked at \$8 per user a month. For large enterprises, Docebo covers all six too, with roleplay, agents and skills sold as add-ons. TalentLMS is the cheapest start with a free plan.

**What does an AI LMS cost?** Published prices we found run from free (TalentLMS, 5 users) to \$8 per user a month (360Learning Team). For 40 learners that is \$1,428 to \$8,286 a year at list price. Half the platforms quote privately.

**Which LMS has AI roleplay?** 360Learning (AI Roleplay), TalentLMS (Learning Playground), EducateMe (AI Roleplay Coach), Litmos (AI video assessments), Docebo (Roleplay add-on) and Cornerstone (Immerse AI Companion, VR).

**Is AI included in the LMS price?** It depends. Disco and Blackboard say AI is included. TalentLMS meters AI with credits, EducateMe caps voice sessions, and Docebo sells some AI as add-ons. Ask for the AI line items in writing.

**Is Moodle an AI LMS?** Moodle has had an AI subsystem since version 4.5, but you connect your own AI provider and pay it directly.
