Operating automated AI voice calling in India legally requires strict compliance with the **Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations (TCCCPR 2018)** enforced by the **Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI)**. Commercial promotional outbound campaigns must originate exclusively from registered **140-series** number blocks allocated through carrier Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) blockchain portals, adhere strictly to the **09:00 AM to 09:00 PM IST** calling window, and undergo programmatic pre-call scrubbing against the national Do Not Disturb (DND) registry.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        TRAI DLT REGULATORY COMPLIANCE ROADMAP                          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Step 1: Principal Entity (PE) Registration on Carrier DLT Portal (PAN/GST/KYC)        │
│ Step 2: Telemarketer Tagging with Registered Cloud Carrier (Exotel/Tata/Airtel)       │
│ Step 3: Allocation of Dedicated 140 Series (Promotional) or 160 Series (Transactional) │
│ Step 4: Outbound Voice Script Template Hashing & Blockchain Registration               │
│ Step 5: Pre-Call Scrubbing Against National Customer Preference Register (NCPR / DND) │
│ Step 6: Enforcement of Permitted Calling Hours: 09:00 AM to 09:00 PM IST Strictly    │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 1. The Legal Prohibition on Standard 10-Digit Mobile SIMs

Many early-stage startups and outbound call centers mistakenly attempt to deploy automated voice AI bots using standard 10-digit consumer SIM cards (`+91 98xxx`, `+91 99xxx`) or unregistered virtual DID pools.

Under TRAI directives and Department of Telecommunications (DoT) orders:

- **Immediate Number Disconnection:** Telecom Service Providers (Airtel, Jio, Vi, BSNL) deploy automated AI traffic analyzers that flag outbound calling patterns (high volume of calls with short durations and repetitive answer rates).
- **Corporate Blacklisting:** Using unregistered numbers for commercial telemarketing results in the permanent blacklisting of the company’s PAN and GST across all Indian telecom carriers for up to two years.
- **Financial Penalties:** Fines up to **₹5,00,000 per violation batch** are levied under TRAI enforcement guidelines.

## 2. The 140 Series vs. 160 Series Mandate

TRAI strictly categorizes commercial telephone calls into distinct numerical series based on intent:

| Number Series         | Permitted Call Category          | Typical Use Cases                                                             | DND Scrubbing Required?              |
| :-------------------- | :------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------- |
| **140 Series**        | **Promotional & Telemarketing**  | Cold outreach, product sales, credit card offers, real estate pitches         | **YES (Strict Mandatory Scrubbing)** |
| **1600 Series**       | **Government Communications**    | Central and State Government public service announcements, civic alerts       | Exempt                               |
| **1601 Series**       | **Regulated Financial Services** | RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA entities (OTP alerts, fraud notices, policy renewals) | Exempt from promotional DND blocks   |
| **Standard 10-Digit** | **Personal Voice Only**          | Individual peer-to-peer consumer telephone calls                              | **PROHIBITED for Automated AI**      |

### The 160 Series for Banking & Fintech

In mid-2024, TRAI introduced the **160 series** specifically to prevent consumers from ignoring critical transactional calls (fraud warnings, OTP confirmations, loan repayment reminders) disguised as telemarketing spam. If your AI voice bot performs account servicing or collections on behalf of an RBI-regulated bank or NBFC, you must secure a **1601-series** allocation.

## 3. Step-by-Step DLT Registration Guide for AI Voice Bots

Before connecting your AI calling engine to an Indian SIP trunk (Exotel, Tata Tele Business Services, Airtel IQ, or Plivo India), you must complete DLT registration:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        DLT REGISTRATION WORKFLOW FOR AI CALLING                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Entity KYC: Submit Certificate of Incorporation, GSTIN, PAN, and Board Resolution  │
│ 2. Telemarketer (RTM) Association: Link your PE ID to your carrier's Telemarketer ID   │
│ 3. Header Allocation: Apply for 140-series promotional headers (e.g., 140-123456)     │
│ 4. Voice Script Template Hashing: Submit system prompt text and openers to DLT ledger │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Entity KYC:** Register as a Principal Entity (PE) on any carrier DLT portal (Jio DLT, Vilpower, or Airtel DLT). Approval takes 2 to 4 business days.
2. **Telemarketer Binding:** Associate your Principal Entity ID with your telecom provider’s Registered Telemarketer (RTM) ID.
3. **Voice Template Hashing:** Under TRAI guidelines, all commercial message text must be hashed and registered on the DLT blockchain. For conversational AI voice agents with dynamic LLM generation, register your deterministic **Opening Hook** and **Mandatory Disclosures** as pre-approved DLT templates.

## 4. Operational Rules: Calling Windows & DND Scrubbing

Operating a compliant AI voice caller in India requires strict adherence to two operational constraints:

### 1. The 09:00 AM to 09:00 PM IST Calling Window

Commercial telemarketing calls are legally prohibited before **09:00 AM** and after **09:00 PM**, regardless of customer time zones. Outbound dialing engines must implement hard scheduling gates that terminate all outbound queues at precisely 20:59:59 IST.

### 2. Pre-Call DND Registry Scrubbing (NCPR)

Under TRAI rules, all phone number lists for promotional campaigns must be scrubbed against the **National Customer Preference Register (NCPR)**.

- Dialing a consumer registered on DND without documented, verifiable consent obtained within the preceding 180 days is an actionable offense.
- Leading telecom cloud carriers provide real-time API endpoints to scrub lead batches against the national database prior to dispatching SIP `INVITE` packets.

### 3. Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 Alignment

Under India's **DPDP Act 2023**, processing voice audio recordings and generating personal profile data requires explicit, notice-backed consent. Voice agents must:

- Disclose that the call is recorded at the start of the interaction.
- Provide an immediate opt-out mechanism (_"If you would like us to remove your number from our calling list, press 9 or say unsubscribe"_).
- Store audio recordings on servers located within the territory of India to comply with national data localization standards.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if an AI voice bot calls someone registered on DND in India?

If a complaint is lodged via the TRAI DND 3.0 mobile application, the telecom provider issues an automated show-cause notice. Continued violations lead to graded financial penalties (starting at ₹1,000 per call up to ₹5,00,000 per violation batch) and revocation of corporate DLT registration.

### Can an AI voice bot use a 160-series number for sales calls?

No. The **160-series** is reserved strictly for service and transactional calls from entities regulated by statutory bodies like the RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, and PFRDA. Using a 160-series number for promotional sales pitches violates carrier allocation rules and results in immediate number reclamation.

### Is DLT registration mandatory for inbound AI customer service bots?

Inbound virtual receptionist bots (where the customer dials your published support number) do not require 140-series telemarketing headers, but the hosting business must maintain verified enterprise telecom KYC with its carrier.

### How do I register dynamic LLM conversational scripts on DLT?

Register your deterministic **Opening Pitch**, **Company Identification**, and **Consent Verification** sentences as pre-approved voice templates on the DLT portal. The subsequent conversational reasoning must operate within the boundary of the registered commercial category.
