## Industry Deep Dive 2: FinTech & SME Lending (Working Capital, Loans & Merchant POS)

India's FinTech ecosystem has expanded rapidly, but whether distributing unsecured business loans, invoice discounting, or merchant Soundbox/POS terminals, **sales is fundamentally a trust game**.

Business owners receive 5 to 10 automated spam calls every day. When an inside sales rep calls a Kirana shop owner, SME manufacturer, or D2C brand founder, the default reaction is suspicion.

### The Core FinTech Objections

- _"Every PSU bank calls me offering 11% interest. Why is your platform charging 16% reducing?"_
- _"What hidden origination, documentation, and prepayment penalties are you hiding?"_
- _"I already have an active Cash Credit (CC) limit with HDFC/SBI. I don't need app loans."_
- _"Why do you need my GST portal login and 12 months of net banking PDF statements?"_

Reps must balance **persuasive consultative selling** with **strict RBI digital lending compliance**. A single inaccurate statement regarding APR or foreclosure fees can trigger regulatory sanctions.

### Battle-Tested Objection Script: The "SBI is 11%, You Are 16%" Pivot

```
PROSPECT: "Why are you charging 16% APR when State Bank of India is offering me 11%?"

AI-TRAINED FINTECH SCRIPT (The Working Capital Velocity Pivot):
1. Reframe Price as Cost of Delay:
   "Suresh Ji, SBI's 11% rate is phenomenal—if you can wait 45 days, pledge your factory land as collateral, and submit audited balance sheets in triplicate."

2. Diagnose Opportunity Cost:
   "You mentioned your peak inventory buying cycle for the festival season starts in 48 hours. If you wait 45 days for PSU bank sanction, what happens to the 12% bulk cash discount your raw material supplier is offering you right now?"

3. The Net Margin Payoff:
   "Our 16% reducing interest over a 90-day seasonal bridge loan costs you approximately ₹18,000 in total interest on a ₹5 Lakh disbursal. But securing your supplier's cash discount saves you ₹60,000 today. You are netting ₹42,000 in pure profit, with funds credited to your current account before 5:00 PM tomorrow without collateral. Should we initiate the 2-minute GST verification?"
```
