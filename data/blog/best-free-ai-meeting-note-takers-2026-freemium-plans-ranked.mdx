**The best free AI note taker is the one whose free limits fit your meetings, and on the vendors' own pages (17 Nov 2026) four free plans offer unlimited meetings with no monthly minute cap: Fathom ("Unlimited recordings + transcriptions"), Circleback ("unlimited meetings", 30-day history), Granola (bot-free, 30-day history) and Grain (unlimited meetings up to 45 minutes each). Otter's free plan gives 300 minutes a month but stops each conversation at 30 minutes; Fireflies transcribes without a cap but keeps 400 minutes of storage. Google Meet's and Microsoft Teams' built-in note takers are not free: both need a paid licence. Whatever you use, tell people the meeting is being recorded.**

<KeyFacts>

- **"Unlimited" free plans hide their limit elsewhere:** Grain caps each meeting at 45 minutes, Circleback and Granola show 30 days of history, tl;dv gives full AI notes for your first 10 meetings ever, then only the first 10 minutes of each.
- **Capped free plans cover 2 to 5 hours of meetings a month at most** (Notta 120 minutes, MeetGeek 180, Otter, Read AI, Jamie and Sembly up to 300).
- **No bot needed:** Granola, Jamie, Tactiq, MeetGeek, and the desktop apps of Circleback and Grain record on the free plan without a bot joining the call; Fathom's bot-free mode is a Mac beta.
- **Meet and Teams note takers are paid:** Google's "Take notes for me" needs Workspace Business Standard or higher (or a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan); Teams intelligent recap needs Teams Premium or Copilot. Zoom's free Basic plan summarises 3 hosted meetings a month.
- **Consent is your job:** US federal law allows one-party consent, but California and several other states require everyone's; two US class actions over note takers (Otter, Granola) are in court.

</KeyFacts>

## Bot or no bot

**A bot joins the meeting as a participant; a bot-free note taker records the audio on your own computer.** Bot-free tools avoid an extra participant in the call, but they only work for the person running them, on a desktop app or browser extension.

- **Bot-free on the free plan:** Granola (desktop and mobile apps), Jamie (desktop app, also in person), Tactiq (Chrome extension for Meet, Zoom and Teams), Circleback and Grain (desktop apps, listed on their free plans), MeetGeek ("on every plan, including Free").
- **Bot-free, plan not stated:** tl;dv and Notta also offer a desktop app that records without a bot; the pages we read don't say whether their free plans include it. Fathom's bot-free capture is a Mac beta.
- **Bot-free is not a consent shortcut.** The 2026 lawsuit against Granola concerns exactly that kind of tool: a participant who never used it says device-audio capture recorded her without consent.

## Is it legal to use an AI note taker?

**Yes, with consent: the rule depends on where each person on the call is.** A note taker records the conversation, so recording law applies even when you only keep the summary.

<ConsentTable />

In practice: say "I'm using an AI note taker, is that OK?" at the start, and make sure the meeting invite says so. In the UK and EU, recording staff calls also needs a lawful basis, and you must tell people why you record.

## What to do if you are…

- **A founder or freelancer with a few client calls a week:** Fathom or Circleback cover any number of calls on the free plan; Granola if you would rather no bot joins. Check that 30 days of history (Circleback, Granola) is enough, or export the notes you need.
- **A sales rep who must log calls in a CRM:** Free plans rarely sync to a CRM: Otter, Read AI, Granola, Grain and Circleback keep CRM sync for paid plans. Fireflies' help page says "All integrations are available on the Free plan"; check its storage cap first.
- **On Google Meet or Teams at work:** ask IT whether your licence already includes Gemini notes or intelligent recap, and whether outside note-taking bots are allowed, before installing anything.
- **Running back-to-back 1-hour meetings:** Fathom, Circleback and Granola are the only free plans here with no stated per-meeting or monthly cap. Otter, Jamie, Grain and Read AI cap each meeting below or at an hour.
- **In a regulated industry (health, finance, legal):** a free consumer plan is the wrong place for client calls. Use your company's approved tool, or none.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free AI note taker?

It depends on your meetings. With unlimited meetings and no monthly minute cap on their own pages (17 Nov 2026): Fathom (unlimited recordings and storage), Circleback (unlimited meetings, 30-day history), Granola (bot-free, 30-day history) and Grain (unlimited meetings up to 45 minutes each). Use the fit check above with your meeting count and length.

### Is there a free AI note taker with no limits?

None is free of all limits. Fathom comes closest on its own pages ("Unlimited recordings + transcriptions", unlimited storage), though AI action items and some other features are marked "Limited use" in its plan table. Circleback and Granola limit history to 30 days; Grain limits meetings to 45 minutes.

### Is Otter.ai free?

Yes, Otter Basic is free: 300 minutes a month per user, up to 30 minutes per conversation, and the 25 most recent conversations kept. Pro is \$8.33 a user a month billed annually or \$16.99 monthly (17 Nov 2026).

### Is Fireflies free?

Yes. Fireflies' free plan has "Unlimited transcription", "Limited AI summaries", a 2-hour recording limit and 400 minutes of storage. Its pricing page says storage is per team; its help page says per seat. Pro is \$10 a seat a month billed annually.

### Is there a free AI note taker without a bot?

Yes. Granola, Jamie and Tactiq record from your device with no bot; Circleback, Grain and MeetGeek offer a bot-free option on their free plans. tl;dv and Notta have bot-free desktop apps without saying whether the free plan includes them, and Fathom's is a Mac beta.

### Is Google Meet's "Take notes for me" free?

No. Google says it requires Workspace Business Standard, Business Plus, Enterprise Standard or Enterprise Plus, or a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan, and the meeting organiser must have it. Free Gmail accounts do not get it.

### Is there a free AI note taker for Zoom?

Zoom's own AI features (now called ZoomMate) on the free Basic plan summarise 3 hosted meetings a month and give 3 AI note-taking uses a month. For more, Fathom, Grain, Fireflies, Otter and tl;dv name Zoom on their pages, each with its own free limits; Tactiq's free Chrome extension also works in Zoom.

### Is it legal to use an AI note taker in a meeting?

Usually, if everyone knows and agrees. US federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2511) allows recording with one party's consent, but California (Penal Code § 632) and several other states require all parties' consent, and the UK ICO says you must tell people you are recording and why. Announce the note taker at the start of every call.

### Can AI note takers record in-person meetings?

Some can. Circleback lists "Online and in-person meetings" on its free plan, and Jamie lists in-person meetings. Most others describe online meetings only.
