## Architectural Comparison: Dedicated ASR vs Multimodal Latent Tokens

The divergence in benchmark results between Google and OpenAI stems from fundamentally different architectural philosophies.

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│           Google Gemini 3.5 Transcribe Pipeline             │
│  PCM Audio ──► Conformer-2 Encoder ──► Dedicated ASR Decoder │
│  - Optimized with Connectionist CTC + Cross-Entropy Loss    │
│  - Explicit Voice Activity Conditioning (No Hallucinations) │
│  - Cost: \$0.0090/min streaming                              │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             OpenAI GPT Live Transcribe Pipeline             │
│  PCM Audio ──► Neural Audio VQ-VAE ──► Autoregressive LLM   │
│  - Joint Next-Token Audio/Text Prediction                   │
│  - Susceptible to looping and hallucinations during silence │
│  - Cost: \$0.0600/min audio input                            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

OpenAI GPT Live Transcribe treats speech recognition as an internal modality transition inside the GPT-4o transformer. Raw audio waveforms are quantized into continuous latent audio tokens, which the general-purpose language model decodes into text.

While this allows OpenAI to understand emotional prosody, it introduces significant speech recognition vulnerabilities. In the absence of speech, the autoregressive decoder can enter degenerate repetition loops, generating phantom text during ambient background noise.

Gemini 3.5 Transcribe operates as a dedicated ASR system. It employs a Conformer-2 acoustic encoder connected to a speech-specific transformer decoder trained on multi-task speech recognition losses. It delivers deterministic transcription with zero silent hallucinations.

## Deconstructing the FLEURS Benchmark: 5.50% vs 8.97% WER

The FLEURS benchmark tests streaming accuracy across 25 global locales representing diverse linguistic structures and phonetic complexities.

![FLEURS Benchmark: Gemini 3.5 Transcribe Live at 5.50% WER vs OpenAI GPT Live Transcribe at 8.97%](/static/images/blog/gemini-3-5-transcribe-fleurs-benchmark.png)

### Why OpenAI Lagged Behind on Multilingual Speech

1. **Sliding-Window Attention Artifacts:** OpenAI Whisper and its real-time variants process audio using 30-second chunking mechanisms or sliding-window attention. When audio streams are truncated near word boundaries, the model experiences edge-frame alignment errors, increasing substitution error rates on streaming audio.
2. **Low-Resource Phonetic Inversion:** In Indic languages (`hi-IN`, `ta-IN`, `te-IN`) and Arabic dialects (`ar-EG`), OpenAI GPT Live Transcribe exhibits phonetic inversion on retroflex consonants. Gemini 3.5 Transcribe uses Google's Universal Speech Model pre-training to preserve consonant distinctions across 85+ languages.
3. **Punctuation and Normalization Errors:** OpenAI models frequently over-punctuate conversational speech, inserting commas and question marks mid-phrase. Gemini 3.5 Transcribe uses context-aware auto-formatting tuned for spoken grammar.

## The Network Latency Penalty: Regional Edge vs US-Only Hosting

For voice agents operating outside North America, network round-trip time (RTT) is the primary determinant of perceived responsiveness.

```text
Telephony Server (Mumbai) to OpenAI US Endpoint:
[Mumbai Server] ──────────────── (180ms - 280ms RTT) ──────────────► [OpenAI US-East]
Total Response Delay: 280ms Network + 150ms Inference = 430ms

Telephony Server (Mumbai) to Google Cloud asia-south1:
[Mumbai Server] ──── (25ms - 45ms RTT) ───► [Google Cloud Mumbai]
Total Response Delay: 45ms Network + 120ms Inference = 165ms
```

OpenAI hosts its Realtime API infrastructure exclusively in North American data centers. When an Indian or European telephony bridge streams audio to OpenAI, packets incur **180ms to 280ms** of cross-continental transit latency.

Google deploys Gemini 3.5 Transcribe across its global edge network, including `asia-south1` (Mumbai). Packets reach the ASR engine in **25ms to 45ms**, saving **150ms** in total voice pipeline turnaround time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does Gemini 3.5 Transcribe outperform OpenAI on the FLEURS benchmark?**
Gemini 3.5 Transcribe is a purpose-built ASR engine pre-trained on Google's Universal Speech Model corpus across 85+ languages, avoiding the alignment errors common in OpenAI's sliding-window decoders.

**How does Gemini 3.5 Transcribe prevent hallucinations during silent audio?**
Gemini uses explicit voice activity conditioning inside its Conformer-2 acoustic encoder, ensuring that the decoder suppresses token emission when audio energy drops below baseline thresholds.

**Can OpenAI GPT Live Transcribe be used as a standalone ASR API?**
OpenAI does not expose GPT Live Transcribe as an independent streaming STT endpoint; it must be consumed through the Realtime API at \$0.06/min audio input rates. Whisper API remains batch-only.

**Does Gemini 3.5 Transcribe support speaker diarization?**
Yes. Gemini 3.5 Transcribe natively clusters audio streams into up to **8 distinct speaker labels**, whereas OpenAI does not expose diarization in its API.
