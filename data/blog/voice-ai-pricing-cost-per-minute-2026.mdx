## Cascade vs Voice-to-Voice: The Core Difference

**Cascade** means three separate API bills. STT converts speech to text. LLM generates a text response. TTS converts that response back to audio. Three sequential calls, three vendors, total latency of **630 to 1200ms**.

**Voice-to-Voice** means one model handles audio end-to-end. One vendor, one bill, total latency of **80 to 220ms**.

Why it matters for pricing: cascade looks cheaper per minute but carries hidden costs in hang-up rate, engineering overhead from managing three failure modes, and billing surprises that this post documents in detail.

## Three Billing Gotchas Nobody Warns You About

### Deepgram Charges for Silence

If your Voice Activity Detection threshold is misconfigured, Deepgram receives and bills for seconds of dead audio before the user speaks. On **100,000 minutes** of calls, this adds **5% to 8%** to your STT bill.

At **$0.0043/min** on **100,000 minutes** (**$430** base), silence billing costs you an extra **$22 to $34 per month**. At **1,000,000 minutes**, that is **$220 to $340/month** billed for silence.

The fix: set `endpointing: 300` and `utterance_end_ms: 1000` in your Deepgram config. Test your VAD sensitivity before going to production, not after.

### ElevenLabs Bills by Character, Not by Minute

Your TTS bill is partly a prompt engineering problem. Consider:

"Your balance is Rs 25,000." = **26 characters**

"Your outstanding balance as of today stands at approximately Rupees twenty-five thousand." = **90 characters**

Same information. **3.5x** the TTS cost. At **$0.30 per 1,000 characters** for Eleven v3, that difference on **10,000 utterances per day** is **$2,190 per month**.

If you are using ElevenLabs in production, audit your response templates for character count. Every unnecessary word is a billing line item.

### OpenAI Realtime API Bills Both Sides of the Conversation

Audio input: **$0.06/min**. Audio output: **$0.24/min**.

For a **4-minute call** where the human speaks 2 minutes and the AI speaks 2 minutes:

- Input (entire call duration, including when AI is speaking): **$0.06 x 4 = $0.24**
- Output (when AI is speaking): **$0.24 x 2 = $0.48**
- Total: **$0.72 for one 4-minute call**

At **10,000 calls per month**: **$7,200 in model costs alone**, before SIP or infrastructure. With overhead, you are at **$10,000 to $12,000/month** for 10,000 calls.

This is not hidden in the fine print. It is on the OpenAI pricing page. Most teams do not do this multiplication before they start building.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the average cost per minute for AI calling in India?**

A budget cascade stack costs **$0.025/min (Rs 2.10/min)** for English. An Indian language cascade stack using Gnani and Smallest.ai costs **$0.148/min (Rs 12.40/min)**. TTGE bundles everything including Indian language support at **Rs 3.50/min ($0.042/min)**.

**Why is ElevenLabs so much more expensive than Cartesia for voice agents?**

ElevenLabs bills per character of text generated. Cartesia bills per minute of audio output. For conversational AI where the AI speaks in short sentences, per-minute billing is substantially cheaper. The difference is **$0.008/min** for Cartesia vs **$0.117 to $0.195/min** for ElevenLabs.

**How much does OpenAI Realtime API actually cost per call?**

A **4-minute call** costs approximately **$0.72** in model fees alone. OpenAI bills **$0.06/min** for all audio input across the entire call duration, plus **$0.24/min** for audio output. Add SIP and infrastructure and a 4-minute call runs **$0.80 to $0.90** total.

**What hidden costs do most AI calling pricing breakdowns miss?**

Three main ones: silence billing from misconfigured VAD (adds **5-8%** to STT bills), character-based TTS billing that rewards shorter prompts, and the engineering cost of managing three vendor APIs. Infrastructure overhead adds **30-50%** to raw API costs in production.

**Is cascade or voice-to-voice more cost-effective for Indian outbound calling?**

It depends on call volume and team size. Below **30,000 minutes/month**, DIY cascade is cheaper. Above that, the engineering cost of maintaining cascade infrastructure makes TTGE competitive. V2V also generates **2.87x more conversations per dial** due to lower hang-up rates, which changes the per-conversation economics entirely.

**What does Tough Tongue AI pricing include?**

Native voice-to-voice processing, Vobiz SIP trunking with **30-48%** pickup rate on Indian calls, post-call transcription, analytics, guardrails, compliance logging, LiveKit session management, Indian language support, infrastructure management, and IST-timezone support. All at **Rs 3.50/min**.

**At what call volume does AI calling pay back vs human SDRs?**

Immediately, in most cases. At the same dial volume, TTGE generates **2.87x more conversations** at **57% lower cost** than a 5-person SDR team. The break-even in pipeline terms happens in the first month.

**Does Deepgram charge for silence and background noise?**

Yes. Deepgram charges for all audio received before the endpointing threshold fires. Misconfigured VAD settings can add **5% to 8%** to your monthly bill. Set `endpointing: 300` and test in staging before production.
