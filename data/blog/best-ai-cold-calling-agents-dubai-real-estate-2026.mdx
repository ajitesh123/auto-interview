Top brokerages employ hundreds of agents to grind the phones, dialing massive lists of international and local investors. But manual cold calling is slow, prone to extreme human burnout, and wildly inconsistent in quality.

In 2026, the competitive advantage has shifted to automation. The **best AI cold calling agents for Dubai real estate** are transforming how brokerages fill their pipelines, doing much of the first-call work that junior dialers do today.

> **Before you dial in the UAE (added 20 Nov 2026).** Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024 applies to every marketing call, AI or human: call only "during the period from 9:00 am to 6:00 pm", never call numbers on the Do Not Call Register (DNCR), "Record Marketing Phone Calls, with the necessity of informing the Consumer of this recording when the call begins", use local numbers from licensed UAE telecom companies, and retry unanswered calls at most once a day and twice a week ([UAE Legislation](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2519/download)). A campaign that dials a whole database at once breaks these rules unless the list is washed and the calls are timed. For rules in the US, India, UK and Australia and a free call check, see [AI calling for real estate: what's legal, costs and scripts](/blog/ai-calling-for-real-estate-convert-more-leads-2026).
