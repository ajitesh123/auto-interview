## What a real ATS does with your resume

**An ATS reads your resume into fields, applies any knock-out questions the employer set, optionally ranks you with an AI score, and puts you in front of a person.** That is the order the vendors' own help docs describe. Nowhere in them did we find a rule that rejects a resume for a missing keyword.

<AtsApplicationFlow />

<AtsRulesTable />

Two things follow for you:

1. **Answer knock-out questions truthfully and carefully.** "Are you authorised to work in…", "Do you have 3+ years of…" are the questions that can reject you in seconds, in Greenhouse, SmartRecruiters and Oracle alike. A perfect resume does not survive a "No" there.
2. **Matching the job's words still matters, for a different reason.** Workday's HiredScore grades A–D on "how well their resumes match the skills and qualifications specified in the job description", and Greenhouse's match score is set by the recruiter's criteria. A recruiter sorting hundreds of applicants also searches and skims for those words. That is what a checker helps with.

Some systems do reject at scale when the employer sets them up that way: HiredScore can move candidates "individually or in bulk", and Lever's auto-archive can send a rejection email. These are rules the employer writes, usually on application answers.

The best survey of recruiters we found points the same way. In [Enhancv's interviews with 25 US recruiters](https://enhancv.com/blog/does-ats-reject-resumes/) (Sep–Oct 2025), 23 said their ATS does not auto-reject resumes for formatting, content or design; 2 said theirs does, using match or experience thresholds the employer set (Bullhorn and BambooHR). 84% said they rely on knock-out questions. Enhancv sells a checker, so read it as a vendor's survey.

## Why the scores disagree

**A checker's score measures that checker's rules, so the same resume gets a different number in every tool.** In the only side-by-side test we could find, a developer ran one 612-word resume against one job post through eight tools in one sitting:

<AtsScoreSpreadFigure />

The vendors say the same thing about their own numbers:

- Jobscan: "your Match Rate is a Jobscan visualization tool, not a score assigned by the employer's ATS" ([resume scanner page](https://www.jobscan.co/resume-scanner)).
- Enhancv: "there's no such thing as an ATS score – no tool online that provides a score gives an actual score" ([resume checker page](https://enhancv.com/resources/resume-checker/)).
- Naukri360 shows bands (80–100, 60–79, below 60) and says there is no universal passing mark ([checker page](https://www.naukri.com/naukri360/ats-resume-checker)).

So use a score to find gaps, not as a target. When two tools agree that a must-have skill from the post is missing, add it (if you have it). When a number moves from 78 to 82 because you repeated a keyword, stop and apply.

## A 10-minute check that works with any tool

You can do most of what a checker does yourself. Do it once per job you really want:

1. **Plain-text test (1 minute).** Open your PDF, select all, paste into a plain-text editor. If the order is scrambled, words run together or your name and contact details are missing, the ATS will have the same trouble. Lever's help page gives the shortcut: "If you cannot highlight text, the document is likely not parseable."
2. **Title (1 minute).** Put the job's own title near the top if it is true for you ("Data Analyst", not "Insights Ninja").
3. **Must-haves (5 minutes).** List the skills the post says are required. Each one you have should appear in a bullet with a result, not only in a skills list. Our [Resume Keyword Checker](/resume-keyword-checker) does this step with the post's own lines.
4. **Layout (2 minutes).** One column, standard headings (Experience, Education, Skills), no tables or text boxes, contact details in the body, not the header. Greenhouse names each of these as a cause of failed parsing.
5. **File (1 minute).** Use the format the posting asks for. Greenhouse accepts .doc, .docx, .pdf, .rtf and .txt; Lever accepts Word, PDF and more but not images. A PDF exported from Word or Google Docs is text; one exported from a design tool as an image is not.

Then apply early. In [Enhancv's interviews with 25 recruiters](https://enhancv.com/blog/does-ats-reject-resumes/), 52% said they review applications in the order they arrive; a week spent pushing a score from 80 to 90 can cost you that place.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free ATS resume checker?

For repeat free checks against a job post: Jobscan (5 scans a month with a free account), SkillSyncer (2 job match scans a week), Teal (basic keyword matching and the top 5 keywords), Naukri360 (free score, India), Enhancv's free checker and our [Resume Keyword Checker](/resume-keyword-checker) (free, no account, nothing uploaded). Kickresume gives free users an overall score; its full report needs Premium.

### What is a good ATS score?

There is no ATS score that employers see. Each checker sets its own scale: Jobscan recommends 80% for its Match Rate and says 75% can work, Teal calls 80% or higher a good match score, and Huntr says to aim for around 80. Use the score to find missing must-have skills, fix those, and apply.

### Is Jobscan worth it?

Its free account gives 5 scans a month, which covers tailoring for your top few jobs. Pay only if you tailor for many jobs a month. Jobscan shows its paid price only after sign-up, so compare it with the published prices above before you pay.

### Do ATS systems automatically reject resumes?

The automatic rejection described in the Greenhouse, Oracle, SmartRecruiters and Lever docs comes from rules the employer sets, mostly knock-out answers to application questions. None of the docs we read describes rejecting a resume for missing keywords. AI scores in Greenhouse, Workday (HiredScore) and Ashby rank or grade you for a recruiter. In Enhancv's interviews, 2 of 25 recruiters said their system auto-rejects on match thresholds the employer set.

### Is it true that 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS?

There is no evidence for it. The figure comes from Preptel, a resume-optimisation company, which told [Computerworld in March 2012](https://www.computerworld.com/article/1458323/5-insider-secrets-for-beating-applicant-tracking-systems.html) that ATS "kill 75% of candidates' chances of landing an interview". No study or method was published with it.

### PDF or Word: which is better for an ATS?

Both work if the file is text. Greenhouse accepts .doc, .docx, .pdf, .rtf and .txt ([supported file types](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/360052218132-Supported-file-types-for-resumes-and-cover-letters)); Lever accepts Word, PDF and others but not images. If the posting names a format, use it.

### Why does the same resume get different scores in different checkers?

Because each tool counts different things: keyword overlap, bullet quality, length, sections. In a May 2026 test, one resume scored from 54 to 91 across six scans in five tools. Pick one tool, fix the gaps it shows, and do not chase another tool's number.

### How much does an ATS resume checker cost?

On published prices (16 Nov 2026), covering a 3-month search costs \$34.86 (SkillSyncer), \$49.95 (Resume.io), \$54 (Kickresume), about \$78 (Teal+), \$87 (Rezi), \$90 (Huntr) or \$99 (Resume Worded). Enhancv showed €59 a quarter; Jobscan shows its price only after sign-up. All 11 tools offer some free check.

### Is there an ATS resume checker for India?

Naukri360's ATS Resume Checker is free, takes an optional job description and shows a score band. Prices on the other checkers were in USD (or EUR, CAD) on the pages we opened; none showed INR. Our [Resume Keyword Checker](/resume-keyword-checker) is free everywhere.

### Can an ATS checker guarantee interviews?

No. It can only fix what software reads: missing skills and broken layout. Whether you get the call depends on your experience matching the role, how early you apply and your answers to the application questions.
