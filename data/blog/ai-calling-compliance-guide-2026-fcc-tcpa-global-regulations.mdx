**Is AI cold calling legal? In the US, Canada, the UK and Germany, an AI sales call to someone who has not agreed to automated calls breaks the law: an AI voice counts as an artificial or automated voice, and those calls need consent first. In India, the UAE, Australia and Singapore, AI cold calls are legal with conditions: registered numbers, do-not-call checks, calling hours and, in India, telling your operator in advance that software places the calls. An AI answering calls people make to you is fine everywhere we checked.**

<KeyFacts>

- **The US did not ban AI calls; it put them under consent rules.** The FCC ruled that the TCPA's "artificial or prerecorded voice" rules "encompass current AI technologies that generate human voices" ([FCC 24-17](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf)). A sales call with an AI voice to a mobile needs prior express written consent, and each violation can cost \$500 in a lawsuit, up to \$1,500 if wilful ([47 U.S.C. 227](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/47/227)).
- **B2B is not a free pass in four countries.** The US mobile rule covers business mobiles, Canada's automated-call rules "apply whether or not the telemarketing telecommunication is exempt" from the Do Not Call list ([CRTC](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/trules-reglest.htm)), the UK rule covers any "subscriber", and Germany requires consent for advertising by automatic calling machine from any addressee, business or consumer ([UWG § 7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__7.html)).
- **The UK counts an AI voice as recorded material:** "no part of a direct marketing call can include recorded material without consent" ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-and-services/innovation-advice/previously-asked-questions/)). Fines now reach £17.5m or 4% of global turnover ([ICO](https://cy.ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/what-we-do/legislation-we-cover/data-use-and-access-act-2025/how-we-will-regulate-as-the-data-use-and-access-act-commences)).
- **India amended its rules on 18 Sep 2026:** calls placed by software, AI agents included, are A2P calls that must be declared to your operator in advance with their number ranges, from 60 days after publication; undeclared calls are treated as unsolicited commercial communication ([TRAI](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/Regulation_18092026.pdf)).
- **Telling every caller up front that it is an AI is law only in the EU so far** (AI Act Article 50, from 2 Aug 2026); Utah requires it when the person asks. The US federal proposal from 2024 has no final date; Canada's regulator asked the question in June 2026.

</KeyFacts>

## Does an AI voice count as a robocall?

**Yes, in every country that has ruled on it.** The FCC said AI-generated voices are "artificial" voices under the TCPA in February 2024 ([FCC 24-17](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf)). The UK's ICO says an AI-produced greeting is recorded material on a marketing call ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-and-services/innovation-advice/previously-asked-questions/)). Singapore's Do Not Call guidelines define a voice call to include "a call that involves a recorded or synthetic voice" ([PDPC](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/Files/PDPC/PDF-Files/Advisory-Guidelines/Advisory-Guidelines-on-the-DNC-Provisions-1-Feb-2021.pdf)).

Two places have not ruled yet. Canada's CRTC asked in June 2026 whether its automatic dialing-announcing device (ADAD) definition is "sufficient to capture software, applications, or technologies that use synthesized voices" ([CRTC 2026-132](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/archive/2026/2026-132.htm)); replies closed on 11 Aug 2026 and we found no decision. Germany's UWG says "automatische Anrufmaschine" and no court has tested a conversational AI against it that we found. In both, the safe reading is that an AI caller is an automated caller.

## Do AI callers have to say they are AI?

**Up front, for every call, only the EU makes it law today.** Article 50 of the AI Act requires that people are told they are interacting with an AI system "at the latest at the time of the first interaction", and the European Commission confirms "these transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026" ([Commission guidelines, 20 Jul 2026](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/guidelines-transparency-obligations-providers-and-deployers-ai-systems)). The 2026 Digital Omnibus delayed other parts of the Act, not this one.

In the US, the FCC proposed in 2024 that callers disclose AI-generated voices; the 2026 Unified Agenda lists it as "Next Action Undetermined" ([reginfo.gov](https://www.reginfo.gov/public/do/eAgendaViewRule?pubId=202510&RIN=3060-AM12&operation=OPERATION_PRINT_RULE)). Utah already requires a business to disclose generative AI "if the individual asks or otherwise prompts" ([Utah Code 13-77-103](https://le.utah.gov/xcode/Title13/Chapter77/C13-77-S103_2025050720250507.html)). Every country we checked requires a caller ID or caller identification.

Our advice is the same everywhere: say it in the first sentence. It is required in the EU, likely coming elsewhere, and a buyer who finds out later stops trusting the call.

## Before an AI dials anyone

<AiCallingPreDial />

1. **Where and who.** The rules follow the person you call, not you. A Bengaluru team calling London follows UK rules.
2. **Consent.** Wording on your form that names your company and covers AI or automated calls, stored with the date, page and number. "I agree to be contacted" on a whitepaper download is weaker than it looks.
3. **Registers.** US National Do Not Call Registry for live sales calls, the UK's TPS and CTPS, Canada's National DNCL, the UAE's DNCR, Australia's Do Not Call Register, Singapore's DNC Registry, India's DND preferences.
4. **Opening line.** "Hi, this is Asha, an AI assistant calling for Northwind about the demo you booked. You can say stop at any time." Business name, AI, reason, a way out.
5. **Stop and log.** In the US, a "stop" said in any reasonable way must be honoured within 10 business days at the latest ([47 CFR 64.1200](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200)). Keep consent, call and opt-out records together.

## What to do if you are...

- **A US SDR team with a bought list.** Do not point an AI voice at it. Let reps dial, scrub the National Do Not Call Registry for consumer numbers, and use AI to prepare: research, scripts and practice. Collect written AI-call consent on every form so follow-ups can be automated.
- **A UK or German B2B agency.** Automated calls need prior consent even to companies. A person may call businesses (after a CTPS check in the UK; where presumed consent is plausible in Germany). Use AI for inbound and for calling people who opted in.
- **An Indian sales team (BFSI, edtech, real estate).** AI calling is allowed: use 140-series numbers as a registered sender, declare software-placed calls and their number ranges to your operator, skip DND-blocked categories, and wait 90 days before asking an opted-out person again. Plan for DPDP notice and consent duties from 13 May 2027.
- **Calling Dubai or Abu Dhabi.** Get the competent authority's prior approval for phone marketing first; automated systems are then expressly allowed. Call only between 9 a.m. and 6 p.m., never DNCR numbers, from licensed local numbers, with a recording notice at the start. Fines reach AED 150,000 for a company ([Resolution 57 of 2024](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2523/download)).
- **Building an AI receptionist.** Inbound is outside these calling rules. Say it is an AI, offer a person, and check recording consent where you are.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AI cold calling illegal?

Not everywhere. Without consent it is unlawful in the US, Canada, the UK and Germany, because an AI voice counts as an artificial or automated voice. In India, the UAE, Australia and Singapore it is legal with conditions such as registered numbers, do-not-call checks and calling hours.

### Did the FCC ban AI calls?

No. The February 2024 ruling put AI voices under the existing TCPA rules for artificial voices. AI calls with the right consent are legal; AI sales calls to mobiles or homes without prior express written consent are not ([FCC 24-17](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf)).

### Is AI cold calling legal for B2B?

In the US, only to company landlines without consent; business mobiles need prior express written consent for AI sales calls, and many B2B data lists are mobile numbers. Canada, the UK and Germany require consent for automated sales calls to businesses too. Singapore excludes messages to organisations, Australia's register cannot hold business numbers, and the UAE's rules protect natural persons without saying whether an employee on a work line counts, so apply them anyway.

### Is AI cold calling legal in the UK?

Only with the person's prior consent. PECR regulation 19 needs consent for automated marketing calls with recorded matter, and the ICO says an AI voice counts. A human may cold call after screening the TPS or CTPS.

### Is AI cold calling legal in India?

Yes, with conditions: promotional calls only from 140-series numbers by a registered sender, respecting DND preferences, and AI or software-placed calls declared to your operator in advance under TRAI's September 2026 amendment. India has no rule written for AI voices.

### Can my AI call existing customers?

Not on the strength of the relationship alone in the four strict countries. In the US, the rules for artificial-voice sales calls to mobiles and homes ask for prior express written consent and have no past-customer exception. In the UK, regulation 19 needs consent for automated marketing calls whatever your history with the person. In Canada, a business relationship exempts you from the Do Not Call list but not from the automated-call consent rule. Service calls about an existing order are a different, easier case: run them through the check above.

### Can an AI leave voicemails?

In the US, a ringless or dropped voicemail with an artificial voice "requires consumer consent because it is a 'call'" ([FCC](https://www.fcc.gov/node/254946)). Treat an AI voicemail as an AI call in every country.

### Can I clone my own voice for outbound calls?

A clone of your voice is still an artificial voice in the US and recorded material in the UK, so the same consent rules apply. Get written consent from anyone whose voice you clone, and still say the call uses AI.

### What happens if I break these rules?

In the US, people can sue for \$500 per call, up to \$1,500 if wilful. The UK's ICO can fine up to £17.5m or 4% of global turnover. Canada's CRTC can impose up to CAD 15,000 per violation on a business ([CRTC](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/dncl/rpt260930/p4.htm)). India bars the sender's outgoing calls for 15 days, then disconnects and blacklists ([TRAI](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf)).
