**For 100 learners, published year-one LMS prices in Australia run from A\$380 (MoodleCloud, billed in AUD) to up to about A\$13,865 (360Learning, priced in US dollars per active user), before GST. Of the 16 learning platforms we checked, 11 publish no price we could turn into Australian dollars at that size, 8 say they can host your data in Australia, and only Workday names an IRAP assessment.**

<KeyFacts>

- **GST is 10%, but a registered business rarely bears it.** Give an offshore vendor your ABN and say you are GST-registered, and it should not charge GST; if it does, ask it for a refund. A vendor that invoices through an Australian company adds GST like any local supplier, and you claim it back as a credit.
- **No law makes ordinary employers host training data in Australia.** APP 8 lets data go overseas if you take reasonable steps, and you stay accountable for what the vendor does with it.
- **10 December 2026 is the next date to note.** From then, an APP entity's privacy policy must say when a computer program uses personal information to make, or help make, decisions that could reasonably be expected to significantly affect someone. AI scoring in an LMS can qualify.

</KeyFacts>

## Which kind of LMS do you need?

Australian buyers fall into four groups, and the shortlist differs for each. Pick yours first.

- **Corporate L&D (onboarding, compliance, upskilling):** you need HR integration, manager reports and a clear privacy position. Look at ELMO, Go1, Absorb, Docebo, LearnUpon, Litmos, iSpring, TalentLMS, 360Learning, Workday and Cornerstone.
- **Frontline and safety teams:** you need short lessons on phones and records you can show a WHS inspector. Look at SafetyCulture Training, Go1, ELMO, Employment Hero and Absorb.
- **Sales teams:** you need product knowledge, certification and practice on real calls. Look at Docebo, LearnUpon, Litmos and 360Learning, or keep your LMS and add a practice layer.
- **Course sellers and RTOs:** you sell courses or issue qualifications, so you need payments or VET reporting. Look at MoodleCloud and Thinkific for selling; RTOs usually shortlist Australian-built tools such as Cloud Assess (RTO plan "From \$600 / month", currency not named on its [pricing page](https://cloudassess.com/pricing/)) and aXcelerate. See the RTO note below.

## GST on an LMS subscription, explained

**GST is ["a broad-based tax of 10%"](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/how-gst-works) on most goods and services, and an LMS subscription is no exception. Who pays it depends on where the vendor sells from and on your own registration.**

| You buy from               | You are                                  | What happens to GST                                           |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| An Australian vendor       | GST-registered                           | Charged on the invoice; you claim it back as a credit         |
| An offshore vendor         | GST-registered, full credits             | Should not be charged once you give your ABN and registration |
| An offshore vendor         | Registered, no full credit (e.g. a bank) | You reverse-charge 10% yourself                               |
| Any vendor that charges it | Not registered                           | A cost to you, with nothing to claim back                     |

The rules behind the table:

- **Offshore vendors.** The ATO tells Australian businesses to give the supplier their ABN and state that they are registered for GST ([ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/in-detail/rules-for-specific-transactions/international-transactions/australian-business-importing-goods-and-services)). Most offshore LMS vendors then invoice without GST. If one charges it anyway, ask it for a refund: an offshore vendor registered under the simplified system has no ABN, so its invoice is not one you can claim a credit on.
- **Reverse charge.** It ["applies if the customer would not be entitled to a full GST credit"](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/international-tax-for-business/gst-for-non-resident-businesses/gst-cross-border-transactions-between-businesses), which mostly means banks, insurers and others making input-taxed supplies.
- **Threshold.** You must register at a GST turnover of \$75,000 or more (\$150,000 for non-profits) ([ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/businesses-and-organisations/gst-excise-and-indirect-taxes/gst/registering-for-gst)). Below that, unless you registered voluntarily, GST charged to you is part of the price.

One practical check: a price in US dollars moves with the exchange rate. At the RBA's 10 Nov 2026 rate, A\$1 bought US\$0.6924 ([RBA](https://www.rba.gov.au/rss/rss-cb-exchange-rates.xml)), so a \$9,600 plan costs about A\$13,865. Ask for an AUD price locked for the contract term.

## Privacy Act, APP 8 and hosting: what applies to your LMS

**An LMS holds your staff's names, results and sometimes recordings, so the Privacy Act 1988 usually applies. It does not force Australian hosting, but it does make you answerable for an overseas vendor.**

- **A private employer's own staff records are mostly exempt; your vendor probably is not.** The employee records exemption covers an employer's records about "training, disciplining, resignation or termination". But the OAIC says it is ["unlikely to apply to organisations that provide ... training ... services under contract to an employer"](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/employee-records-exemption). Contractors, subcontractors, future hires and unsuccessful applicants are outside it, and government agencies get no such exemption.
- **Data can go overseas, with care.** Before disclosing personal information overseas, APP 8 says you ["must take such steps as are reasonable in the circumstances to ensure that the recipient does not breach the APPs"](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-8-app-8-cross-border-disclosure-of-personal-information), and section 16C can make you accountable for what the recipient does. One nuance: if the vendor only stores data for you and you keep it under your "effective control", the OAIC can treat that as a use, not a disclosure. Australian hosting by a foreign-owned provider is residency, not sovereignty, so ask about access from overseas too.
- **Small businesses are mostly outside the Act.** The OAIC defines a small business as one with ["an annual turnover of \$3 million or less"](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/small-business). Some are covered at any size, including health service providers, businesses that trade in personal information and Commonwealth contractors.
- **Breaches have a clock.** You must take all reasonable steps to assess a suspected breach within 30 calendar days, and, if it is an eligible breach likely to result in serious harm, notify the people at risk and the OAIC as soon as practicable. If the breach affects both you and your vendor, ["only one entity needs to notify"](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/notifiable-data-breaches/quick-reference-guide-for-responding-to-data-breaches), so agree in the contract who that is.
- **AI features meet a new rule on 10 December 2026.** From that date, APP entities must say in their privacy policy when computer programs use personal information to make decisions that could significantly affect people ([OAIC](https://www.oaic.gov.au/news/media-centre/new-resources-on-transparency-for-use-of-ai-and-automated-decision-making)). If your LMS auto-scores assessments that feed certification or promotion, list it. For your own current staff the employee records exemption may cover the scoring; for contractors, applicants or customers you train, it does not.

<details>
<summary>Banks, insurers, government and health: the stricter cases</summary>

**APRA-regulated entities:** CPS 230 has applied since 1 July 2025, and to contracts signed before then from the earlier of their next renewal or 1 July 2026. An APRA-regulated entity ["must identify and maintain a register of its material service providers"](https://www.apra.gov.au/system/files/2023-07/Prudential%20Standard%20CPS%20230%20Operational%20Risk%20Management%20-%20clean.pdf), each under a legally binding agreement. CPS 234 adds that it must assess a third party's information security capability and tell APRA about material incidents within 72 hours ([CPS 234](https://www.apra.gov.au/sites/default/files/cps_234_july_2019_for_public_release.pdf)).

**Government agencies:** expect to need Australian hosting and an IRAP assessment against the ISM. The ASD's guide warns that a completed assessment ["does not imply that a system is compliant, endorsed or approved by ASD"](https://cyber.gov.au/sites/default/files/2024-08/IRAP%20consumer%20guide.pdf), so read the report, its date and its scope.

**Health providers:** state laws such as Victoria's [Health Records Act 2001](https://www.health.vic.gov.au/legislation/health-records-act) apply on top of the Privacy Act. Keep real patient details out of courses and roleplays.

</details>

## Training duties: WHS and RTO rules

**Two rules decide what your LMS has to prove.**

- **WHS training is a legal duty.** Under the model WHS Act, a business must ensure, so far as reasonably practicable, ["the provision of any information, training, instruction or supervision that is necessary to protect all persons"](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2011A00137/2024-07-01/2024-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) (section 19). Completion records with dates are your evidence, so check you can export them. Victoria has its own OHS Act rather than the model Act, so check its wording if you operate there.
- **Only an RTO can issue qualifications.** ASQA says ["only registered providers can deliver nationally recognised vocational education and training"](https://www.asqa.gov.au/for-providers/about-providers). Internal, non-accredited training on an LMS needs no registration; if you issue units of competency, you need an RTO and an LMS that supports its reporting.

## Questions to send every vendor

Paste these into your RFP. Tick each one when you get a written answer.

- [ ] In which region is our learner data stored, including backups and disaster recovery?
- [ ] Which sub-processors (cloud, video, AI model providers) see our data, and where are they?
- [ ] Will you sign contract terms that meet APP 8 and say who notifies the OAIC after a breach?
- [ ] Do you hold ISO 27001, SOC 2 or an IRAP assessment, and can we see the report and its scope?
- [ ] Is the price in AUD, fixed for the term, and does it include GST?
- [ ] Do you price per registered user or per monthly active user, and is there a minimum?
- [ ] Which AI features make or suggest decisions about learners, and can we switch them off?
- [ ] Does the mobile app work offline for frontline and shift staff?
- [ ] Which HR systems and single sign-on providers do you integrate with out of the box?
- [ ] What do setup, content and implementation cost, on top of the subscription?
- [ ] Which entity invoices us, and does it charge GST with an ABN?
- [ ] Do any AI features send our data outside Australia, even when hosting is in Australia?
- [ ] How do you define an active user, and what happens in a month when everyone logs in?
- [ ] Can we export all completion records and delete our data when we leave?

## FAQ

**Which is the best LMS in Australia?** For a low published price, MoodleCloud (A\$380 a year for 100 users) and TalentLMS. For Australian hosting with an APP statement, ELMO. For a big course library, Go1. For an IRAP-assessed vendor, Workday. Most others quote privately.

**How much does an LMS cost in Australia?** For 100 learners, published year-one prices run from A\$380 (MoodleCloud) to up to about A\$13,865 (360Learning, converted from US dollars), before GST. Eleven of the 16 platforms we checked publish no price we could convert at that size.

**Is GST charged on LMS software in Australia?** Yes, 10% when an Australian vendor sells to you. A GST-registered business that gives an offshore vendor its ABN should not be charged GST, and reverse-charges it only if it cannot claim a full credit.

**Does an LMS have to be hosted in Australia?** Not under the Privacy Act. APP 8 allows overseas disclosure if you take reasonable steps, and you stay accountable. Government agencies and APRA-regulated entities usually need stricter assurance, such as Australian hosting and an IRAP assessment.

**Does the employee records exemption cover our LMS?** It covers your own handling of current and former employees' training records. It applies to private-sector employers, not to contractors or job applicants. The OAIC says it is unlikely to cover a training provider working under contract, so your LMS vendor should still follow the APPs.
