If your reps talk, buy an AI parallel dialer: [Trellus](https://www.trellus.ai/pricing) (\$149.99 a seat a month, no minimum), [Salesfinity](https://salesfinity.ai/pricing) (\$249) and [Regie.ai](https://regie.ai/pricing) (\$41 for one person, \$125 a seat for teams) publish prices; [Orum](https://www.orum.com/pricing) and [Nooks](https://nooks.ai/pricing) quote on request. <KeyFacts>

- **Three kinds of tool, three bills:** AI voice agents bill by the minute, AI parallel dialers by the seat, phone systems by the user. Pick the kind first ([picker below](#picker)).
- **Price secrecy:** 4 of the 17 tools publish no price at all (Synthflow, Orum, Nooks, Koncert); Orum also sets a 9-seat minimum.
- **AI voices are robocalls in the US.** The FCC ruled that the TCPA's "artificial or prerecorded voice" rules "encompass current AI technologies that generate human voices" ([FCC 24-17](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf)). Marketing calls with one to mobiles need prior express written consent.
- **Parallel dialers drop pickups.** US telemarketing campaigns may abandon no more than 3% of live-answered calls over 30 days, and a call is abandoned if no rep is on within 2 seconds of the "hello" ([47 CFR 64.1200(a)(7)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/47/64.1200)). The UK "3%" many vendor pages still quote was dropped by Ofcom in 2016.

## The rules that bite cold-calling tools

**Two rules decide which tool you may use: AI voices need prior consent in the US and UK, and parallel dialers must not leave answered calls hanging.** Vendor lists rarely mention the second one.

<ColdCallingRules />

What this means in practice:

- **A parallel dialer is not an AI voice.** Your rep talks, so the artificial-voice consent rule does not apply to the conversation. The Do Not Call rules for live sales calls still do.
- **The 3% cap is not only for consumer calls.** The rule covers "all telemarketing calls that are answered live", and its definition of telemarketing does not stop at consumers. On a dropped pickup the rule expects a short recorded message with your business name and a number for do-not-call requests, unanswered calls must ring at least 15 seconds or four rings, and you must keep records of your rate.
- **The second pickup is the risk.** When two people answer at once, the dialer can connect the rep to only one. Ask the vendor what the other person hears, how fast, and how the tool reports your abandoned-call rate per campaign.
- **The UK changed in 2017.** Ofcom's current policy says: "We have not included as a prioritisation criterion in the revised policy a reference to a specific abandoned call rate" ([Ofcom, A4.27](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0024/96135/Persistent-Misuse-Policy-Statement.pdf)). It still looks at abandoned and silent calls as misuse, expects an information message within about 2 seconds, and treats a withheld caller ID as aggravating.
- **India:** every promotional call, from a rep or an AI, goes out from a 140xx number ([TRAI 91/2026](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/PR_No91of2026.pdf)).

For 8 countries and a call-by-call check, see [is AI cold calling legal? AI calling laws by country](/blog/ai-calling-compliance-guide-2026-fcc-tcpa-global-regulations).

## 8 questions to ask any vendor before you sign

**Ask these on the demo call; a vendor that cannot answer them in writing is a risk.**

1. What does a person hear if they answer while the rep is on another call, and after how many seconds?
2. Can you report our abandoned-call rate per campaign over 30 days?
3. How many lines per rep can an admin set, and can we set it to one for consumer lists?
4. Whose phone numbers do we dial from, and how do you register them with carriers and watch for spam labels?
5. Is caller ID shown on every call, and is it a number people can call back?
6. What do phone minutes cost to our countries, on top of the seat or minute price?
7. Is there a seat minimum or an annual-only contract?
8. For AI agents: does the agent say it is an AI and who it calls for, and can it stop calling someone who says "stop" in any words?

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AI cold calling software?

For cold lists, an AI parallel dialer where your reps talk: Trellus, Salesfinity and Regie.ai publish prices; Orum, Nooks and Koncert quote on request. For consented callbacks, an AI voice agent such as Retell or Bland, priced per minute. The right one depends on team size, seat minimums and where you call.

### Can AI make cold calls?

Technically yes; legally it depends on the country. In the US an AI voice counts as an artificial voice, so marketing calls to mobiles and home lines need prior express written consent, which a cold list does not have. The UK also needs prior consent for automated marketing calls with recorded material, and the ICO counts an AI voice as recorded material.

### What is the difference between an AI dialer and an AI voice agent?

An AI dialer dials for a human: it rings several numbers, skips voicemail and connects your rep to whoever answers. An AI voice agent is the caller: it speaks, listens and books on its own. Dialers bill per seat; agents bill per minute.

### Is a parallel dialer legal?

Yes, if the campaign stays within the abandoned-call rules. In the US, no more than 3% of live-answered telemarketing calls per campaign over 30 days may be abandoned, a call counts as abandoned if no rep is on the line within 2 seconds of the greeting, and the rule is not limited to consumer calls. Ask your vendor how it reports this.

### Is the UK abandoned call limit still 3%?

No. The 3% figure was in Ofcom's 2010 policy. Ofcom's current policy, in force since 1 March 2017, sets no specific abandoned-call rate. It still treats abandoned and silent calls as possible misuse and looks at how quickly an information message plays and whether caller ID is shown.

### How much does AI cold calling cost?

AI voice agents: \$0.07–\$0.31 a minute at Retell, \$0.14 at Bland (no-fee plan), \$0.25–\$0.29 at Aircall, so 1,000 minutes cost roughly \$70–\$310 a month before phone numbers. AI parallel dialers: \$125–\$249 a seat a month where published, or \$7,500–\$14,940 a year for 5 reps.

### Orum vs Nooks: which is better?

Both ring up to 5 lines at once and publish no price. Orum states a 9-seat minimum, detects screeners, voicemail and phone trees, and names Salesforce, Outreach and Salesloft in its help pages; Nooks names HubSpot and Salesforce and lists spam protection with number rotation and reputation monitoring. Get both quotes for the same seat count and ask the 8 questions above.

### Why are my numbers flagged as spam?

Carriers verify caller ID with STIR/SHAKEN and analytics apps label numbers that make many short or unanswered calls. Dial from numbers registered to your business, keep call volume per number reasonable, show a number people can call back, and ask your dialer vendor how it monitors reputation.

### Do I have to tell people they are talking to an AI?

In the EU, AI Act Article 50 applies from 2 August 2026: AI systems that talk with people must be built so the person is told they are dealing with an AI ([European Commission FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act)). US rules require the business name at the start of an artificial-voice call and a number to call back, and some states add their own disclosure laws. Saying "this is an AI assistant calling for [company]" is the safe default everywhere.

### Will AI replace SDRs?

Not on cold lists in the US or UK, where an AI voice needs prior consent. The pattern that fits the rules is AI doing the dialling, research and voicemail sorting while reps talk, plus AI agents on consented inbound and follow-up calls.
