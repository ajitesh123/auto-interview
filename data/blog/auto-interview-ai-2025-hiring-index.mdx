## Why You MUST Check Your ATS Score Before Applying

### The Brutal Truth About ATS Systems

**75% of resumes are rejected before a human ever sees them.**

Here's how it works:

1. You submit your resume → Company's ATS software scans it
2. ATS parses text, identifies keywords, scores against job description
3. If score < 70-75 → Auto-rejected (no human review)
4. Only top-scoring resumes reach recruiters

## Top 5 Reasons Resumes Failed ATS Scans (Aug-Nov 2025)

### 1. Formatting Errors (42% of Failures)

**What Breaks ATS**:

- Tables, text boxes, columns
- Headers/footers with key info
- Fancy fonts, graphics, icons
- PDF with images (not text-based)

**Fix**: Use our ATS-friendly templates with simple, clean formatting.

---

### 2. Missing Domain Keywords (31% of Failures)

**Problem**: Generic resumes that don't match the specific role's terminology.

**Example**:

- ❌ "Managed projects"
- ✅ "Led cross-functional go-to-market initiatives"

**Fix**: Use the domain tables above to identify your gaps.

---

### 3. No Quantified Achievements (18% of Failures)

**ATS looks for numbers**: Revenue, %, team size, timeline, users, etc.

**Example**:

- ❌ "Improved sales performance"
- ✅ "Increased sales by 43% ($2.1M→$3M) across 5-state territory in 8 months"

**Fix**: Add metrics to every bullet point.

---

### 4. Generic Job Titles (6% of Failures)

**Problem**: "Specialist," "Analyst," "Manager" without domain context.

**Fix**:

- ❌ Business Analyst
- ✅ SaaS Product Analyst | Data & Growth

---

### 5. Outdated Skills Listed (3% of Failures)

**Dead weight**: Java 6, Flash, outdated frameworks.

**Fix**: Remove anything 5+ years old unless still industry-standard.

## Domain-Specific Action Plans

### For Product Managers

1. Add "cross-functional," "stakeholder management," "go-to-market" to your bullets
2. Quantify impact: revenue, users, retention, time-to-market
3. Use STAR format for achievement bullets

### For Software Engineers

1. Highlight "system design," "microservices," "CI/CD" in projects
2. Add metrics: performance improvements, scale handled, uptime
3. Update tech stack (remove outdated, add modern frameworks)

### For Data Analysts

1. Lead with "SQL," "Python," add specific tools (Tableau, etc.)
2. Show business impact of insights (revenue, cost savings, decisions influenced)
3. Include end-to-end projects (data → insight → action)

### For Marketing Managers

1. Emphasize "growth marketing," "CAC," "conversion optimization"
2. Prove ROI with channel-specific metrics
3. Show multi-channel orchestration experience

### For Sales Professionals

1. Lead with quota attainment % (e.g., "147% of quota, $3.2M in ARR")
2. Include "pipeline management," CRM proficiency
3. Highlight deal sizes and sales cycle optimization

### For Consultants

1. Use "strategic planning," "stakeholder engagement," "change management"
2. Quantify client impact (cost savings, revenue increase, efficiency gains)
3. Name recognizable clients (if allowed) or industries

### For Finance Professionals

1. Start with "financial modeling," "FP&A," "budgeting"
2. Show strategic impact beyond reporting (decisions enabled, savings identified)
3. Include certifications (CPA, CFA, etc.) prominently
