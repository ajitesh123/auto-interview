## The Hidden Costs Nobody Tells You About

The true sales training cost in India extends far beyond the trainer's invoice. Corporate decision-makers frequently overlook the massive logistical and operational expenditures required to execute a traditional classroom session.

Second, trainer travel, accommodation, and per diems add another ₹10,000 to ₹25,000. Third, printed materials, workbooks, and visual aids consume ₹5,000 to ₹15,000.

## Why Knowledge Retention Is the Real ROI Metric

Budgeting for sales training in India is meaningless if the representatives fail to retain and execute the strategies. The fundamental flaw in traditional corporate training is human neurology, specifically the Ebbinghaus forgetting curve.

Research by Hermann Ebbinghaus demonstrates that individuals forget 84% of passively consumed lecture content within 30 days. Traditional training operates entirely within this flawed paradigm. The only proven mechanism to reverse this curve is spaced repetition and deliberate practice. Deliberate practice through active roleplay permanently outperforms passive listening.
