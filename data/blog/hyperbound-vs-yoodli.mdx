## The Core Difference: Sales Simulator vs. Speech Coach

Most comparison guides list features. We are going to look at **intent**.

### Hyperbound: The "Flight Simulator" for Sales
[Hyperbound](https://hyperbound.ai) is built for **revenue teams**. Its core engine is designed to replicate the friction of a sales call. It doesn't just listen to *how* you say things; it listens to *what* you say and pushes back.
*   **Primary Use Case:** Cold calling, discovery calls, objection handling.
*   **The "Magic" Moment:** When the AI prospect interrupts you or gives you a "soft no," forcing you to pivot instantly.

### Yoodli: The "Toastmasters" in Your Pocket
[Yoodli](https://yoodli.ai) is built for **communicators**. Its roots are in public speaking and presentation coaching. It cares deeply about your delivery mechanics.
*   **Primary Use Case:** Preparing for a keynote, practicing a pitch deck, improving executive presence.
*   **The "Magic" Moment:** Seeing a transcript of your speech with every "um," "ah," and "like" highlighted, along with a pacing graph.

### 1. AI Roleplay Realism
**[Hyperbound](https://hyperbound.ai)** shines here for sales. You can configure a "Grumpy CFO" or a "Busy VP of Engineering." The AI mimics their tone and typical pushback. It feels like a battle.

**[Yoodli](https://yoodli.ai)** offers roleplay, but it feels more like a structured interview. It’s excellent for "Tell me about yourself" or "Why should we hire you?" but struggles with the chaotic, non-linear nature of a cold call.

**Insider Tip:** Realism isn't just about the voice; it's about the *latency*. Sales calls happen in milliseconds. If the AI takes 3 seconds to respond, the illusion breaks.

### 2. Feedback Granularity
**Yoodli** is the king of delivery metrics. If you have a bad habit of saying "basically" every sentence, Yoodli will catch it. It tracks your eye contact (if using video) and your smiling.

**Hyperbound** focuses on outcome. Did you book the meeting? Did you overcome the objection? It cares less about your "ums" and more about your *win rate*.
