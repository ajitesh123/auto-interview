## 3. Psychometric and Acoustic Evaluation Metrics

Modern AI interview engines evaluate both **linguistic content** and **paralinguistic acoustic delivery**.

```text
Multimodal Candidate Evaluation Dimensions:

1. Content & Domain Competence (60% Weight):
   - Correctness of technical architecture explanation.
   - Alignment with role requirements and past project claims.

2. Behavioral Delivery & Structure (25% Weight):
   - Adherence to the STAR framework.
   - Precision and conciseness (avoiding tangential rambling).

3. Acoustic Delivery & Fluency (15% Weight):
   - Speech pacing (Target: 130 to 160 WPM).
   - Pitch variance (\sigma_{F_0}^2) measuring vocal confidence vs nervousness.
```

### Mathematical Formulation of Candidate Delivery Score

The acoustic delivery index $D_{\text{score}}$ combines normalized speech rate $R$, filler density $F$, and pitch stability:

$$D_{\text{score}} = \alpha \cdot \exp\left(-\frac{(R - 145)^2}{2\sigma_R^2}\right) + \beta \cdot (1 - F) + \gamma \cdot \left(1 - \frac{\sigma_{F_0}}{\mu_{F_0}}\right)$$

This mathematical scoring provides an objective, repeatable benchmark across thousands of candidate submissions.

### 4. Acoustic Pitch and Energy Formant Tracking

To measure candidate confidence and nervousness without human interviewer bias, the front-end processor computes fundamental frequency ($F_0$) contours across **128-channel Log-Mel spectrograms**:

$$m = 2595 \log_{10}\left(1 + \frac{f}{700}\right)$$

When candidates experience cognitive stress, vocal jitter and rapid micro-tremors appear in the higher formant frequencies ($F_2$ and $F_3$). By analyzing these acoustic waveforms over 25ms windows with a 10ms frame stride, the Conformer encoder measures pacing stability without penalizing regional accents.

## 6. Python Implementation: Automated Interview Rubric Evaluation

Below is a complete Python implementation illustrating how candidate transcripts and audio metrics are parsed against a structured STAR evaluation rubric:

```python
import asyncio
from typing import Dict, Any

class AIInterviewEvaluator:
    async def evaluate_candidate_turn(self, question: str, answer_text: str, wpm: float, filler_count: int) -> Dict[str, Any]:
        """
        Evaluates candidate response across technical depth, STAR structure, and acoustic fluency.
        """
        await asyncio.sleep(0.05) # Simulated 50ms evaluation inference

        # Calculate structure score based on STAR keywords
        star_markers = ["situation", "task", "action", "result", "metric", "scaled"]
        matched_markers = [m for m in star_markers if m in answer_text.lower()]
        structure_score = min(10.0, len(matched_markers) * 2.0)

        # Calculate fluency score
        fluency_penalty = min(5.0, filler_count * 0.5)
        fluency_score = max(0.0, 10.0 - fluency_penalty)

        composite_rating = round((structure_score * 0.6) + (fluency_score * 0.4), 2)

        return {
            "question": question,
            "speech_rate_wpm": wpm,
            "filler_words_detected": filler_count,
            "star_structure_score": structure_score,
            "fluency_score": fluency_score,
            "composite_score_out_of_10": composite_rating,
            "recommended_next_action": "advance_to_technical_round" if composite_rating >= 7.0 else "review_needed"
        }
```
