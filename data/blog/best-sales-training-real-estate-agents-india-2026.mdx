## 6 High-Ticket Real Estate Objection Scenarios Every Agent Must Master

Selling a ₹2 crore apartment requires vastly different skills than selling a ₹2,000 software subscription. The stakes are immense, and buyers are rightfully defensive. Real estate agents must master exact verbal frameworks to overcome stalling tactics. Here are six high-ticket objection scenarios, complete with word-for-word scripts to practice.

**1. "Your property is overpriced — I can get a similar flat for less nearby"**
_Script:_ "I completely understand checking the comparables in the area. The project down the road has a lower base price, but they use Mivan shuttering instead of conventional brickwork, and they lack the 40,000 square foot podium level our residents get. Are you looking strictly at the lowest square-foot price, or are you prioritizing the lifestyle and build quality for your family?"

**2. "I'll wait for the market to drop before buying"**
_Script:_ "Many buyers feel that way, but history shows waiting usually costs more. Over the last five years, this specific corridor has seen a 12% year-on-year appreciation. Even if prices stagnate, construction costs and interest rates are trending upward. If we secure this unit today at the pre-launch price, you lock in immediate equity. Let's look at the financial breakdown."

**3. NRI Qualification: "I'm based abroad — how do I verify RERA compliance and documentation?"**
_Script:_ "That is a very valid concern for our NRI clients. This project is strictly MahaRERA registered, and I will share the exact RERA registration number and the official portal link via email right now. You can independently verify the land title, the builder's past delivery records, and the escrow account details. Shall we schedule a virtual site tour tomorrow at your local time?"

**4. Site-visit conversion: Converting a walk-in browser into a same-day booking**
_Script:_ "It is great that you loved the sample flat. We currently have only three units left with this exact east-facing Vastu-compliant layout. Because it is a weekend, we have five more site visits scheduled for this wing today. A fully refundable token amount of ₹1 lakh locks this specific unit for 48 hours while you consult your family. Shall we block it now before the evening rush?"

**5. "What's the rental yield? Is it better to rent or buy?"**
_Script:_ "If you are strictly looking at a 24-month horizon, renting is cheaper. But real estate in this IT corridor is an asset-building play. The current rental yield here is around 3.5%, but capital appreciation is tracking at 9%. Every EMI you pay builds equity, whereas rent is a 100% expense. Let me show you a 5-year ROI calculation comparing renting versus buying this exact unit."

**6. "The builder has a bad reputation / past delays"**
_Script:_ "I appreciate your directness. It is true the developer faced a six-month delay on their 2020 project due to pandemic supply chain issues. However, under the strict new RERA guidelines, project funds are locked in an escrow account, ensuring zero diversion. This current tower has already topped out three months ahead of schedule. Let us walk over to the actual site so you can see the construction pace yourself."
