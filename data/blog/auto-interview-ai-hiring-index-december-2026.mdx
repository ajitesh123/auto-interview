## 💡 Industry Secrets Recruiters Won't Tell You

### Secret #1: ATS Systems Are Dumber Than You Think

They can't read:

- ❌ Tables
- ❌ Text boxes
- ❌ Headers/Footers
- ❌ Fancy fonts
- ❌ Graphics/icons

**Fix:** Use our templates designed specifically for ATS parsing.

### Secret #2: Generic "Skills" Sections Don't Work

Listing "Communication" or "Leadership" without context = instant reject.

**Instead:** Embed keywords in achievement bullets:

- ❌ "Strong communication skills"
- ✅ "Facilitated cross-functional stakeholder alignment across 5 teams, improving go-to-market execution by 40%"

### Secret #3: Location Keyword Matters More Than You Think

If you write "Bengaluru" but the JD says "Bangalore" → ATS might miss it.

**Fix:** Use both spellings in your location field or target cities section.
