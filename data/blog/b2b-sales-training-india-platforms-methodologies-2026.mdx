## The Indian B2B Sales Crisis: Rapid Hiring, 40% Churn, and the Forgetting Curve

India is home to the world's most dynamic B2B tech, SaaS, and enterprise sales talent pool. However, Indian Chief Commercial Officers (CCOs) and VPs of Sales face three structural bottlenecks:

1. **High SDR & Inside Sales Turnover:** Junior sales reps turn over at 35% to 45% annually. Training a new hire only to have them leave after four months drains company capital.
2. **The "Passive Lecture" Trap:** Companies bring in external corporate coaches for a 2-day hotel workshop. Reps nod along, take notes, but on Monday morning, freeze when an enterprise buyer demands a 30% discount or refuses an initial discovery call.
3. **The Cross-Border Friction Gap:** Many Indian teams sell to US and European buyers at night. Domestic training firms have zero expertise in preparing reps for the aggressive directness of American buyers or the subtle politeness of British executives.
