## What Are Corporate Training Companies?

> **Definition:** **Corporate training companies** are specialized firms that design and deliver structured learning programs to improve employee performance. Unlike generic course libraries (Coursera, Udemy), they provide **customized curriculum**, **expert facilitation**, and **measurable behavior change** strategies aligned with business goals.

**Core Capabilities:**

- **Sales Methodology:** Frameworks for closing deals (e.g., SPIN, Challenger, RAIN)
- **Leadership Development:** Transitioning individual contributors to managers
- **Communication Skills:** Public speaking, presentation, and interpersonal dynamics
- **Executive Coaching:** High-touch development for C-suite leaders

## Buyer Decision Checklist

Use this 5-step framework to choose the right partner:

1.  **Define the Gap:** Is it a _knowledge_ gap (they don't know what to do) or a _skill_ gap (they know but can't do it)?
    - _Knowledge_ -> FranklinCovey / RAIN Group
    - _Skill_ -> Dale Carnegie / Tough Tongue AI
2.  **Check the Scale:**
    - _&lt;50 employees_ -> Public workshops (Dale Carnegie) or SaaS (Tough Tongue AI)
    - _>1,000 employees_ -> Enterprise subscription (FranklinCovey)
3.  **Delivery Preference:**
    - _Remote team?_ -> Virtual-first (RAIN Group) or AI (Tough Tongue)
