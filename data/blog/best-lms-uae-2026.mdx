## Which kind of LMS do you need?

UAE buyers fall into four groups, and the shortlist differs for each. Pick yours first.

- **Corporate L&D (onboarding, compliance, upskilling):** you need HR system integration, manager reports and Arabic for some staff. Look at TalentLMS, iSpring, 360Learning, Disprz, Docebo, Absorb, LearnUpon and Lumofy.
- **Sales teams:** you need product knowledge, certification and practice on real conversations. Look at Mindtickle, Disprz, 360Learning and Docebo, or keep your LMS and add a practice layer.
- **Training firms and course creators:** you sell courses, so you need payments, AED pricing and an Arabic storefront. Look at Paradiso, Thinkific and Teachable, and check the KHDA rules below.
- **Schools and universities:** you need open standards, Arabic and local support. Look at Classera, MoodleCloud, or Moodle through a Dubai partner.

## VAT on an LMS subscription, explained

**An LMS subscription is a taxable service at the UAE's standard VAT rate of 5%.** Article 3 of Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 sets ["a standard rate of 5%"](https://government.ae/-/media/Information-and-services/Finance-and-Investment/En-VATDecreeLawNo8of2017.pdf) on supplies and imports. Who pays it depends on where the vendor bills from and whether you are registered.

| You buy from               | You are                 | Who accounts for the 5%                      | Can you get it back?                           |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| A UAE vendor (AED invoice) | Any buyer               | Vendor charges it on the invoice             | Yes, as input tax, if you are VAT-registered   |
| A foreign vendor           | VAT-registered business | **You**, under the reverse charge (Art. 48)  | Yes, in the same return, if the use is taxable |
| A foreign vendor           | Not VAT-registered      | Depends on the vendor's own UAE registration | No                                             |

The rules behind the table:

- **Thresholds.** You must register at AED 375,000 of taxable supplies and imports a year and may register from AED 187,500. The Federal Tax Authority adds that ["this threshold is not applicable to foreign businesses"](https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/Vat.Topics/registration.for.vat.aspx).
- **Imports count.** An unregistered business whose imported services push it past AED 375,000 a year must register, then reverse-charge them.
- **No self-invoice since 2026.** Amendments in force from 1 January 2026 clarify that under the reverse charge "taxpayers are not required to issue invoices to themselves", though keeping the vendor's documents still matters for recovery ([Baker McKenzie, 8 Dec 2025](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/alerts/2025/12/united-arab-emirates-vat-and-tax-procedures-law-amendments)).
- **E-invoicing is coming.** Businesses with revenue of AED 50 million or more go live from 1 January 2027 and the rest from 1 July 2027 ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/ae/en/insights/tax-insights/implementation-of-the-electronic-invoicing-system-in-the-uae.html)). Ask a UAE vendor whether its invoicing is ready.

One practical check: MoodleCloud shows US dollars but says "all plans are billed annually in Australian Dollars (AUD)" ([plans](https://www.moodlecloud.com/standard-plans/)), so your card or bank's conversion fee applies on top.

## PDPL, DIFC or ADGM: which data law covers your LMS

**An LMS holds your staff's personal data, so a data protection law applies. Which one depends on your licence, not on the vendor.**

| If your company is                               | The law                                                                                                     | Registration fee                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Mainland or a free zone without its own data law | [UAE PDPL](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download) (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) | None in the law                                                                                                                                              |
| In DIFC                                          | DIFC Data Protection Law No. 5 of 2020                                                                      | [USD 750, then USD 250 a year](https://www.difc.com/business/registrars-and-commissioners/commissioner-of-data-protection/forms-fees) (non-DFSA, non-retail) |
| In ADGM                                          | ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021                                                                       | [USD 300, then USD 300 a year](https://adgm.com/operating-in-adgm/office-of-data-protection/fee-sections)                                                    |

What the federal PDPL says that matters for an LMS:

- **It is law, but not yet fully switched on.** It came into force on 2 January 2022. Companies get up to six months to comply after its Executive Regulations are issued (Art. 29); as of our check, trackers report they have not been published ([Business CISO, Sept 2026](https://business-ciso.com/blog/uae-pdpl-executive-regulations-status)).
- **Employment is a consent exception.** Consent is the default (Art. 4), but processing needed for obligations "in the field of employment" is a listed exception. Optional courses and pre-hire tests are safer with consent.
- **Data can leave the UAE, with a basis.** Transfers go to countries approved as adequate (Art. 22) or rely on other bases, such as the person's ["explicit consent"](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1972/download) (Art. 23).
- **It does not cover everything.** Government data, health and banking data with their own laws, and free zones with their own data law (DIFC, ADGM) are outside it (Art. 2).

<details>
<summary>Healthcare and government: the stricter cases</summary>

**Health data:** Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 says health data from services provided in the UAE ["may not be stored, processed, generated or transferred outside the State"](https://elaws.moj.gov.ae/UAE-MOJ_LC-En/00_Health/UAE-LC-En_2019-02-06_00002_Kait.html?val=EL1) except in defined cases. Keep patient cases out of a foreign-hosted LMS.

**Government and critical infrastructure:** the UAE Cyber Security Council's National Cloud Security Policy binds cloud providers and, as cloud buyers, government entities and critical infrastructure operators. Its sovereign tiers keep consumer data and encryption keys inside the UAE ([Al Tamimi, 15 Oct 2026](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update/the-technology-issue-2026/articles/new-data-sovereignty-rules-for-cloud-consumers-and-providers-in-the-uae/)). An ordinary private employer is not bound by it.

</details>

## Do you need KHDA approval?

**Usually only if you sell training to others in Dubai; free zones and Abu Dhabi have their own regimes.** Dubai's Administrative Resolution No. 2 of 2018 defines a training institute as one authorised to conduct training for ["others in the Emirate"](<https://dlp.dubai.gov.ae/Legislation%20Reference/2018/Administrative%20Resolution%20No.%20(2)%20of%202018.html>), and lets other businesses apply for approval of a single course. Internal training for your own staff on an LMS is a different activity; confirm with KHDA if you plan to charge outsiders.

## Questions to send every vendor

Paste these into your RFP. Tick each one when you get a written answer.

- [ ] In which country is our learner data stored, and can it be hosted in the UAE?
- [ ] Which sub-processors (cloud, video, AI model providers) see our data, and where?
- [ ] Will you sign PDPL Art. 22/23 transfer terms, or DIFC / ADGM standard clauses?
- [ ] Is the interface fully Arabic and right-to-left, including admin and mobile?
- [ ] Do you invoice from a UAE entity with a TRN, and is the price in AED or USD?
- [ ] Do you price per registered user or per monthly active user?
- [ ] Which AI features are included, and which are metered or add-ons?
- [ ] Does the mobile app work offline for frontline and shift staff?
- [ ] Which HR systems and single sign-on providers do you integrate with out of the box?
- [ ] What do setup and implementation cost, on top of the subscription?
- [ ] Can we export all learner records and delete them when we leave?

## Where an LMS stops: practice

An LMS delivers courses and records completions. It does not make a sales rep better at handling "your price is too high" from a Dubai procurement team, or a graduate better at a final-round interview. That takes speaking practice with feedback, repeated.

The UAE has a reason to measure training. Private companies with 50 or more employees have had to raise Emirati skilled hires by ["2 per cent annually"](https://u.ae/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/emiratis-employment-in-private-sector), reaching 10% by the end of 2026, and the government grants incentives to establishments that perform well "in the training and employment of citizens". Completions alone do not show that someone can do the job.

## FAQ

**Which is the best LMS in the UAE?** For a published price and a free start, TalentLMS and MoodleCloud. For a vendor with a Dubai entity, Docebo or Disprz. For an Arabic-first interface, Paradiso or Classera. None names a UAE hosting region on its public pages.

**How much does an LMS cost in the UAE?** For 100 learners, published year-one list prices run from AED 992 (MoodleCloud) to AED 35,256 (360Learning), before 5% VAT. Ten of the 17 platforms we checked publish no price at that size.

**Is VAT charged on LMS software in the UAE?** Yes, 5%. A UAE vendor adds it to the invoice. A VAT-registered company buying from abroad accounts for it under the reverse charge and recovers it in the same return if the use is taxable.

**Does UAE law require LMS data to stay in the UAE?** Not for ordinary employee training data. The PDPL allows transfers to adequate countries or on other bases such as explicit consent. Health data and government cloud workloads have stricter localisation rules.

**Which LMS supports Arabic and right-to-left?** Paradiso states a native right-to-left interface and Classera runs a full Arabic product. Absorb lists Arabic as a language, Docebo has a right-to-left setting, and 360Learning translates courses into Arabic. Ask for a demo of the admin screens in Arabic.
