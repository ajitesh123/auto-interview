> **Executive Summary & Quick Answer for Engineers**
>
> - **What is Latency in Voice AI?** Conversational latency is the total elapsed duration in milliseconds between the exact moment a human finishes speaking and the exact moment the AI agent emits its first audible sound wave back to the caller.
> - **The 200ms Psychoacoustic Threshold:** Natural human conversation operates with turn-taking gaps of **200ms to 300ms**. When voice AI latency exceeds **500ms**, callers perceive hesitation; when it exceeds **800ms**, callers experience the _"Hello?" moment_ and drop-off rates spike by **35%**.
> - **The Latency Stacking Trap:** Cascaded pipelines compound delays across Voice Activity Detection (**150ms**), Speech-to-Text (**200ms**), LLM Time-to-First-Token (**250ms**), and TTS synthesis (**100ms**), totaling **700ms+**. Native Voice-to-Voice models slash this turnaround to **&lt;200ms**.

## 1. The Psychoacoustics of Human Conversation

Human communication is governed by precise, unconscious social and biological timing rules.

```text
The Psychoacoustic Latency Spectrum:

0ms ──────── 200ms ──────── 400ms ──────── 600ms ──────── 800ms ──────── 1200ms+
 │             │              │              │              │               │
 ▼             ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼               ▼
[Interruption] [Natural Flow] [Acceptable]   [Hesitation]   ["Hello?"]     [Hang-Up]
```

Sociolinguistic research demonstrates that across all human languages, the average gap between spoken turns in natural dialogue is **200ms to 280ms**.

When a conversational gap drops below **50ms**, humans perceive it as an aggressive interruption. When the gap expands beyond **600ms**, the human brain interprets the pause as hesitation, confusion, or a dropped telephone line.

## 2. The "Hello?" Moment: Why Latency Kills Phone Conversations

In phone sales and customer support, latency is the single greatest determinant of call conversion.

```text
The Conversational Collision Anatomy:

0.00s: [Customer Finishes]: "Can you tell me about the pricing?"
0.20s: (Customer waits for response)
0.60s: (AI is still processing LLM tokens; Silence on the line)
0.80s: [Customer assumes call dropped]: "Hello? Are you there?"
0.85s: [AI starts speaking synthesized audio simultaneously]: "Our pricing starts at..."
0.90s: [CONVERSATIONAL COLLISION]: Both speak at once ──► [CALLER DROPS CALL]
```

When latency exceeds **700ms**, callers instinctively speak again to confirm the connection. This triggers conversational collisions where both the caller and AI talk over each other, creating an awkward, robotic experience.

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the ideal latency for a Voice AI phone agent?**
The target turnaround latency is **200ms to 300ms**, which matches natural human conversational pacing. Turnaround times above **600ms** trigger noticeable conversational lag.

**What is the difference between TTFT and TTFA?**

- **Time-to-First-Token (TTFT):** The time required for a language model to emit its first text word.
- **Time-to-First-Audio (TTFA):** The time required for a speech synthesizer to output its first playable audio packet.

**Why does Voice Activity Detection (VAD) add latency?**
VAD algorithms must wait for a brief period of silence (typically **150ms to 250ms**) before declaring that the user has stopped speaking, preventing the AI from interrupting during mid-sentence breaths.

**How does network location affect voice agent speed?**
Hosting models far from your callers adds **150ms to 250ms** of transatlantic network transit delay. Deploying on regional edge servers (such as Mumbai for Indian traffic) cuts network RTT to **&lt;35ms**.
