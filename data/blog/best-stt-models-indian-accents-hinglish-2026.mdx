## The Acoustic Reality of Indian Telephony Audio

Engineering teams transitioning from clean desktop microphones to real-world phone calls across India experience an immediate drop in speech recognition accuracy. Three acoustic challenges define the Indian telephony landscape:

1. **Intra-Sentence Code-Switching (Hinglish):** In commercial calls, users speak Hindi mixed with English business terms (e.g., _"Mera loan account number verify kardo, I need to check my balance."_). Standard Western models transcribe English words into Devanagari script phonetically or translate Hindi words arbitrarily, causing downstream LLM hallucinations.
2. **Bandwidth Compression (8,000 Hz):** Cellular phone calls in India stream over G.711u or AMR-NB codecs at 8kHz sample rates. High-frequency acoustic information above 3.8kHz is eliminated, making subtle dental and retroflex consonants (_Ta_ vs _Tha_, _Da_ vs _Dha_) difficult for models trained on 16kHz audio to distinguish.
3. **Severe Ambient Background Noise:** Urban Indian callers frequently speak from busy streets, trains, autos, or open offices with ceiling fan noise. Voice Activity Detection (VAD) models and STT endpointing algorithms require aggressive acoustic filtering to avoid hallucinating text during silent pauses.

According to research from the [IIT Delhi Speech and Audio Processing Lab (2025)](https://home.iitd.ac.in), standard non-adapted Whisper models exhibit a 47% hallucination rate on silent background noise common on Indian cellular carrier lines.

## Detailed Evaluation of the Top 10 Indian STT Engines

### 1. Sarvam AI (Saaras v2)

- **Primary Strength:** The undisputed benchmark for conversational Hinglish and 12 regional Indic languages.
- **Telephony Accuracy:** **9.2% WER** on real-world 8kHz carrier recordings.
- **Latency Profile:** **190ms Time-to-First-Transcript (TTFT)** via WebSocket streaming.
- **Pricing:** ₹28.00 per audio hour (~$0.34 USD).

### 2. Soniox (v4 Indic)

- **Primary Strength:** Contextual biasing and native Romanized code-switching tokenization.
- **Telephony Accuracy:** **9.8% WER**. Keeps English entity names (brand names, software terms, locations) properly formatted in Latin script while transcribing Hindi words cleanly.
- **Latency Profile:** **210ms TTFT**.
- **Pricing:** $0.36 per audio hour.

### 3. Gnani.ai (Prisma 8kHz)

- **Primary Strength:** Telecom-native acoustic models trained on millions of Indian cellular calls.
- **Telephony Accuracy:** **10.4% WER**. Highly resilient to low-bitrate carrier audio and background noise.
- **Latency Profile:** **220ms TTFT**.
- **Pricing:** ₹32.00 per audio hour (~$0.38 USD).

### 4. Deepgram (Nova-3)

## Technical Implementation: Streaming STT with Custom Word Boosting

To eliminate transcription errors on corporate brand names and technical jargon, implement contextual word boosting over persistent WebSockets:

import asyncio
import websockets
import json

async def stream_audio_to_stt(audio_generator):
    uri = "wss://api.deepgram.com/v1/listen?model=nova-3&language=en-IN&smart_format=true&endpointing=150"
    headers = {"Authorization": "Token YOUR_DEEPGRAM_API_KEY"}

    async with websockets.connect(uri, extra_headers=headers) as ws:
        # Send initial configuration with custom entity boosting
        config = {
            "keywords": ["Tough Tongue:3", "EMI:2", "UPI:2", "KYC:2", "Aadhaar:2"]
        }
        await ws.send(json.dumps(config))

        async def send_audio():
            async for audio_chunk in audio_generator:
                await ws.send(audio_chunk)

        async def receive_transcripts():
            async for message in ws:
                data = json.loads(message)
                if data.get("is_final"):
                    transcript = data["channel"]["alternatives"][0]["transcript"]
                    print(f"User Spoke: {transcript}")
                    # Dispatch directly to LLM stream

        await asyncio.gather(send_audio(), receive_transcripts())

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which Speech-to-Text model is best for conversational Hinglish?

**Sarvam Saaras v2** and **Soniox v4 Indic** are the two best models for conversational Hinglish. They transcribe intra-sentence code-switches cleanly into Romanized English script without translating or hallucinating Devanagari text.

### Why does OpenAI Whisper hallucinate during Indian phone calls?

OpenAI Whisper is an autoregressive sequence-to-sequence model. When it encounters continuous background static or silence typical of cellular PSTN lines, it attempts to predict the next word from token probability distributions rather than acoustic inputs, causing repeated hallucinations like _"Thank you for watching"_.

### What is the maximum acceptable latency for speech recognition in voice bots?

To keep total conversational latency under 500ms, the Speech-to-Text engine must deliver its final transcript within **150ms to 200ms** of the user finishing their utterance.

### Can Deepgram Nova-3 transcribe Hindi?

Yes, Deepgram Nova-3 supports Hindi transcription, but it achieves its best accuracy on Indian English and mixed urban speech. For pure regional Hindi or vernacular dialects, Sarvam Saaras v2 delivers lower Word Error Rates.
