## Which kind of LMS do you need?

Indian LMS buyers fall into four groups, and the right platform differs for each. Pick your group first; the rest of the page follows that split.

- **Corporate L&D (onboarding, compliance, upskilling):** you need HRMS integration, reports for managers and a per-learner price. Look at Learnyst Corporate, Calibr, Paradiso, iSpring, TalentLMS, Disprz and Adobe Learning Manager.
- **Sales teams:** you need product knowledge, certification and practice on real conversations. Look at Mindtickle, Disprz, 360Learning and Docebo, or keep your LMS and add a practice layer.
- **Coaching institutes and course creators:** you sell courses, so you need payments, a branded app, video protection and WhatsApp. Look at Classplus, Graphy, Edmingle and Learnyst.
- **Schools and colleges:** you need open standards and a low cost per student. MoodleCloud is the cheapest published option; government courses run on SWAYAM.

## GST on an LMS subscription, explained

**An LMS subscription is a software service taxed at 18% GST in India.** The September 2025 rate change, which moved most goods to two main slabs, did not change the rate on software services ([Xflow](https://www.xflowpay.com/blog/gst-on-software-services); [EY alert](https://www.ey.com/content/dam/ey-unified-site/ey-com/en-in/alerts-hub/2025/09/gst-council-announces-major-rate-rationalization-and-trade-facilitation-measures.pdf)). Who pays the 18% depends on where the vendor sits and who you are.

| You buy from                   | You are                         | Who pays the 18%                          | Can you get it back?                                             |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| An Indian vendor (INR invoice) | Any buyer                       | Vendor charges it on the invoice          | Yes, as input tax credit, if you are GST-registered and eligible |
| A foreign vendor (USD invoice) | GST-registered business         | **You**, under reverse charge, as IGST    | Yes, after paying it in cash                                     |
| A foreign vendor (USD invoice) | Individual or unregistered body | The foreign vendor, under the OIDAR rules | No                                                               |

The rules behind the table, from official sources:

- **Imports are taxed on you.** The GST Council's flyer on imports says ["the importer of services will have to pay tax on reverse charge basis"](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/e-version-gst-flyers/Imports_in_GST_Regime.pdf).
- **Credit comes after cash.** The CBIC reverse charge flyer says the liability ["cannot be discharged by using input tax credit"](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/e-version-gst-fliers/reverse-charge-mechanism-08aug2017.pdf), but credit "can be taken by the recipient, if he is otherwise eligible". So a dollar invoice costs you 18% extra in cash flow until your next return.
- **Foreign sellers to individuals register in India.** Under the OIDAR rules, the supplier outside India is liable for IGST and takes a single registration in Form GST REG-10 ([CBIC OIDAR flyer](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/pdf/e-version-gst-fliers/OIDAR_online%20version_online%20version_20_July_2017.pdf)).
- **The old equalisation levy is gone.** The 2% levy on e-commerce supplies does not apply to payments on or after 1 Aug 2024 ([Income Tax Department](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/equalisation-levy-1)).

Two practical checks: ask an Indian vendor for a GST invoice with your GSTIN on it, and ask a foreign vendor whether its price is in US dollars or another currency. MoodleCloud, for example, shows INR but bills in Australian dollars ([plans](https://www.moodlecloud.com/standard-plans/)), so your bank's forex fee applies.

## The DPDP Act and your LMS

**An LMS holds employees' or students' personal data, so the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies to it.** Your company decides why the data is used, which makes it the **data fiduciary**; the LMS vendor processes it on your behalf, which makes it a **data processor**. The Act lets a fiduciary use a processor ["only under a valid contract"](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf) (section 8(2)).

The dates, from the Gazette notification of the DPDP Rules, 2025 ([G.S.R. 846(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)):

| When                           | What applies                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 13 Nov 2025                    | Rules published; definitions and the Data Protection Board rules (Rules 1, 2 and 17–21) start        |
| November 2026 (one year later) | Consent Manager rules                                                                                |
| May 2027 (18 months later)     | Notice, consent, security safeguards, breach reporting, data principal rights, children's data rules |

In January 2026, MeitY was reported to be considering a shorter 12-month window. As of the latest trackers we read, that change had not been notified, so May 2027 stands ([Pyroniq tracker](https://www.pyroniq.ai/resources/dpdpa-compliance-timeline)). Check the eGazette before you plan against it.

What it means for each buyer:

- **Employers:** training records can usually be processed without fresh consent, because section 7(i) allows personal data to be used "for the purposes of employment". You still owe security safeguards and a processor contract. Our [DPDP Act guide for L&D teams](/blog/dpdp-act-for-l-and-d-teams-2026) maps which learner data needs consent and has a 12-point readiness check.
- **Coaching, schools and ed-tech:** anyone under 18 is a child under the Act. You need ["verifiable consent of the parent"](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf) and must not do "tracking or behavioural monitoring of children" (section 9). Check what your LMS's learner analytics actually track.
- **Penalties:** failing to take reasonable security safeguards can cost up to ₹250 crore; failing to report a breach or breaking the children's rules, up to ₹200 crore (Act Schedule).
- **Data localisation:** the Act does not require data to stay in India in general. It lets the government restrict transfers to countries it notifies (section 16). That is why hosting region is a preference for most buyers, and a requirement only where your sector regulator or customer contract says so.

## Questions to send every vendor

Paste these into your RFP. Tick each one off when you get a written answer.

- [ ] Do you sign a data processing agreement that names you as our data processor under the DPDP Act?
- [ ] In which region is our learner data stored, and can we choose India?
- [ ] Which sub-processors (cloud, video, AI model providers) see our data?
- [ ] How fast will you tell us about a personal data breach, in hours?
- [ ] Can we export all learner records and delete them when we leave?
- [ ] Is your price in INR or foreign currency, and does it include GST?
- [ ] Do you price per registered user or per monthly active user?
- [ ] Which AI features are included, and which are metered or add-ons?

## Where an LMS stops: practice

An LMS delivers courses and records completions. It does not make a sales rep better at handling "your price is too high", or a fresher better at a final-round interview. That takes speaking practice with feedback, repeated.

## FAQ

**Which is the best LMS in India?** For coaching and creators, Classplus, Graphy and Edmingle publish the clearest INR plans. For companies, Learnyst Corporate bills in INR from ₹49,999 a month, and Adobe Learning Manager is the only one we found that names an Indian data centre.

**How much does an LMS cost in India?** For 100 learners, published year-one list prices run from ₹23,988 (Graphy, plus a cut of each sale) or ₹24,560 (MoodleCloud) to ₹9,06,240 (360Learning), before 18% GST. Four of the 16 publish no price at all.

**Is GST charged on LMS software?** Yes, 18%. An Indian vendor charges it on the invoice. If a GST-registered company buys from a foreign vendor, it pays the 18% IGST itself under reverse charge and can claim it back as input tax credit.

**Does the DPDP Act require my LMS data to stay in India?** No, not in general. The Act allows transfers abroad unless the government restricts a country. You still need a processor contract and security safeguards, and the main duties apply from May 2027.

**Is there a free LMS in India?** Moodle is open source and free to self-host. TalentLMS has a free plan for 5 users and 10 courses. Government learners use SWAYAM and iGOT Karmayogi at no cost.
