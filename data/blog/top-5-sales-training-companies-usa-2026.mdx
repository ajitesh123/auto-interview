## #2: Challenger (Best for Insight-Driven Enterprise Selling)

**Website:** [challengerinc.com](https://challengerinc.com/)

[Challenger](https://challengerinc.com/) is one of the most influential sales methodologies in American enterprise selling. Originally developed through extensive research by CEB (now Gartner), Challenger teaches reps to challenge customer thinking, lead with insights and differentiate through commercial teaching rather than relationship-building alone.

### Strengths

- **Research-proven methodology.** Research shows that 54% of star sales performers are Challengers, making it the most common profile among top performers in complex B2B selling.
- **Massive revenue impact.** Clients have reported \$1.1B+ in attributed revenue impact since 2022, including an SAP Brandon Hall Gold Medal for global sales training.
- **Ideal for complex enterprise sales.** Designed specifically for high-consideration B2B sales where reps need to shift buyer perspective and create urgency.
- **Comprehensive ecosystem.** Combines live workshops, virtual training, digital content and coaching into a sustained development program.

### Limitations

- **Consulting-heavy implementation.** Deploying Challenger across an organization requires significant consulting investment in assessment, customization and change management.
- **Limited AI practice.** No AI-native roleplay platform for continuous, on-demand practice between coaching sessions.
- **Enterprise pricing.** Positioned for Fortune 500 and large enterprise budgets. Mid-market and growth companies may find entry costs challenging.
- **Not ideal for transactional selling.** Challenger is designed for complex, insight-driven selling. Companies with simpler sales motions may not see the same impact.

### Best For

Large American enterprise organizations selling complex B2B solutions where reps need to differentiate through commercial insight, challenge buyer assumptions and drive consensus among multiple stakeholders.

## #3: Sandler Training (Best for Process-Driven Sales Systems in the USA)

**Website:** [sandler.com](https://www.sandler.com/)

[Sandler Training](https://www.sandler.com/) has trained over 50,000 businesses across the United States with their seven-step Sandler Selling System. Their approach emphasizes discovery, qualification and genuine relationship-building without pressure tactics. The ongoing reinforcement model addresses knowledge decay better than most traditional providers.

### Strengths

- **Continuous reinforcement.** Sandler's model includes ongoing training sessions rather than one-time workshops, making it one of the few traditional providers that actively addresses the forgetting curve.
- **Common selling language.** The seven-step system creates a universal framework across sales teams, improving collaboration and coaching quality.
- **Massive US network.** Largest franchise network of sales trainers in the country, with local trainers in every major market.
- **Psychology-based approach.** Combines behavioral science with practical selling techniques to change how reps think about buyer interactions.
- **96% client recommendation rate.** Exceptional client satisfaction demonstrates consistent quality and outcomes.

### Limitations

- **Franchise quality varies.** Training experience depends on the individual Sandler franchise and trainer.
- **No AI practice platform.** No technology-powered tools for on-demand, AI-driven practice between sessions.
- **Mid-market sweet spot.** Strongest for SME and mid-market companies. Largest enterprises or very early-stage startups may need different solutions.

### Best For

American SMEs and mid-market companies that want a structured, repeatable sales system with ongoing reinforcement, strong accountability and a consultative approach to buyer engagement.
