## What is a sales training platform?

A sales training platform is software or a programme that teaches sellers what to say and lets them practise it, then shows a manager who has learned what. The phrase covers four products that do different jobs, so the first decision is which job you are buying for.

## What should a platform have in 2026?

Five features decide whether a platform is used after the first month. Check each on a demo with your own content, not the vendor's.

1. **Practice out loud, scored.** Reading and quizzes do not prepare a rep for a buyer who interrupts. Look for spoken roleplay with a score against criteria you set.
2. **Your scenarios and your rubric.** Generic buyers teach generic answers. You should be able to write the buyer, the objections and what a good answer contains.
3. **A manager view.** Who practised, what they scored, where the team keeps losing. Without it, a manager cannot see who practised.
4. **Real-call scoring, if you want one score.** Some tools score live calls on the same rubric as practice (Gong, Allego, Mindtickle, Hyperbound, Outdoo per their pages).
5. **Short time to first use.** A free start or a published plan lets one manager test in an afternoon. A three-year contract does not.

## Does sales training work?

Survey data points to a practice gap. Salesforce's 2026 survey of 4,050 sellers found 47% lack roleplay opportunities and 46% "rarely get feedback on their sales conversations" ([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/state-of-sales-report-announcement-2026/)). The largest controlled evidence we could open on simulated practice is from health professions, not sales; we summarise it in our [AI sales roleplay guide](/blog/best-sales-roleplay-software-platforms-2026#does-ai-roleplay-practice-actually-work).

Two claims you will see on vendor pages and lists: that training is forgotten within weeks, and that a platform "cuts ramp time by X%". We could not trace either to a study with a method, so we do not repeat them.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best sales training platform?

There is no single best one, because the four kinds do different jobs. For knowledge, HubSpot Academy is free. For one selling method, Winning by Design publishes prices and Sandler and RAIN Group quote. For running onboarding at scale, Mindtickle, Highspot and Allego are suites we read, and TalentLMS publishes prices. For spoken practice, Hyperbound, Exec, Outdoo and Tough Tongue AI (our product) let you start free.

### What is the difference between a sales enablement platform and an LMS?

An LMS hosts courses and tracks who completed them. An enablement suite adds sales content, coaching and, now, AI roleplay and call scoring. Of the 12 we read, TalentLMS and Docebo describe themselves as LMS; Mindtickle, Highspot, Seismic, Allego and Showpad sell themselves as enablement platforms.

### How much does sales training cost per rep?

At published list prices, \$0 for free course libraries, about \$380–\$400 a seat a year for LinkedIn Learning or Coursera, \$1,500–\$2,500 for one live Winning by Design course, and \$240 a year per seat for Exec Starter (5-seat minimum). Suites and methodology firms quote, so ask for a per-seat, per-year number at your team size.

### Are there free sales training platforms?

Yes. HubSpot Academy and Salesforce Trailhead are free, TalentLMS has a free plan for up to 5 users, Winning by Design has a free one-hour SPICED course, and Hyperbound, Exec, Outdoo and Tough Tongue AI (our product) let you start practising free.

### Can AI replace a sales trainer?

No. AI practice tools give reps more repetitions and consistent scores, but someone still has to decide what good looks like and coach the reps who keep losing the same moment. Use AI for the repetitions and keep the manager for the judgement.
