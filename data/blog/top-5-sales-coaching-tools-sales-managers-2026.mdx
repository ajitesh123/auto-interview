## What is AI sales coaching?

**AI sales coaching is software that uses call recordings, transcripts and AI scoring to show a sales manager what to coach, and in some tools coaches the rep directly.** Before these tools, a manager could only coach the few calls they listened to. Now a tool can score every recorded call against one scorecard and surface the weak moments.

The tools fall into three jobs. Pick by the problem you have, not by the feature list.

<AiCoachingJobs />

An AI coach does not replace the manager. It decides which calls deserve a human's time, and it gives the rep repetitions the manager cannot. The 1:1 conversation about what to change still matters most.

## How much time should a sales manager spend coaching?

**Start with one reviewed call per rep a week, as Gong recommends, and expect that to cost about 40 minutes per rep once you include feedback.** The maths is simple: reps × calls reviewed × (call length + feedback time). At 30-minute calls and 10 minutes of feedback, 8 reps take 5.3 hours a week and 20 reps take 13.3 hours.

<AiCoachingTimeChart />

That is why automatic scoring matters. Gartner found that "58% of sale managers report they struggle to accomplish all the tasks they have been given in the time allotted" ([Gartner, 29 Jul 2020](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2020-07-29-gartner-says-high-performing-sales-managers-prioritiz)). A manager who reviews one call per rep still sees only 4% of a 25-call week. Scoring every call does not add coaching time; it makes sure the one call you pick is the one worth coaching.

## Which kind of AI coaching tool do you need?

**Buy for the problem you can name: blind spots on calls need call review, forgotten talk tracks need live guidance, and new reps need practice.** Here is how we would decide.

- **If you are a founder or first sales manager with 2 to 10 reps:** start with call review and one scorecard. A published-price tool like Avoma lets you start without a sales cycle; for 5 reps it is \$2,880 a year at list price on the Startup plan with the coaching add-on.
- **If you already pay for Salesloft or Outreach:** ask what their conversation features add before buying a separate tool; both state live guidance on their own pages.
- **If you run an inside-sales or support floor with 50+ agents:** look at the contact-centre tools (Balto, Cresta, Observe.AI, and Convin in India). They are built for live checklists and scoring every interaction.
- **If you sell in India in Hindi or Hinglish:** Convin's page says you can speak to its AI agent "in English, Hindi, Hinglish". Ask any vendor for a test on your own recorded calls before you sign.
- **If your problem is ramp time for new reps:** call review only shows you their mistakes after a real buyer heard them. Add AI roleplay so they practise the first 20 calls on an AI buyer. Our [roleplay scenario guide](/blog/12-realistic-sales-role-play-scenarios-sdr-mastery-2026) has 14 scenarios and a scoring rubric.
- **If your team is in the EU:** do not switch on any feature that reads your reps' emotions from their voice (see below).

## A weekly coaching routine that works with any tool

**The routine is: score every call, pick one call per rep, run a 15-minute 1:1 on one skill, practise the fix, and re-score next week.** Coaching fails when it covers ten things at once.

<AiCoachingLoop />

Keep one scorecard for the whole team, with 4 to 6 lines a manager can judge in seconds (for example: agenda set, two discovery questions on impact, next step agreed with a date). Change one line per month at most, so this month's scores compare with last month's. Our [sales roleplay scoring rubric and scenarios](/blog/12-realistic-sales-role-play-scenarios-sdr-mastery-2026) has a ready-made rubric you can copy.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AI sales coaching software?

There is no single best one; it depends on the job. For reviewing recorded calls with a published price, Avoma is the easiest to start with. Gong is a widely used call-review tool but publishes no price. For live guidance on a large calling floor, Balto, Cresta and Observe.AI are built for it. For practice before calls, use an AI roleplay tool.

### How much does Gong cost?

Gong does not publish a price. Its pricing page says licences are priced per user, with a platform fee based on the number of users, and asks you to request a proposal. Ask for the per-seat price, the platform fee and the minimum contract in writing.

### Can AI replace a sales manager for coaching?

No. The tools score calls, pick moments and give reps practice, but deciding what one rep should change next and holding them to it is still the manager's job. What AI changes is how many calls the manager can see without listening to them.

### How many calls should a sales manager review each week?

Gong's own guidance is to start with one call per direct report each week and review its coaching inbox weekly. With automatic scoring, pick the lowest-scoring call on the skill you are coaching rather than a random one.

### Is sentiment analysis of sales calls legal in the EU?

Inferring your own employees' emotions at work from biometric data such as their voice has been prohibited since 2 Feb 2025, except for medical or safety reasons. Analysing your customers' emotions is not banned, but it is still regulated and people must be told. Emotion read from text only is not biometric and falls outside the ban. Check GDPR and works-council rules either way.

### Is there a free AI sales coaching tool?

Partly. Sybill has a free plan ("Start building deal context in Sybill, totally free"), Avoma makes viewers free and gives a 14-day trial of its Organization plan, and Jiminny offers a 14-day trial. For practice, Tough Tongue AI (our product) gives 25 free minutes. Among the call-review tools, Sybill is the only one with a free plan on the pages we read.

### Can I record my team's sales calls?

Usually yes, if you tell people. In the US, check whether any caller is in an all-party consent state such as California, Florida or Pennsylvania. In the UK, treat it as worker monitoring under ICO guidance; in the EU, do not analyse your reps' emotions from their voice. See the rules table above.

### What is the difference between conversation intelligence and an AI sales coach?

Conversation intelligence records, transcribes and analyses calls, mainly for managers and deal reviews. An AI sales coach is the coaching layer on top: scorecards, feedback to the rep, live prompts or practice. Many tools now sell both under one name.
