## #2: Gong (Best for Real Conversation Intelligence and Call Coaching)

**Website:** [gong.io](https://www.gong.io/)

[Gong](https://www.gong.io/) is the market leader in conversation intelligence. It records, transcribes and analyzes your team's real sales calls using AI to surface coaching insights, deal risks and win/loss patterns. For managers who want to coach based on what is actually happening in live customer conversations, Gong is an extremely powerful tool.

### Strengths

- **Best-in-class call analysis.** Gong's AI analyzes talk-to-listen ratios, question frequency, topic coverage, competitor mentions, pricing discussion patterns and dozens of other signals across every recorded call. Managers get automated coaching suggestions based on real data.
- **Deal intelligence.** Beyond individual calls, Gong analyzes deal progression across the pipeline. It identifies deals at risk, flags conversations where key topics were not covered and predicts deal outcomes based on conversation patterns.
- **Proven at scale.** Used by thousands of companies including [LinkedIn, Hubspot, Paychex and dozens of Fortune 500 organizations](https://www.gong.io/customers/). One of the most established revenue technology companies in the market.
- **Coaching playlists.** Managers create curated playlists of exemplary call segments to share with reps. "Here is how Sarah handles the pricing objection" becomes a shareable, repeatable coaching asset.

### Limitations

- **Expensive.** Gong typically costs $100 to $200/user/month depending on the package and team size. For a 20-person team, that is $24,000 to $48,000/year. This makes it prohibitive for startups and SMBs.
- **Reactive, not proactive coaching.** Gong analyzes calls after they happen. It shows you what went wrong but does not give reps a place to practice and fix those issues before the next live call. You need a separate practice platform like Tough Tongue AI to close the loop.
- **No AI roleplay.** Gong identifies coaching needs but does not provide AI-powered practice. Reps cannot practice objection handling, cold call openers or discovery frameworks within Gong.
- **Complex implementation.** Full deployment with call recording, CRM integration and analytics configuration typically takes 4 to 8 weeks.

### Best For

Mid-market to enterprise sales teams (50+ reps) with budget for premium conversation intelligence that want to base coaching on real call data. Best used alongside a practice platform like Tough Tongue AI for a complete coaching ecosystem.

## 5 Coaching Frameworks Every Sales Manager Should Build in Tough Tongue AI

### 1. The Weekly Objection Drill

Build a scenario with your team's top 5 objections. Assign every rep to practice it weekly. Review scores on Friday. Coach the bottom 3 performers in your Monday one-on-one.

**Time investment:** 15 minutes to build, 0 minutes to run (AI handles it)

### 2. The New Hire Ramp Accelerator

Create a 4-week training sequence: Week 1 (elevator pitch), Week 2 (discovery questions), Week 3 (objection handling), Week 4 (meeting booking). New hires complete daily practice in each week's scenario.

**Result:** Typical ramp time drops from 6 months to [6 weeks](/blog/how-to-onboard-train-sales-reps-faster-ai-2026)

### 3. The Competitive Battlecard Drill

Build a scenario where the AI prospect mentions each of your top 3 competitors. Reps must differentiate without bashing the competitor and redirect to your unique value proposition.

**Result:** Reps handle competitive objections 3x more confidently on live calls

### 4. The Product Launch Readiness Test

Before every product launch or feature release, build a scenario that covers the new feature positioning, use cases, objection handling and competitive impact. Every rep must practice and score above threshold before the launch date.

**Result:** Launch readiness goes from "hope they read the email" to "proven they can sell it"

### 5. The Deal-Specific Coaching Scenario

When a specific rep has a critical upcoming call (VP-level meeting, pricing negotiation, renewal discussion), build a custom scenario that simulates that specific conversation. The rep practices 5 to 10 times before the live call.

**Result:** Win rates on critical opportunities increase significantly when reps walk in mentally rehearsed
