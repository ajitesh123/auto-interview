## Shadowing, workshops or practice calls: what each onboarding model does

|                            | Call shadowing                                 | Trainer workshop                                 | Scored AI practice calls                             |
| :------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------- |
| **What the new hire does** | Listens to senior reps                         | Learns a method in a group, a few roleplays each | Speaks on many practice calls alone                  |
| **Feedback**               | Informal, when someone has time                | From the trainer, on the day                     | A score against your rubric after every call         |
| **Who can see progress**   | Nobody, unless the manager listens             | The trainer, on the day                          | The manager, for every session                       |
| **Risk to real leads**     | High: first live calls are the practice        | Medium                                           | Lower: live calls start after a passed certification |
| **Cost**                   | Senior reps' and managers' time                | A day rate per workshop                          | A software subscription plus manager review time     |
| **Best used for**          | Hearing real buyers and the product in context | Teaching a new sales method                      | Repetition until the talk track is automatic         |

Most teams use all three: a workshop for the method, a few shadowed calls for context, and practice calls for the repetitions.

## How do you measure ramp time on your own team?

Measure it before you change anything, so you can tell whether the new plan helped. Pick one definition and keep it:

- **Ramp time:** months from start date to the first month at full quota (or at the activity target, for SDRs).
- **Time to first meeting booked** and **time to first live call** after the start date.
- **Practice minutes per rep per week** in weeks 1–3, from the roleplay tool's report.
- **Certification pass rate** at the week-three gate, and how many attempts it took.

Compare the next cohort with the last two. A cohort of 3–5 hires is small; read the result as a direction, not a percentage.

## Why practice calls instead of shadowing?

Consider how commercial aviation trains pilots. No airline lets a new pilot shadow an experienced captain for three months and then hands them the controls of a Boeing 777.

Airlines train pilots in flight simulators, practising engine failures and emergency landings until the response is automatic. Sales practice calls apply the same idea to the cold-call opener and the price objection.

## The 30-day sales onboarding plan

Here is the plan, day by day. Adjust the call counts to your product and the time your managers have to review sessions.

### Week 1 (Days 1–7): Value Proposition & Cold Call Reflexes

- **Morning:** 60 minutes studying customer pain points, competitor differentiation, and the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
- **Afternoon (AI Flight Simulation):** Complete **15 simulated cold calls per day** on Tough Tongue AI.
- **Core Scenarios Drilled:**
  - The 15-Second Pattern Interrupt Opener.
  - Handling the Gatekeeper Brush-Off (_"Send an email to info@"_).
  - Deflecting the Immediate Reflex Objection (_"Not interested, we're busy"_).
- **Week 1 Milestone:** Rep completes 50 AI calls and achieves an 80%+ score on cold call control.

---

### Week 2 (Days 8–14): Consultative Discovery & Qualification

- **Focus:** Moving from a "pitcher" to a "diagnostician" using MEDDPICC or BANT frameworks.
- **Afternoon (AI Flight Simulation):** Complete **10 full 15-minute discovery simulations per day**.
- **Core Scenarios Drilled:**
  - Uncovering the Economic Buyer and Decision Criteria.
  - Quantifying the Cost of Inaction (COI) when prospects hesitate.
  - Handling the Incumbent Competitor (_"We already use Vendor X"_).
- **Week 2 Milestone:** Rep scores 85%+ on discovery question depth and active listening rubrics.

---

### Week 3 (Days 15–21): High-Stakes Adversarial Sparring & Certification

- **Focus:** Emotional resilience, price defense, and difficult enterprise scenarios.
- **Afternoon (AI Flight Simulation):** Complete **10 adversarial stress-test calls per day**.
- **Core Scenarios Drilled:**
  - Aggressive C-Suite Interruption (_"Why did you call me? What do you want?"_).
  - The 30% Price Discount Trap.
  - The Indefinite Delay (_"Call me back in six months"_).
- **Week 3 Milestone: THE PHONE LICENSE GATE.** The new hire must pass three randomized AI roleplays with an 85%+ aggregate score. If they fail, they remediate in the AI simulator until they pass.

---

### Week 4 (Days 22–30): Live Pipeline Activation with Daily AI Warm-Ups

- **Focus:** Transitioning to live prospect dials.
- **Daily Routine:**
  - **9:00 AM – 9:15 AM:** Complete **two 5-minute AI roleplays on Tough Tongue AI** to warm up vocal cords and tune conversational cadence.
  - **9:15 AM – 12:00 PM:** Active live outbound cold calling blitz.
  - **4:30 PM – 5:00 PM:** Review AI scorecard metrics and address friction points.
- **Week 4 Milestone:** Rep books their first 3 to 5 qualified enterprise discovery meetings within 30 days of hiring.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long should SDR onboarding take?

Set it by your sales cycle and product, then measure it. The plan above puts new hires on live calls after a passed certification in week three and keeps daily practice afterwards; track the months to full quota for each cohort to see what your real number is.

### Can sales managers see transcripts and scores of new hire roleplays?

In most AI roleplay tools, yes: managers see each session, its transcript and its score. Check this on any tool you pilot; it is what lets a manager coach without sitting in on every practice call.

### Is AI roleplay better than call shadowing?

They do different jobs. Shadowing shows a new hire real buyers; practice calls make them speak and get feedback many times. Use a few shadowed calls for context and practice calls for repetition.
