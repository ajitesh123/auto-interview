_Rewritten 21 Nov 2026. The March version compared AI calling with a human SDR team using salary, cost-per-lead and "40 to 60% cheaper" figures we could not trace to a source, and quoted no vendor prices. Those claims are gone. Every price below was read on the vendor's own pricing page on 21 Nov 2026. This page also replaces our two older guides to voice AI cost per minute._

**An AI phone call costs about \$0.06 to \$0.29 a minute in the US once you add up everything the call uses: the platform, the language model, the voice and the phone line. The middle of that range is about \$0.13. In India, Sarvam and Bolna sell all-in or nearly all-in minutes for ₹3.90 to ₹5.52 before GST. The advertised headline price (often \$0.05 or \$0.07) usually covers only the platform, so read what the rate includes before you compare.**

<KeyFacts>

- **Headline prices leave things out.** Vapi's \$0.05 a minute is hosting only; the model, voice, transcription and phone line are billed on top at cost ([Vapi pricing](https://vapi.ai/pricing)). Retell's own page says "\$0.07–\$0.31 / min" for the full range ([Retell pricing](https://www.retellai.com/pricing)).
- **Bundled rates are easier to budget.** Bland AI charges \$0.14 a minute on its free Start plan and says the rate covers the model, transcription and voice with "no token charges"; the phone line is extra ([Bland pricing](https://www.bland.ai/pricing)).
- **India is priced in rupees.** Sarvam lists ₹3.50 a minute, "excluding GST", plus ₹0.20 per 30 seconds on a Sarvam number ([Sarvam pricing](https://www.sarvam.ai/products/voice-agents/pricing)). Bolna lists "From ₹5.52 per minute (6¢)" ([Bolna pricing](https://www.bolna.ai/pricing)).
- **Rounding and silence cost money.** Telnyx and Sarvam bill in 60-second steps; Deepgram bills on WebSocket connection time; Air.ai says it is moving to billing dial time as well as talk time on outbound calls.
- **One of 16 platforms publishes no price at all** (Synthflow: annual agreement, "no rate by the minute").

</KeyFacts>

## How much does an AI call cost per minute?

**On the platforms we could price fully, a typical setup costs \$0.06 to \$0.29 a minute, and half of the US platforms have a midpoint between \$0.11 and \$0.16.** Each band below runs from a cheap setup to a typical one, rebuilt from the line items on the vendor's own pricing page. Where the vendor bills the phone line separately and sells no line of its own, we added the same US outbound rate (\$0.014 a minute); Retell, Telnyx and Sarvam use their own listed line rates, and Aircall and Bolna include the line.

<AiCallPriceRanges />

Two things move a platform along its band. The **language model** is the biggest swing: on Retell, GPT 5 nano adds \$0.0016 a minute and GPT 4.1 adds \$0.045. The **voice** is next: Retell's own voices cost \$0.015 a minute and ElevenLabs Flash \$0.040.

Real bills run higher than list prices when prompts are long. Retell says prompts averaging over 4,000 tokens raise billed time, and bills calls under 10 seconds with a dynamic greeting as 10 seconds ([Retell pricing](https://www.retellai.com/pricing)). One agency builder on Retell's community forum reported an average of "\$0.17 US per minute" across his clients in 2026, Retell charges only, before telephony ([Retell community, 16 Jun 2026](https://community.retellai.com/t/average-cost-per-minute-for-ai-voice-agents-built-on-retell/3118/2)).

## Where one minute goes

**On Retell's typical setup, the platform fee is about a third of the minute; the language model, the voice and the phone line make up the rest.** Retell prints every part of the minute as its own line, so it is the clearest example.

<AiCallMinuteBreakdown />

The same parts exist on every platform, even when they are bundled:

1. **Platform or orchestration**: the software that runs the call, listens for turns and connects the parts (Vapi \$0.05, Retell \$0.055, Telnyx \$0.05).
2. **Speech-to-text**: about \$0.01 a minute on Vapi's page for Deepgram.
3. **Language model**: from under \$0.01 to about \$0.06 a minute, depending on the model and the length of your prompt.
4. **Voice (text-to-speech)**: \$0.015 to \$0.04 a minute on Retell's list.
5. **Phone line**: \$0.014 a minute for a Twilio US outbound call; no Retell charge if you bring your own SIP trunk (your carrier still bills you).

Speech-to-speech models fold steps 2 to 4 into one. Google lists Gemini 3.1 Flash Live (preview) at \$0.005 a minute of audio in and \$0.018 out ([Gemini API pricing](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing)). OpenAI prices gpt-realtime-2.1 by audio tokens (\$32 in and \$64 out per million) ([OpenAI pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing)), and Retell resells it at \$0.38 a minute. Raw models also need hosting, a phone line and engineering, which are not in those numbers.

## AI calling cost in India

**In India, an AI call minute costs about ₹3.90 to ₹5.52 before GST on the two rupee-priced platforms, and GST at 18% takes the top of that to about ₹6.51.** Sarvam's ₹3.50 covers "the models that run your agent"; a Sarvam number adds ₹0.20 per 30-second pulse and ₹59 per 30 days. Its higher plans list ₹3.25, ₹3.15 and ₹3.00 a minute, and Bolna sells a one-time Pilot pack at ₹3.83 to ₹4.25.

- **Dollar-priced platforms cost more in India once you add an Indian phone line.** On Retell's forum, users estimated India calls at about \$0.15 a minute through Twilio or \$0.25 through Telnyx, on top of the AI minute ([Retell community, Jul 2026](https://community.retellai.com/t/pricing-for-india/3428)). Those are user estimates, not Retell's price.
- **Enterprise rupee rates go much lower.** Bolna lists enterprise "as low as ₹1.5" a minute, and Inc42 reported Bolna's enterprise pricing at about ₹1.75 to ₹2 a minute against about ₹5.5 self-serve ([Inc42, 15 Jul 2026](https://inc42.com/features/the-unit-economics-of-indias-voice-ai-boom/)).
- **Promotional calls need the right numbers.** TRAI's rules route promotional calls through 140-series numbers; see our [AI calling in India: DPDP, TRAI and DLT guide](/blog/ai-calling-india-dpdp-trai-dlt-compliance-complete-guide-2026) before you budget for outbound.

## Costs the per-minute price leaves out

**The minute price is the smallest decision; these are the lines that surprise people on the first invoice.**

| What          | Example from a pricing page                                                           |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Phone numbers | Retell \$2 a month; Smallest.ai \$10 a month; Sarvam ₹59 per 30 days                  |
| Concurrency   | Retell: 20 free, then \$8 a month each; Vapi: 4 free, extra lines \$10 a month        |
| Plan fees     | Bland Build \$299 a month; Vapi Core \$29 a month; Aircall \$99 a month minimum       |
| Rounding      | Telnyx and Sarvam bill whole minutes; Retell bills short greeting calls as 10 seconds |
| Silence       | Deepgram bills WebSocket connection time, so hold music and pauses count              |
| Transfers     | Bland charges \$0.04 to \$0.05 a minute for transferred time                          |
| Add-ons       | Retell: knowledge base and denoise \$0.005 a minute each, PII removal \$0.01          |

Then there is the work: writing the prompt, connecting your CRM and calendar, testing, and listening to calls every week. On self-serve platforms that is your team's time; on quote-only platforms it is usually inside the contract.

## What to do if you are...

- **Trying AI calling for the first time (under 500 minutes a month):** use the free credits above. Test the same script on a bundled platform (Bland, Bolna, Sarvam) and a build-your-own one (Retell, Vapi); at this volume the difference is tens of dollars a month.
- **A US sales team calling leads:** budget about \$0.13 to \$0.16 a minute for a typical setup, and check consent first. In the US an AI voice is an "artificial voice" under the TCPA ([FCC 24-17](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf)); our [AI calling laws guide](/blog/ai-calling-laws-regulations-compliance-guide-2026) covers the rules.
- **A business in India:** start with a rupee-priced platform and add 18% GST to your budget (GST-registered buyers also pay 18% under reverse charge on dollar services). Dollar platforms work, but Retell users on its forum estimated the Indian phone line alone at \$0.15 to \$0.25 a minute.
- **A developer building your own stack:** price the model and voice first; they are the lines that move. Gemini Live is the cheapest raw model minute on this list and Telnyx the cheapest hosted engine, but a raw model costs you engineering time.
- **Running 50,000+ minutes a month (collections, reminders, support):** ask for enterprise rates. Aircall, Bolna, Smallest.ai, Deepgram (Growth, \$0.068) and Bland (Build, \$0.12 with a \$299 plan) publish lower volume prices; Telnyx's minimum-spend tiers discount telephony.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does an AI voice agent cost per minute?

About \$0.06 to \$0.29 a minute all-in in the US on the platforms we priced, with the middle at about \$0.13. Headline prices of \$0.05 or \$0.07 usually cover only the platform; the model, voice and phone line are extra.

### How much does AI calling cost in India?

Sarvam lists ₹3.50 a minute plus ₹0.40 a minute for its telephony (₹3.90), and Bolna ₹5.52 (₹4.60 on larger top-ups), all before 18% GST. Enterprise rates can be much lower; Bolna lists "as low as ₹1.5".

### What is the cheapest AI calling platform?

On list prices, Telnyx's AI Assistants (about \$0.063 a minute with its own model and SIP) and Ultravox (\$0.05 plus SIP and a carrier) are the cheapest full setups we could build, and Sarvam is the cheapest in India. Gemini Live is cheaper as a raw model, but you run everything around it yourself.

### Retell vs Vapi vs Bland: which costs less?

At list price, Retell runs about \$0.09 to \$0.16 a minute, Vapi about \$0.10 to \$0.14 and Bland \$0.14 plus the phone line. Retell and Vapi move with the model and voice you pick; Bland is one rate.

### Do unanswered calls cost money?

Usually only connected time is billed, but read the page. Sarvam says it does not charge unanswered, busy or failed calls; Air.ai says it is moving to billing dial time on outbound calls; Telnyx and Sarvam round each call up to a full minute; and Retell bills short calls with a dynamic greeting as 10 seconds.

### Is AI calling cheaper than a human caller?

Per minute of talk, list prices here run from about \$0.06 to \$0.38 (Retell's speech-to-speech model), so compare that with what a minute of your callers' time costs you. Whether it is cheaper per meeting or per sale depends on how many calls connect and convert, so compare on cost per outcome with your own numbers in the [ROI calculator](/ai-calling-roi-calculator).

### Is there a free AI calling tool?

Not for real volume, but most platforms give free credit: Deepgram \$200, Retell \$10, Smallest.ai \$10, Vapi \$5, Bolna \$5, Ultravox 30 minutes, ElevenLabs 15 minutes a month and Tough Tongue AI 25 minutes.
