## The $20 Billion Illusion: Why Corporate Sales Training Fails

Global corporations spend an estimated **$20 billion each year on sales training programs, workshops, and coaching retainers**.

Yet, when Gartner, McKinsey, and Harvard Business Review audit post-training performance, the findings are consistent:

- **87% of all sales training is forgotten within 30 days** (The Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve).
- **Less than 15% of sales reps** change their live conversation behaviors after a seminar.
- **Quota attainment remains stubbornly stagnant**, with over 50% of B2B sales reps missing their annual targets.

```
THE EBBINGHAUS FORGETTING CURVE IN SALES TRAINING:
Hour 0 (Seminar Ends):     100% Information Retention (High Motivation)
Hour 24 (Next Morning):    54% Information Retention
Hour 48 (First Dialing):   33% Information Retention (Severe Knowledge Decay)
Day 30 (End of Month):     13% Information Retention (Complete Regression to Old Habits)
```

The fundamental flaw is pedagogical: **sales is a motor and cognitive reflex, not a lecture**. You cannot learn to swim by watching a PowerPoint presentation, and you cannot learn to defeat an aggressive procurement officer by listening to an external trainer tell war stories.
