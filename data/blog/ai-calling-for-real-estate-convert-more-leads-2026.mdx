The earlier version said agents using AI calling get "3x more viewings", quoted a "78% of leads go to the first agent" figure we could not trace to NAR, and said a property inquiry falls under a business-relationship exemption for AI calls. That exemption does not exist for artificial-voice marketing calls. Those claims are gone; every rule below is quoted from the regulator or statute, opened on 20 Nov 2026. This page also replaces our separate "10 use cases" post._

**AI calling for real estate means an AI voice that answers or places calls for an agent: picking up after-hours inquiries, calling back web leads in minutes, confirming showings and booking site visits. In the US, an AI voice counts as an "artificial voice" under the TCPA ([FCC, Feb 2024](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf)), so an AI may call a lead to sell only with written consent that covers those calls. Cold calling expireds, FSBOs or a bought list with an AI voice breaks that rule on every call. Inbound calls, booked-showing confirmations and consented inquiries are where AI calling is safe and pays off.**

<KeyFacts>

- **AI voices are robocalls under US law.** The FCC ruled the TCPA's "artificial or prerecorded voice" rules "encompass current AI technologies that generate human voices" ([FCC 24-17](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf)). Marketing calls with an AI voice to mobiles need prior express written consent ([47 CFR 64.1200(a)(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200)).
- **Past clients are not an exception.** The residential-line rule for artificial-voice marketing calls has no business-relationship exception in its current text ([64.1200(a)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200)).
- **Speed still wins.** Calling a web lead at 30 minutes instead of 5 cut the odds of reaching them 100 times and of qualifying them 21 times ([InsideSales.com with MIT, 2007](https://www.mortech.com/hs-fs/hub/25649/file-13535879-pdf/docs/mit_study.pdf)).
- **Minutes are cheap; consent is the hard part.** 200 leads at 4 billed minutes each is 800 minutes: \$56–\$248 a month at Retell's published rate, or ₹4,416 at Bolna's ([prices below](#what-it-costs)).
- **Outside the US:** UAE marketing calls only 9 a.m.–6 p.m., recorded and announced ([Cabinet Resolution 56 of 2024](https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2519/download)); India promotional calls only from 140-series numbers ([TRAI](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf)); UK AI marketing calls need consent, because the ICO treats an AI voice as "recorded material" ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/advice-and-services/innovation-advice/previously-asked-questions/)).

</KeyFacts>

## Which real estate calls to automate first

**Start with the calls nobody has to give consent for: calls people make to you, then confirmations of showings they booked.** Next come inquiry callbacks, but only where your form carries consent wording. Cold lists stay with people.

<RealEstateCallLadder />

- **Inbound and after-hours calls.** The US robocall rules govern calls you start ([47 CFR 64.1200](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200)). An AI that answers your listing line at 10 p.m., asks two questions and books a showing is the safest and most useful place to start.
- **Showing confirmations and reminders.** A call that sells nothing is not telemarketing. In the US an AI voice to a mobile still needs the person's prior consent, so keep a record of how they gave you the number, and keep offers off the call.
- **Inquiry and valuation callbacks.** This is where the speed research bites: minutes matter. In the US and UK, an AI may make this call only with written consent that covers it. Without consent, a person calls back fast.
- **Open-house and past-client calls.** Same rule as inquiries. A sign-in sheet or a closing three years ago is not consent to AI calls.
- **Cold calls to expireds, FSBOs and bought lists.** A person dials, after scrubbing the National Do Not Call list (at most 31 days old) and your own list ([16 CFR 310.4](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-310/section-310.4)). NAR keeps its own [telemarketing and cold-calling resources](https://www.nar.realtor/telemarketing-cold-calling) for agents.

To make future leads eligible for AI calls, add consent wording to every form and sign-in: it should name your brokerage, say the person agrees to calls and texts that may use automated technology or an AI-generated voice, and say agreeing is not a condition of buying. Keep the wording, the date and the page with each lead. Have your broker's counsel approve the text.

## The rules, country by country

**Every country here lets an AI answer calls; they differ on who you may call, when, and from which number.** The US and UK hinge on consent, India on registered numbers, the UAE on hours and recording, and Australia on its Do Not Call Register.

<RealEstateRulesTable />

Two more US points matter in 2026. The FCC's "one-to-one consent" rule, which would have required consent to name a single seller, was struck down by the 11th Circuit on 24 Jan 2025 ([Insurance Marketing Coalition v. FCC](https://media.ca11.uscourts.gov/opinions/pub/files/202410277.pdf)); consent should still name you. And the FCC's latest revocation order (FCC 26-67, published 26 Oct 2026, effective 25 Nov 2026) says a "stop" to a telemarketing call revokes consent "to all future calls or text messages containing an advertisement" ([Federal Register](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-10-26/pdf/2026-21821.pdf)). For purely informational calls, a "stop" can now be read as covering only that kind of call.

In the EU, the AI Act requires that people are "informed that they are interacting with an AI system" from 2 Aug 2026 ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act)). We script that disclosure into every call below, wherever you are.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is AI cold calling legal for real estate agents?

In the US, not without written consent. The FCC treats AI-generated voices as artificial voices under the TCPA, and marketing calls with an artificial voice to mobiles and home lines need prior express written consent. Expired, FSBO and bought-list owners have not given it, so those calls stay with a person who has scrubbed the Do Not Call lists.

### Can I use AI to call back people who filled in my website form?

Yes in the US, if the form had consent wording that names you and covers automated or AI-voice calls, and you call within 8 a.m. to 9 p.m. their time. Without that wording, a person should make the callback. Add the wording now so future leads qualify.

### Can an AI call my past clients?

Only with their written consent in the US. The rule for artificial-voice marketing calls to home lines has no past-client exception, and calls to mobiles need written consent too. A "thinking of selling?" check-in is marketing.

### Does the AI have to say it is an AI?

The EU requires it from 2 Aug 2026. US rules require naming the business at the start of an artificial-voice call and giving a phone number. Saying "this is an AI assistant calling for [agent]" covers both and avoids a buyer feeling tricked.

### How much does AI calling cost for a real estate agent?

The minutes are the small part. 800 minutes a month costs \$56–\$248 at Retell's published rate or \$120 at JustCall's add-on rate, plus plans and numbers; Lofty's AI sales agent is \$60 a month for 200 leads. In India the same minutes cost ₹4,416 to ₹7,534 at published rates before telephony and GST.

### What hours can an AI call buyers in Dubai?

9 a.m. to 6 p.m., under Cabinet Resolution 56 of 2024. The call must be recorded and the buyer told so at the start, numbers on the Do Not Call Register are off limits, and unanswered calls can be retried at most once a day and twice a week.

### Can AI calling work for builders in India?

Yes, for portal-lead callbacks and site-visit booking, if promotional calls go out from a 140-series number registered on DLT and confirmations carry no offers. TRAI's new 1601 series for service calls is opening sector by sector, so check whether real estate is eligible. Quote the project's RERA registration on the call. TRAI can bar a sender's outgoing calls for 15 days on a first violation.

### Do I need consent for an AI receptionist that answers my calls?

Not under the US robocall rules, which govern calls you place. If the AI records the call, check your state's recording law: some states require everyone on the call to agree, so have the AI say the call is recorded.

### Is AI calling legal in Florida?

Florida is stricter than federal law. Unsolicited sales calls that use automated dialing or play a recorded message need prior express written consent, sales calls are limited to 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., and no more than 3 calls in 24 hours on the same subject.

### Will buyers hang up on an AI?

Some will. The calls that work are the ones buyers asked for: a callback on the home they enquired about, a reminder of a showing they booked. Say it is an AI, ask at most three questions and offer a person.
