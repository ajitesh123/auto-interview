## The 2026 AI Sales Training Landscape

In B2B sales, credibility is everything. When software vendors claim they are _"#1 at everything for everyone,"_ sophisticated buyers roll their eyes.

The useful question is not "which is best?" but "which is built for a team like mine?"

## Hyperbound: Enterprise Cold-Call Roleplay

Hyperbound recognized early that junior SDRs were burning valuable leads by practicing on real prospects, and built AI buyer personas for cold-call simulation. It is a common choice for larger enterprise sales teams.

**Where it fits:**

- Enterprise SDR programs that want standardized cold-call certification.
- Teams that prefer vendor-assisted setup of buyer personas.

**What to check:** pricing and contract minimums for your team size, how much scenario editing your managers can do themselves, and how natural the response pacing feels in a recorded trial.

## Tough Tongue AI: Sales Roleplay Plus AI Voice Agents

[Tough Tongue AI](https://app.toughtongueai.com/) runs AI voice agents for calls and meetings and AI sales roleplay practice. It streams speech in and out so the AI buyer can be interrupted mid-sentence, and can push back when a rep rambles.

### 1. Custom scenarios

Sales managers write their own scenarios without code, for example:

- Buyer temperaments (Aggressive, Analytical, Sarcastic, Rushed).
- Hidden objections (e.g., _"Will push back on SOC 2 until the rep cites our HIPAA compliance"_).
- Rules for when the buyer pushes harder or opens up.

### 2. Scored feedback

Each session is scored against the rubric you define for the scenario, such as MEDDPICC, BANT, Challenger, or your own criteria, with a transcript and notes on strengths and weaknesses.

### 3. Practice and live conversations in one place

The same platform runs AI voice agents for phone calls and meetings, so a team can practice a conversation with reps and also have a voice agent handle similar conversations.

### 4. Self-serve start

Teams can sign up and start running roleplays without a sales process. Check current pricing on the website.

## Considerations for Indian and Cross-Border Sales Teams

India is a major hub for inside sales. Teams across **Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Gurgaon, and Mumbai** sell software and services to North America, Europe and the Middle East, as well as to domestic buyers.

When evaluating any platform, check:

1. **Cross-border scenarios:** Can you build personas such as American executive gatekeepers, skeptical procurement officers, or compliance-focused buyers (GDPR, SOC 2, US data sovereignty)?
2. **Accent and Hinglish handling:** Does the AI understand Indian English and code-switched Hinglish from your reps? Test with your own team, not a demo script.
3. **Pricing in context:** A large annual USD contract is a big commitment for a startup or mid-market team. Compare total cost against a small pilot.
4. **Training plus execution:** If you also want AI agents to handle live calls, a platform that does both saves a second vendor.

## Which Should You Choose?

### Choose Hyperbound if:

- You are a large enterprise with a dedicated enablement team that wants vendor-assisted setup.
- You only need roleplay practice, not live AI calling.

### Choose Tough Tongue AI if:

- You want sales managers to write and change scenarios themselves.
- You want an AI buyer that can be interrupted and push back.
- You sell **across borders** and want scenarios built around those buyers.
- You want the option to also run AI voice agents on phone calls and meetings.
- You want to start self-serve.

Whichever you shortlist, record the same scenario on each and compare realism, feedback quality and price.
