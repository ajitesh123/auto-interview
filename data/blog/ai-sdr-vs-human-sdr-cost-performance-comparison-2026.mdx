_Rewritten 26 Nov 2026. The earlier version gave cost ranges, "60 to 80 percent savings" and a 12-point scoreboard we could not trace to any source. This version prices six AI SDRs from their own pricing pages, costs a human SDR from The Bridge Group's 2025 survey and US government data, and gives you a calculator. It also replaces our older post "AI calling vs human calling". Tough Tongue AI, which runs this site, sells AI voice agents and AI sales roleplay; that section says "our product"._

**A median US SDR costs about \$114,286 in year one (\$80,000 pay plus employer benefits) and, at the median quota of 10 held meetings a month with a 3-month ramp, that is about \$1,088 per meeting.** Six AI SDRs that publish a price cost \$14,559 to \$44,571 a year at 1,000 new contacts a month, including three hours a week of a person checking their work. So an AI SDR needs only 1.1 to 3.4 held meetings a month to match the human on cost. Price is not the hard part; getting those meetings, and meetings worth having, is.

<KeyFacts>

- **Human SDR:** median on-target pay is "\$80K (Unchanged since 2022)", split \$55K base and \$25K variable; average ramp is 3.0 months and tenure 1.9 years ([The Bridge Group, 2025](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group)). Employer benefits are 30% of total compensation in US private industry ([BLS, June 2026 data](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.nr0.htm)).
- **AI SDR:** published prices run from AiSDR's "\$1.50 / person prospected" to 11x's "Starting at \$36,000 / yr". Artisan, Qualified and Relevance AI publish no price.
- **The catch:** no independent study we could find measures how many held meetings AI SDRs book. Gartner predicts that by 2028 "less than 40% of sellers will report that AI agents have improved their productivity" ([Gartner, Nov 2025](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-11-18-gartner-predicts-by-2028-ai-agents-will-outnumber-sellers-by-10x-yet-fewer-than-40-percent-of-sellers-will-report-ai-agents-improved-productivity)).
- **Outside the US the maths changes:** the same AI SDR costs 23% of a US SDR, 29% of a UK SDR and 99% of a median Indian SDR.

</KeyFacts>

Three inputs decide most answers:

- **Held meetings a month for the human.** The Bridge Group's median monthly quota is 10 "Stage 0 held", but only 60% of SDRs hit quota. An SDR who holds 6 a month, paid 60% of target variable, costs about \$1,587 per meeting, not \$1,088.
- **Held meetings per 100 contacts for the AI.** No vendor-neutral number exists. AiSDR's own pricing page says "you can expect 1-3 meetings for each 100 contacts", meaning booked, not held; treat that as a claim to test, not a benchmark.
- **Oversight hours.** SaaStr, which ran AI SDRs for six months, reported "15-20 hours per week actively managing five AI SDRs" ([SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/6-months-of-ai-sdrs-whats-worked)). That is three to four hours a week each; our default is three.

## What a human SDR really costs

**A human SDR costs pay plus employer costs, tools and a share of a manager, and books almost nothing in the first months.** The Bridge Group's 2025 survey of 351 B2B companies gives these medians (pay is US; the quota is a global median):

| Line                | Value                        | Source                                                                                                       |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| On-target pay (OTE) | \$80,000 (\$55K + \$25K)     | [Bridge Group 2025](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group) |
| Monthly quota       | 10 "Stage 0 held" (median)   | Bridge Group 2025                                                                                            |
| SDRs at quota       | 60%, lowest in study history | Bridge Group 2025                                                                                            |
| Ramp                | 3.0 months                   | [Bridge Group, tenure and ramp](https://bridgegroupinc.com/research/sdr-tenure-and-ramp-time)                |
| Tenure              | 1.9 years                    | Bridge Group, tenure and ramp                                                                                |
| SDRs per manager    | 6.4                          | Bridge Group 2025                                                                                            |
| Employer benefits   | 30.0% of total compensation  | [BLS ECEC, June 2026](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ecec.nr0.htm)                                         |

Two things push the real cost up from the table. First, ramp: if output climbs in a straight line over 3 months, a new SDR loses 1.5 months of meetings in year one. Second, tenure: at 1.9 years, you rehire and re-ramp roughly every other year, which this year-one figure does not count. Our [SDR ramp time guide](/blog/sales-onboarding-training-cut-sdr-ramp-time-ai-roleplay-2026) prices the ramp itself and gives a 90-day plan to shorten it.

In the UK, RepVue's employee-reported medians are £37,580 base and £56,399 on-target ([RepVue UK](https://repvue.com/salaries/sales-development-representative/GB)); employers add 15% National Insurance above £5,000 a year and at least 3% pension on earnings between £6,240 and £50,270 ([gov.uk NIC](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rates-and-thresholds-for-employers-2026-to-2027), [gov.uk pensions](https://www.gov.uk/workplace-pensions/what-you-your-employer-and-the-government-pay)). In India, RepVue's medians are ₹13,17,667 base and ₹18,15,709 on-target, skewed to tech and SaaS employers ([RepVue India](https://repvue.com/salaries/sales-development-representative/IN)); Payscale's 71 profiles average ₹7,30,080 base. Employer EPF comes on top; its wage ceiling rose to ₹25,000 a month on 17 Sep 2026 ([SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/09/19/labour-ministry-notifies-25000-wage-ceiling-under-social-security-code/)) ([Payscale](https://www.payscale.com/research/IN/Job=Sales_Development_Representative_%28SDR%29/Salary)).

## Why the answer flips in India

**AI SDRs are priced in dollars and people are paid in local currency, so the same plan is cheap next to a US salary and expensive next to an Indian one.** At the RBI reference rate of ₹92.49 to the dollar on 25 Nov 2026, AiSDR at 1,000 contacts a month costs ₹16.6 lakh a year before oversight, about what RepVue's median Indian SDR earns on target, before EPF and gratuity. Against Payscale's average base of ₹7.3 lakh, the AI SDR costs more than twice the person.

## Do AI SDRs actually book meetings?

**Some do, but there is no independent benchmark, and the most public failure is well documented.** What we could verify:

- **SaaStr's own deployment** (a user, not a vendor, though its vendors, including Artisan for outbound, helped set the agents up): "19,847 total messages sent in six months", a "6.7% overall response rate (industry average ~3%)" and a "4% positive response rate". It published no count of meetings from outbound AI SDRs; its "100+ meetings booked" came from an inbound agent ([SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/6-months-of-ai-sdrs-whats-worked)).
- **11x**, the best-funded AI SDR, was reported by TechCrunch to list customers that said they were not customers. One employee told TechCrunch, "We were losing 70-80% of customers that came through the door"; 11x said its "retention rate is currently 79%" ([TechCrunch, Mar 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have)).
- **Salesforce's 2026 survey** of 4,050 sales professionals: "54% of sellers say they've used agents, and nearly 9 in 10 plan to by 2027", and "48% say they lack bandwidth to do adequate cold outreach" ([Salesforce, Feb 2026](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/state-of-sales-report-announcement-2026/)). Salesforce sells an AI SDR.
- **Gartner:** "By 2028, AI agents will outnumber human sellers by tenfold, but less than 40% of sellers will report that AI agents have improved their productivity."

Vendor case studies (HubSpot's "26 deals across 1,000 contacts", Qualified's "does the work of 13 SDRs") are claims from the seller. The only number that settles it is yours, from a pilot that splits the same list.

## AI calling vs human calling: the phone version

**On the phone, the comparison is per-minute cost against a human caller's connect and meeting rates, and Gong has the only large first-party data on the human side.** Gong analysed 300M+ cold calls: "The average rep only connects with 5.4% of prospects, whereas the top quartile reps connect with 13.3%", and "the top reps booked more than 3x the meetings compared to the average rep (16.7% vs 4.6%)", measured per conversation ([Gong Labs](https://www.gong.io/resources/labs/the-hidden-power-of-cold-calling-insights-from-300m-calls/)).

## The hybrid model: where each one fits

**Most teams that use AI SDRs split the work by deal size and by channel, not by replacing people one for one.** A common split, and the one the calculator lets you price:

| Work                                                    | Who does it                                             | Why                                                                |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| List building, research, first emails to small accounts | AI SDR, with a person approving at first                | High volume, low value per account; the cost per meeting is lowest |
| Replies, objections, "call me now"                      | Human SDR                                               | Judgement; a bad reply costs the account                           |
| Accounts above your average deal size                   | Human SDR (AI for research only)                        | One meeting is worth more than the whole AI licence                |
| Cold calls                                              | Human caller, or an AI voice agent where consent allows | Gong's top callers book 3x the average; check the rules first      |

One AI SDR rarely replaces a team of five: the meetings it books still need someone to answer replies and hand over well, and SaaStr's experience was three to four hours a week of a person per agent.

## What to do if you are...

- **A founder with no SDR yet:** do the first 100 conversations yourself; you need to hear the objections before any SDR, human or AI, can handle them. Then pilot one AI SDR on a monthly plan.
- **A US team of 5+ SDRs:** split one segment for 60 days between an AI SDR and a human, count held meetings that AEs accept, and put both into the calculator. Keep humans on accounts above your average deal size.
- **An Indian team selling to the US:** a dollar-priced AI SDR costs about what a good SDR in Bengaluru or Pune does. Price both, and compare against what your best SDR books today.
- **An Indian team selling in India:** look at voice and WhatsApp agents paid per minute or per message, and check the TRAI and DPDP rules first ([India AI calling rules](/blog/ai-calling-india-dpdp-trai-dlt-compliance-complete-guide-2026)).
- **A VP whose SDRs miss quota:** before replacing them, check held meetings per SDR against the median quota of 10. An SDR at 6, paid 60% of variable, costs about \$1,587 per meeting; the same SDR at 8, paid 80%, costs about \$1,276, a fifth less, with no new software.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the cheapest AI SDR?

Among plans with a published price on 26 Nov 2026, Salesforge (\$499 a month, billed quarterly or annually) and Reply.io's Jason AI (\$500 a month billed annually, for 1,000 active contacts) cost about \$6,000 a year. AiSDR's pay-as-you-go plan has no monthly fee and costs \$1.50 per person prospected, so it is cheapest at low volume.

### Is an AI SDR cheaper than a human SDR?

On list price, yes in the US and UK: six published AI SDR plans cost \$14,559 to \$44,571 a year including oversight, against about \$114,286 for a median US SDR. Per held meeting it depends on how many meetings the AI books; it breaks even at 1.1 to 3.4 a month. In India, a dollar-priced AI SDR costs about the same as a median SDR's pay.

### How much does an AI SDR cost per month?

Published prices on 26 Nov 2026: Salesforge \$499 a month, Reply.io's Jason AI from \$500 a month billed annually, Valley \$500 for one seat, AiSDR \$1.50 per person prospected, HubSpot's prospecting agent \$1 per lead it recommends, and 11x from \$36,000 a year. Artisan, Qualified and Relevance AI quote on request.

### What is the fully loaded cost of an SDR?

In the US, about \$114,286 in year one at The Bridge Group's median \$80,000 on-target pay plus employer benefits at BLS's 30% of total compensation, before tools and a manager's time. In the UK, RepVue's median £56,399 on-target plus employer National Insurance and pension comes to about £65,430.

### Will AI replace SDRs?

Not on the evidence so far. AI SDRs send far more messages than people, but no independent study shows they book more held meetings per pound or dollar, and Gartner predicts fewer than 40% of sellers will say AI agents made them more productive by 2028. Most teams that use them keep people for calls, replies and larger accounts.

### What are AI SDRs bad at?

Conversations that need judgement: an unexpected objection, a buyer who wants to talk now, a reply that should go to a manager. They also burn email domains if pointed at bad lists, which is why every plan needs a person checking its work.

### How do I compare AI SDR and human SDR performance fairly?

Split one list at random for 60 days, give both arms the same offer and calendar, and count held meetings the AE accepts, not emails sent or meetings booked. Then divide each arm's full cost by those meetings.

### Is AI calling better than a human calling?

It is cheaper per minute and never tires, but Gong's data shows how much a skilled human caller moves the result: top-quartile reps connect 13.3% of the time and book a meeting from 16.7% of conversations, against 5.4% and 4.6% for the average rep. Compare cost per meeting, not cost per dial, and check the consent rules first.
