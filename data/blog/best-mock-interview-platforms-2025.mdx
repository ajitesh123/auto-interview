## Why Mock Interviews Actually Work (The Science)

When you're in an interview hot seat, your brain's amygdala interprets it as a threat, flooding your system with stress hormones. This shuts down your prefrontal cortex (logical thinking), disrupts memory retrieval, and triggers freeze responses.

**Mock interviews are exposure therapy for your brain.** Each practice session desensitizes your amygdala. By your fifth mock, your brain stops treating interviews like crises and starts treating them like conversations.

Beyond neuroscience, mock interviews deliver three critical benefits:

**Pattern Recognition** – After answering common questions 10 times, your brain creates neural shortcuts. You stop consciously constructing sentences and start retrieving polished narratives.

**Blind Spot Revelation** – You don't know you say "um" 47 \times in 10 minutes until someone counts. You don't realize your answers ramble until you watch yourself on video.

**Authentic Confidence** – Real confidence comes from knowing you've successfully done this before, even if it was "just practice."

## 5 Best Practices to Maximize Results

**1. Leverage Custom Scenarios Strategically**  
Don't just use generic interview scenarios. On [Tough Tongue AI](https://www.toughtongueai.com/), create specific scenarios for:

- Each company you're interviewing with (replicate their known interview style)
- Your weak spots (create scenarios focused on questions you struggle with)
- Unusual situations in your background (employment gaps, career switches, unique experiences)

**2. Simulate Real Conditions**  
Dress appropriately, use the same physical space, eliminate distractions, set genuine stakes. Your brain is context-dependent - practice in conditions matching your real interviews.

**3. Record Everything & Review Within 2 Hours**  
Memory consolidation research shows reviewing performances within 2 hours solidifies learning. With Tough Tongue AI's video analysis, watch for filler words, body language, facial expressions, and whether you actually answered the question.

**4. Space Out Practice (Don't Cram)**  
Early prep: 2-3 mocks/week. Mid prep: 3-4 mocks/week. Final push: 4-5 mocks/week. The 24-48 hour gap between mocks lets your brain consolidate lessons during sleep.

**5. Progress From Generic to Specific**  
Week 1-2: Practice with general scenarios to build confidence. Week 3-6: Create custom scenarios replicating your target companies. Week 7-8: Practice your exact interview (if you know the format), create scenarios with likely questions based on your research.
