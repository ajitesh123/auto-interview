## What is SIP Trunking and Why Does It Matter for AI Calling?

SIP trunking is the bridge between your AI voice software and real phone numbers in India. Every time your AI agent dials a prospect, the audio travels through your SIP provider's infrastructure.

The SIP provider handles five critical functions for AI calling:

1. **Number routing**: Connecting calls from your AI's virtual number to the prospect's mobile or landline
2. **Media encryption**: SRTP encryption protecting call audio from interception
3. **Regulatory compliance**: TRAI mandates, DNC scrubbing, KYC verification
4. **Caller ID presentation**: What the prospect sees when your AI calls them
5. **Network quality**: Latency, jitter, and packet loss directly affecting call clarity

```text
AI Voice Platform            SIP Provider (Plivo/Vobiz)     Indian Phone Network
(LiveKit/Vapi/Retell)              |                         (Airtel/Jio/BSNL)
         |                         |                                 |
         |--- SIP INVITE -------->|                                  |
         |    (audio stream)       |--- PSTN route ----------------->|
         |                         |                                 |--- rings prospect
         |<--- RTP audio ----------|<--- carrier audio --------------|
         |    (real-time)          |                                 |
         |                         |
    [AI processes audio]   [Plivo/Vobiz handles]
    [generates response]   [- Caller ID]
                           [- Encryption]
                           [- TRAI rules]
                           [- DNC scrub]
```

_Bottom line: Your AI agent's call quality, answer rates, and legal compliance depend entirely on which SIP provider you choose._

## TRAI Compliance: The Non-Negotiable Layer

Both Plivo and Vobiz are TRAI-compliant. But compliance is also your responsibility as the AI calling operator. Here is exactly what you must do regardless of which provider you choose.

### 1. DLT Registration (Mandatory for 140-Series)

TRAI's Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) platform is the regulatory backbone for commercial voice calling in India. For any AI outbound campaign using 140-series numbers:

- Register your entity on any DLT portal (BSNL BSNL-IT, Airtel DLT, Vodafone Vi DLT, Jio Trueconnect)
- Obtain your **Principal Entity ID** (usually within 3-5 business days)
- Register each campaign header and message template
- Link your registered DID to your DLT entity

Without DLT registration, carriers will auto-block your 140-series calls at the network level. Vobiz's 7972 and 92 series are mobile-format numbers that do not require DLT for all use cases, but you should verify current TRAI rules with your provider.

### 2. TRAI Calling Hours Restriction

Commercial AI calls are permitted only between **9:00 AM and 9:00 PM** in the prospect's local time zone. Build this into your AI agent's scheduling logic. Both providers allow calls outside these hours technically; the restriction is regulatory, not technical.

### 3. Do Not Call (DNC) Registry Compliance

The National Do Not Call Registry lists numbers that have opted out of commercial calls. Both Plivo and Vobiz scrub against this registry automatically. However, operators must also:

- Honor verbal opt-outs immediately during calls
- Update your DNC list within 24 hours of any opt-out
- Retain call records for 90 days minimum for TRAI audit purposes

### 4. AI Disclosure (Emerging Requirement)

India does not yet have a formal AI voice disclosure mandate. However, TRAI's 2025 consultation paper on AI-generated calls signals incoming regulation. Current best practice is to disclose at the start of every call:

> "Hello, I am an AI assistant calling on behalf of [Company Name]. This call may be recorded for quality purposes."

This reduces complaint rates and positions you ahead of the regulation.

# Your WebSocket audio handler

## Latency: SIP Network vs AI Engine

A common mistake is treating SIP network latency as the primary latency variable. It is not.

**Network latency** (what Plivo and Vobiz contribute): 80-100ms for India-to-India calls. This is the time for audio to travel from your AI platform to the SIP provider and out to the prospect's phone.

**AI engine latency** (the bigger variable): For cascade architecture (STT -> LLM -> TTS), total processing adds 800-1200ms per turn. For native voice-to-voice engines, total latency is under 200ms.

```text
Latency breakdown for a typical AI calling turn:

CASCADE ARCHITECTURE (most AI calling platforms):
  STT processing:     150-300ms
  LLM inference:      400-800ms
  TTS synthesis:      200-400ms
  SIP network:         80-100ms
  Total:             830-1600ms   <-- Noticeable pause. Prospects hang up.

NATIVE VOICE-TO-VOICE (TTGE architecture):
  Voice processing:    80-120ms
  SIP network:         80-100ms
  Total:             160-220ms    <-- Below human perception threshold.
```

Indian mobile users have extremely low tolerance for call pauses. A 1-second response delay is detectable and causes hang-ups. The SIP provider's 80ms vs 100ms network latency difference matters far less than whether your AI engine uses a cascade or native voice architecture.

**TTGE (Tough Tongue's voice engine)** solves this at the model level. It processes audio natively without a cascade pipeline, achieving sub-200ms total latency even on Plivo's standard India infrastructure. When combined with Vobiz's 80ms network latency, calls feel indistinguishable from a human conversation.
