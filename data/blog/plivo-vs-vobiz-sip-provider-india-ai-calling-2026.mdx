## Understanding SIP Trunking for AI Calling in India

**Definition:** A SIP (Session Initiation Protocol) trunk is the telephony infrastructure that connects your AI voice agent software to real Indian phone numbers. Without a SIP provider, your AI agent can only call other software. With a SIP trunk, it can dial any mobile or landline number in India.

For AI calling specifically, the SIP provider sits between your voice AI platform (LiveKit, Vapi, Retell, custom) and the Indian phone network. Every call your AI makes goes through the SIP provider's infrastructure.

```text
AI Voice Platform                SIP Provider               Indian Phone Network
(LiveKit / Vapi / Custom)        (Plivo / Vobiz)            (Airtel, Jio, BSNL)
         |                              |                              |
         |--- SIP INVITE (audio) ------>|                              |
         |                              |--- PSTN route -------------->|
         |                              |                              |--- rings prospect
         |                              |                              |
         |<-- audio stream (RTP) -------|<-- carrier audio ------------|
```

The SIP provider handles: routing, caller ID presentation, media encryption (SRTP), TRAI compliance, KYC verification for number assignment, and DNC (Do Not Call) scrubbing.

_Bottom line: Your AI is only as good as its SIP layer. A bad SIP provider means dropped calls, wrong caller IDs, TRAI violations, and carrier spam flags._

## Technical Deep Dive: SIP Configuration for AI Voice Agents

### Setting Up Plivo for LiveKit / Vapi

```python
# Example: Configuring a Plivo SIP trunk for a LiveKit-based AI agent

import plivo

client = plivo.RestClient(auth_id="YOUR_AUTH_ID", auth_token="YOUR_AUTH_TOKEN")

# Step 1: Create a SIP trunk
trunk = client.phlo.get(phlo_id="your_phlo_id")

# Step 2: Set India-pinned SIP endpoint
# Plivo India SIP edge: sip.plivo.com (auto-routes to IN when calling Indian numbers)
# For media pinning, configure via Plivo console -> Zentrunk -> Region: Asia-Pacific (India)

# Step 3: Outbound call configuration
response = client.calls.create(
    from_='+918069XXXXXX',   # Your Plivo DID (080 Bengaluru format)
    to_='+919XXXXXXXXX',      # Prospect mobile
    answer_url='https://your-livekit-agent.com/answer',
    answer_method='POST',
)

print(response)
```

**Key Plivo configuration notes for AI calling:**

1. Set `answer_url` to your LiveKit agent's webhook endpoint
2. Enable **SRTP** in trunk settings to encrypt media before it reaches the AI model
3. Configure region pinning to `ap-south-1` (Mumbai) for TRAI compliance
4. Set call timeout to 30 seconds maximum for AI agent efficiency

### Setting Up Vobiz for LiveKit / Vapi

# Example: Configuring a Vobiz SIP trunk for a Vapi-based AI agent

import requests

VOBIZ_API_KEY = "your_vobiz_api_key"

## TRAI Compliance: What Every AI Calling Operator Must Know

TRAI (Telecom Regulatory Authority of India) has strict regulations for commercial voice calling. Both Plivo and Vobiz are TRAI-compliant, but compliance is also your responsibility as the operator.

**Mandatory compliance requirements for AI calling in India:**

### 1. Number Series Registration (DLT)

The **140 series** is the only TRAI-approved number series for commercial/promotional voice calls to consumer mobile numbers. You must:

- Register your entity on the DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology) platform (run by BSNL, Airtel, Vodafone)
- Get your **Principal Entity ID** approved
- Register each call template/script
- Link your Plivo or Vobiz DID to your DLT registration

Without DLT registration, carriers will auto-block your calls.

### 2. AI Voice Disclosure (Emerging Requirement)

India does not yet have a formal AI disclosure mandate equivalent to the EU AI Act. However, TRAI's 2025 consultation paper on AI-generated calls indicated pending regulations. Best practice is to disclose at the start of the call:

> "Hello, I am an AI assistant calling on behalf of [Company]. This call may be recorded."

This disclosure protects you legally and reduces complaint filings.

### 3. Do Not Call (DNC) Compliance

Both providers support DNC scrubbing. However, you must also:

- Never call numbers registered on the National Do Not Call Registry
- Honour verbal opt-out requests immediately (update your CRM/dialer within 24 hours)
- Maintain call records for 90 days minimum for audit purposes

### 4. Calling Hours

TRAI mandates no commercial calls before **9:00 AM** or after **9:00 PM** in the prospect's local time zone. Configure your AI agent's dialing schedule accordingly.

## A Note on AI Voice Latency: The Layer Above SIP

The SIP provider is one layer of latency. But the bigger latency challenge for AI calling in India is the voice AI engine itself.

Most AI calling platforms use a **cascade architecture**: Speech-to-Text converts audio to text, an LLM generates a text response, and Text-to-Speech converts it back to audio. This pipeline introduces 800-1200ms of additional latency on top of your SIP network latency.

The result: even with Vobiz's 80ms network latency, a cascade-architecture AI agent still has a noticeable 1+ second response delay. Indian prospects, accustomed to the immediacy of mobile conversations, hang up at higher rates when this pause is detectable.

**TTGE (Tough Tongue's proprietary voice engine)** solves this at the model level. Rather than the cascade approach, TTGE processes audio natively, responding in under 150ms total end-to-end latency. When combined with Vobiz's 80ms network latency, the total call latency stays inside the threshold of human conversational perception. The prospect never detects they are speaking to an AI.

If you are serious about AI calling in India at scale, the SIP provider choice (Plivo vs Vobiz) is your first infrastructure decision. The voice AI engine powering those calls is your second, and often the more consequential one.

**Is AI calling TRAI-compliant in India?**
AI calling is permitted in India, but it requires using TRAI-approved number series (140 for promotional calls), DLT entity registration, DNC compliance, and calling only within permitted hours (9 AM to 9 PM). Both Plivo and Vobiz are TRAI-compliant infrastructure providers, but DLT registration is the operator's responsibility.

**What latency should I expect for AI calling in India?**
The SIP network layer (Plivo or Vobiz) contributes approximately 80-100ms. The AI voice engine contributes the majority of perceived latency: cascade-architecture engines add 800-1200ms, while native voice engines like TTGE respond in under 150ms total. For natural-sounding AI calls in India, native voice architecture is strongly recommended.
