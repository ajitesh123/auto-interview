## Why Real Estate Needs "Made-for-India" AI Calling

Using a generic global AI caller for Indian real estate is a mistake.

1. **The Code-Switching Requirement:** A buyer might say, _"Budget around 1.5 crores hai, but possession jaldi chahiye"_ (Budget is 1.5Cr, but need early possession). The AI must instantly parse "1.5 crores" into a numerical value and "jaldi" into a timeline (0-3 months) and log this structured data into the CRM. Global models fail at this level of Hinglish nuance.
2. **Truecaller Spam:** If you dial 5,000 leads from one number, it will be marked as spam. Indian platforms manage intelligent number rotation and DLT headers.
3. **WhatsApp Follow-Ups:** In India, business happens on WhatsApp. The AI must trigger a WhatsApp message containing the project brochure instantly after the call ends.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can AI calling software book site visits automatically?**
A: Yes. Platforms like Tough Tongue AI connect to your sales team's calendar via APIs (like Calendly or native CRM calendars). The AI asks the prospect for a preferred time, checks availability in real-time, books the slot, and sends a WhatsApp confirmation.

**Q: How much does AI calling cost for real estate leads?**
A: In India, premium platforms charge per connected minute or per successful outcome. This usually ranges from ₹5 to ₹15 per qualified lead. Compared to the cost of generating a Facebook lead (₹500 - ₹2000), spending ₹10 to ensure it is contacted instantly and qualified provides massive ROI.

**Q: What happens if an AI agent cannot answer a complex real estate question?**
A: You provide the AI with a Knowledge Base (RAG - Retrieval-Augmented Generation) containing your project brochure, pricing sheet, and FAQs. If the user asks something outside that scope, the AI is programmed to say, "That's a great question, let me have our senior sales manager call you right back with the exact details," logging the specific query for the human team.
