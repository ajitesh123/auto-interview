## Why "Global" Cold Callers Fail in India

If you browse developer forums, you'll see a recurring theme: _"I used Vapi/Retell for my Indian outbound campaign and the unit economics killed me."_

1. **The Cost Crisis:** Global platforms charge in USD per minute (often $0.12 - $0.20). Add Indian SIP trunk costs, and you are paying ₹15-₹20 per minute. A human Indian SDR is significantly cheaper. True Indian platforms like **Tough Tongue AI** offer localized INR pricing that makes AI cheaper than human labor.
2. **The "Hello? Hello?" Loop:** When an Indian prospect mumbles "Hello" against a background of traffic noise, global VAD (Voice Activity Detection) models panic, causing the AI to repeat itself or talk over the prospect.
3. **Truecaller Destruction:** If your AI makes 5,000 cold calls a day without intelligent rotation and verified DLT headers, Truecaller will mark your numbers as "Spam" within 48 hours, destroying your pickup rate.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can AI cold calling software handle Indian accents and Hinglish?**
A: Yes, but only if you use an India-native platform. Tough Tongue AI and Bolna use Speech-to-Text models trained on narrowband Indian telephony data, allowing them to comprehend heavy accents and Hinglish code-switching with 95%+ accuracy. Global tools like Retell degrade severely on Indian lines.

**Q: Is AI cold calling legal in India under TRAI rules?**
A: Yes, provided you adhere to TRAI's UCC (Unsolicited Commercial Communication) regulations. You must scrub your lists against the National Do Not Call (NDNC) registry and use registered DLT headers. The best platforms (like Tough Tongue AI) handle this scrubbing automatically at the orchestration layer.

**Q: What is the ROI of an AI SDR compared to a human BPO in India?**
A: An AI SDR costs approximately ₹5 to ₹15 per connected minute (or outcome), operates 24/7 with zero attrition, and dials infinitely faster. For high-volume top-of-funnel qualification, AI reduces Cost-Per-Lead (CPL) by over 60% compared to traditional Indian BPOs.

**Q: Will Truecaller block my AI calling numbers?**
A: If you blast thousands of calls from a single SIP trunk, yes. Top platforms mitigate this via intelligent number rotation, verified business caller IDs, and maintaining a high conversation-to-hangup ratio by sounding natural, minimizing user spam reports.
