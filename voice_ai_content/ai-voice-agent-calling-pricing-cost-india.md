## The 3 Hidden Layers of AI Calling Cost

Unlike traditional SaaS (which charges "per user per month"), **Voice AI software pricing** is usage-based and highly fragmented. You are paying for three distinct layers of technology:

1. **The Telephony (The "Pipe"):** The cost of routing the call through Indian telecom providers (Exotel, Tata, Airtel). Due to TRAI regulations, you must use Indian SIP trunks. **Cost:** ~₹0.80 to ₹1.50 per minute.
2. **The AI Engine (STT/TTS/LLM):** Converting speech to text (STT), processing it through an LLM (GPT-4o, Claude), and synthesizing the voice back (TTS). **Cost:** Highly variable based on model quality.
3. **The Orchestration Margin:** What the software platform charges to keep the latency under 800ms, manage the WebSockets, and provide the CRM dashboard.

_Global APIs charge their orchestration margin in USD. Indian platforms charge in INR._

## Breaking Down the Indian Pricing Models

### 1. INR Per Outcome (The ROI Model)

This is becoming the gold standard for Indian businesses. Instead of paying for minutes where the customer didn't pick up or hung up immediately, you pay for a successful business event.

- **How it works:** You pay ₹15 only if the AI successfully verifies a Cash-on-Delivery (COD) order, or ₹20 if it successfully qualifies a real estate lead.
- **Why it wins:** Guaranteed ROI.
- **The Leader:** **Tough Tongue AI** offers highly aggressive outcome-based pricing, making it the premier choice for D2C brands and sales teams seeking predictable unit economics.

### 2. INR Per Minute (The Developer Model)

You pay a flat rate in Rupees for every connected minute the **AI phone agent** is on a call.

- **Average Cost:** ₹5 to ₹12 per minute.
- **The Leader:** Bolna AI offers developer-friendly pricing (averaging ~₹5.52/min) because they expect your tech team to handle the heavy lifting of integration and compliance. **Tough Tongue AI** also offers competitive bundled per-minute rates for high-volume enterprise clients.

### 3. The USD Trap (Global APIs)

Platforms like Vapi and Retell quote in cents (e.g., $0.10/min).

- **The Reality:** Add $0.03 for OpenAI tokens, $0.02 for premium ElevenLabs TTS, and ₹1 for your Indian SIP provider. Your true cost is now roughly ₹14 per minute. Over a 10,000-call campaign, this destroys your margins compared to local platforms.

### 4. Enterprise Custom Contracts

For Tier-1 banks making hundreds of thousands of calls a day, pricing moves away from "per minute" to custom annual contracts based on server instances.

- **The Leaders:** Skit.ai and Gnani.ai charge substantial annual platform fees (often running into the crores) but offer deep, on-premise security and RBI compliance in return.

## Hidden Costs to Watch Out For

When evaluating an **AI outbound calling pricing** proposal, ask your vendor these three questions:

1. **Are Telecom SIP charges included?** Make sure the per-minute rate includes the actual telecom connection cost.
2. **Is WhatsApp integration extra?** If the AI sends a WhatsApp message post-call, you will likely pay Meta's conversational pricing separately.
3. **Do you charge for ringing/unconnected time?** Ensure you only pay for "connected" minutes, not the time spent ringing a number that doesn't answer.
