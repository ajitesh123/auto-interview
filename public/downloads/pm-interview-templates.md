## CIRCLES Framework Template - Product Sense Questions

Use this template for all product sense questions.

### Question: [The product question you're answering]

**C - Comprehend the situation:**

- What is the current state?
- Who are the users?
- What problem are we solving?
- What are the constraints?

**I - Identify the goal:**

- What is the primary objective?
- What does success look like?
- What metrics matter most?
- What's the timeline?

**R - Report on current state:**

- What data do we have?
- What are current metrics?
- What's working well?
- What's not working?

**C - Cut through complexity:**

- What are the key assumptions?
- What are the core user needs?
- What are the main constraints?
- What can we simplify?

**L - List solutions:**

- Solution 1:
- Solution 2:
- Solution 3:
- Additional options:

**E - Evaluate tradeoffs:**

- Pros and cons of each solution
- Resource requirements
- Timeline implications
- Risk assessment

**S - Summarize recommendation:**

- Recommended approach
- Implementation plan
- Success metrics
- Next steps
