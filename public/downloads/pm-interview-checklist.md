# PM Interview Prep Checklist - 6 Week Study Plan

## Week 1-2: Foundation Building

### Days 1-3: Study PM Frameworks

- [ ] Master CIRCLES framework (Comprehend, Identify, Report, Cut, List, Evaluate, Summarize)
- [ ] Learn STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- [ ] Study Impact vs Effort prioritization matrix
- [ ] Practice MoSCoW prioritization (Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have)

### Days 4-7: Company Research

- [ ] Research target companies and their products
- [ ] Study company culture and values
- [ ] Understand their interview process
- [ ] Review recent company news and product launches

### Days 8-14: Behavioral Story Preparation

- [ ] Prepare 10 behavioral stories using STAR method
- [ ] Practice telling stories in 2-3 minutes each
- [ ] Record yourself telling stories and review for clarity
- [ ] Get feedback from peers or mentors

## Week 3-4: Practice & Refinement

### Days 15-21: Product Sense Practice

- [ ] Practice 5 product sense questions daily (30 minutes)
- [ ] Use CIRCLES framework consistently
- [ ] Time yourself: 3 minutes to think, 5 minutes to present
- [ ] Focus on thinking aloud and showing your process

### Days 22-28: Mock Interview Practice

- [ ] Complete 4-6 mock interviews with peers or AI
- [ ] Practice with Tough Tongue AI PM Collection
- [ ] Focus on timing, clarity, and framework consistency
- [ ] Record mock interviews and review performance

## Week 5-6: Final Preparation

### Days 29-35: Company-Specific Preparation

- [ ] Create custom scenarios for each target company
- [ ] Practice company-specific questions
- [ ] Research interviewer backgrounds on LinkedIn
- [ ] Prepare thoughtful questions about the role and team

### Days 36-42: Final Review

- [ ] Complete final mock interviews
- [ ] Refine answers based on feedback
- [ ] Practice elevator pitch and "Tell me about yourself"
- [ ] Prepare interview day logistics

## Daily Practice Schedule

### Morning (30 minutes)

- [ ] Review 2-3 behavioral stories
- [ ] Practice 1 product sense question
- [ ] Read industry news and trends

### Evening (30 minutes)

- [ ] Practice estimation questions
- [ ] Review company-specific research
- [ ] Practice answering common questions

## Interview Day Checklist

### Night Before

- [ ] Research interviewer(s) on LinkedIn
- [ ] Review company's recent news
- [ ] Prepare 3-5 thoughtful questions
- [ ] Test technology (video, audio, internet)
- [ ] Prepare environment (lighting, background)
- [ ] Get good night's sleep

### Day Of

- [ ] Arrive 5 minutes early (virtual or in-person)
- [ ] Dress professionally
- [ ] Have resume and notes accessible
- [ ] Smile and make eye contact
- [ ] Listen actively and ask clarifying questions
- [ ] Use frameworks consistently
- [ ] Show enthusiasm for the role

## Key Metrics to Track

- [ ] Number of mock interviews completed
- [ ] Time spent practicing each day
- [ ] Confidence level (1-10 scale)
- [ ] Areas needing improvement
- [ ] Questions you struggled with
