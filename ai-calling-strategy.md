## Section 16: Six-Week Measurement and Test Plan

### Objective

Validate whether a hybrid AI-human calling workflow outperforms human-only calling for your specific sales motion.

### Pre-Test Requirements

- Minimum 500 leads per group (AI-only, human-only, hybrid)
- Equal lead quality across all groups (same source, same scoring)
- Consistent CRM tracking for all groups
- Baseline metrics documented before test begins

### Week-by-Week Plan

| Week | Action | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Document baseline. Human team performance: connect rate, meetings, cost per meeting. | Baseline report |
| **Week 2** | Launch AI-only group. 500 leads assigned to AI caller. | AI connect rate, qualification rate, cost |
| **Week 3** | Launch hybrid group. AI qualifies, humans close. Train reps on handoff using [Tough Tongue AI](https://app.toughtongueai.com/). | Handoff success rate, meeting quality |
| **Week 4** | Full operation, all three groups running simultaneously. | Side-by-side comparison of all KPIs |
| **Week 5** | Mid-test analysis. Identify winning approach by metric. Adjust scripts and handoff triggers. | Interim report with statistical significance check |
| **Week 6** | Final analysis. Calculate ROI for each approach. Document recommended model. | Final report with go/no-go recommendation |

### Success Criteria

- Hybrid must beat human-only on cost per meeting by at least 30 percent
- Hybrid must match or exceed human-only on meeting show rate
- Rep satisfaction score must remain above 7/10
- Compliance violations must remain at zero

### Post-Test Actions

- Scale winning model across full sales team
- Implement ongoing [Tough Tongue AI](https://app.toughtongueai.com/) practice sessions for all reps
- Schedule quarterly re-tests as AI technology improves
- Share results internally as a case study for leadership buy-in
