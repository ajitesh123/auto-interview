## 3. What ranks today (checked by the writers)

- PG's own essays rank for their titles but are long, undated in context and not sequenced into a
  practice plan; summary blogs (Medium, Substack, Readwise lists) paraphrase without sources, mix in
  generic advice and misattribute lines (e.g. "build something 100 people love", "write code, talk to
  users, exercise").
- YC pages (ycombinator.com/about, /deal, /faq) answer the batch facts but not "what do I do each month".

- Verified YC batch facts as of Nov 2026 (deal $125K for 7% + $375K uncapped MFN SAFE; four batches a
