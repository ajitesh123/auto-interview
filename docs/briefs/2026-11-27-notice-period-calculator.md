# Brief: /notice-period-calculator (Part 2, job value, 27 Nov 2026)

## Why

Every job switcher in India hits the notice-period wall: 1 in 3 IT roles carry 90 days (AIM 2022) while 27% of hiring demand wants joiners in 15 days (foundit, Mar 2026). Forum questions (Fishbowl, Blind, greytHR, LawyersClubIndia, r/developersIndia) ask: does the resignation day count, calendar or working days, can leave shorten it, how is buyout calculated (basic vs gross, ÷30 vs ÷26), can the company refuse, when is F&F paid. VALUE BACKLOG listed a notice-period / joining-date planner since Nov 24.

## SERP teardown (27 Nov)

SalaryBox, Hyring, Intervue, LoopCV, Petpooja, calculator.academy, Toolisky, TotalCalcHub, TaxFly (UK). None combines LWD (start rule + calendar/working + holidays) + leave set-off + buyout against a joining date + F&F due date + gratuity line; they disagree on whether the resignation day counts; most cite no law; none reflects the Labour Codes (in force 21 Nov 2025) in the tool.

## What the reader can do after the page

1. Get their last working day the way their contract counts it (start day, calendar/working days Mon–Fri or Mon–Sat, calendar month or 30 days, holidays).
2. See the new LWD if leave is set off.
3. Enter the new employer's joining date: days short and buyout cost at ÷30 (calendar) and ÷26 (Mon–Sat working).
4. See when final wages are due (Code on Wages s.17(2), 2 working days) and whether they cross the 5-year gratuity line (1 year fixed-term).
5. Copy an early-release email to the manager and a joining-date email to the recruiter.
