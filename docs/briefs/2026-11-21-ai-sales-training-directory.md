# Brief: /ai-sales-training rebuilt as an honest directory (Part 1, Nov 21 2026)

## Why

Walk on Nov 18 and Nov 21 scored it 2/2/2/4 (modern/consistent/valuable/fast): legacy raw hex (122 hits), a sans
display H1, a "Comprehensive benchmark matrix" with "Published latency" and "Architecture" columns we cannot source,
"biological threshold (500ms)" copy, invented "Live Dial Gating" practices, and a 6-type schema blob. It is linked from
the homepage and sits in the AI-sales cluster that makes up ~44% of the site's clicks (Brain SEARCH DATA).

## What the reader can do after the page

1. Tell the three jobs apart (practise calls with an AI buyer / teach and track training / let AI make the calls) and
   jump to the right list.
2. Compare every tool in that list on price as written on the vendor's own page, free start and fit, with the date
   each price was checked and a link to the page.
3. Go to the owner guide for the deep comparison, cost calculator or legal check.
4. Start a free practice call on Tough Tongue AI (our product, marked).

## Data (no new numbers; all from files already sourced on vendor pages)

- data/salesRoleplayData.ts: AI practice tools, checked 13 Nov 2026.
- data/salesTrainingPlatforms.ts: course libraries, programmes, enablement suites and LMS, checked 18 Nov 2026.
- data/aiCallingSalesData.ts: AI calling agents and dialers, checked 11 Nov 2026.
  Every count on the page is computed from these arrays.

## Visuals

- StatRow: tools listed, how many publish a price, how many offer a free start (computed).
- SegmentBar in Figure: price status across all listed tools (published / free then quote / quote only).
- Three tables (stacked cards under sm), names linked to the vendor page we opened.

## Removed

Latency column, "Architecture" and "Methodology" labels, the 500 ms "biological threshold", unsourced practice claims,
the ItemList/SoftwareApplication/speakable schema blob. Kept: URL, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage from visible FAQs.
