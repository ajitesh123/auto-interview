## 1. Who is searching and why

Sales leaders, founders and RevOps deciding whether to put an AI on the phone, what it costs and
whether it is legal; L&D and sales managers looking at AI roleplay. They want a straight answer,
prices and rules, not a vendor pitch.

Cluster to link: pricing breakdown, ROI calculator, US TCPA guide, India compliance guide,
roleplay scenarios, rubric template, /learn-ai/voice-ai-agents, /cold-call-practice.
