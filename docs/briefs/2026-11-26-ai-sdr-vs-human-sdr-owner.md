## Original asset

- data/aiSdrVsHuman.ts: pure engine. AI = vendor price at N contacts × 12 × FX + extras + oversight hours × hourly. Outputs cost per held meeting, break-even meetings a month.
- 6 AI SDRs priced from their own pages (AiSDR, 11x, Reply Jason AI, Salesforge, HubSpot, Valley); 5 quote-only listed.
