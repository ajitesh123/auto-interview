# Brief: /tell-me-about-yourself (Part 2, job value, 22 Nov 2026)

**Keyword:** tell me about yourself answer / tell me about yourself for freshers / tell me about yourself generator / how long should tell me about yourself be.
**Why now:** queued as the next job tool since 16 Nov (STAR builder follow-up). It is the most-asked opening question; our only page was a blog post about freezing on it, full of unsourced stats.

## What the reader can do after the page

- Write a 60–90 second answer in three parts (present, past, future, or past first) for their stage (fresher, experienced, career switcher).
- See live: spoken length at 130/150/170 wpm or their measured pace, where the time goes, and 8 rule checks (all parts, length, no life story, no year-by-year CV recital, a number in the proof, ending on this job, skills from the job post, hedge words).
- Get a 30-second short version (first sentence of each part) and copy either.
- Read it aloud with a timer that measures their pace; then practise on Tough Tongue AI via the mock-interview CTA.

## Original asset

- A working no-AI builder (data/tmay/engine.ts; no key, no network, saved on device).
- Length guidance from 6 sources compared on one scale (RangeList) with exact quotes.
- What 8 career services/employers say, quoted with links (table).
- Weak vs strong fresher answer run through the same engine (2/8 vs 8/8 checks).
- 5 variants of the question with where each was seen.
