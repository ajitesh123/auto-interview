# Brief: The 12-week YC challenge (run a Y Combinator batch on your own)

- URL: /paul-graham/yc-batch and /paul-graham/yc-batch/week-1 … week-12
- Slot: one-off build requested by Rahul (Nov 9, 11:32 and 11:39 IST)
- Date: 2026-11-09

## 1. Who is searching and why

- Rahul: "A lot of content should be on what happens inside the YC batch... how 3 months of YC look...
  if someone is not joining YC but wants to do it on their own through this platform, they should be
  able to... week on week... if they explain a framework, you have to explain it... everything should
  be here... like a challenge: do you want to start this challenge? Each week a challenge."
- Queries: what happens at y combinator, yc batch week by week, yc group office hours, yc weekly
  update, yc demo day pitch, how long is yc, yc kickoff, startup school curriculum, how to set kpis
  startup, how to talk to users yc, how to plan an mvp, startup pricing 101, how to pitch your startup.
- Reader: founders and would-be founders (India + US) who will not get into YC this batch, or want to
  prepare for it.

## 2. What we already have

- /paul-graham/what-happens-at-yc (deal, batch facts) and /paul-graham/how-to-get-into-yc. The
  challenge extends them into a week-by-week program; it does not duplicate the lesson (linked both ways).

## 3. What ranks today

- YC's own pages describe the structure (kickoff, groups, office hours, talks, Demo Day) but publish no
  weekly calendar and no self-run version. Startup School is YC's free online course (weekly modules,
  updates) but is a separate platform. Founder blog posts describe single batches. Nothing combines the
  documented structure, founders' week-by-week accounts and the partners' frameworks in one program.

## 4. Information gain

- 12 weekly challenges, each with: what happens inside YC around that week (labelled documented vs
  reported, with named founders and batches), the partner framework fully explained with steps and a
  worked example, YC talks embedded, assignments with definitions of done, office-hours questions with
  answer boxes, a weekly update builder and growth tracker (5–7% band from PG's "Startup = Growth"),
  and a Demo Day pitch builder (60-second target sourced to YC's S22 post and 2024–2026 accounts).
- 23 frameworks, 66 assignments, 62 office-hours questions, all on our page.

## 5. Sources

- Each week lists every opened source. Two independent fact-checkers re-opened them (Nov 9): quotes
  from auto-captions de-quoted into paraphrase, founder/batch names corrected, our own additions
  labelled as ours, arithmetic recomputed (growth targets, funnel, default-alive cases, SAFE math),
  and where YC's own pages disagree (Demo Day audience ~1,500 vs ~1,000) both are stated.

## 6. Shape and engagement

- Hub asks "Do you want to start the challenge?"; start date, optional company name and one number;
  shows "Week N of 12" and this week's challenge; pause/restart, no streaks or guilt.
- Week page: challenge card first, then inside YC → frameworks → talks → assignments → office hours →
  weekly update → (weeks 11–12) pitch builder → checkpoint → FAQ → sources.

## 7. Review

- Critics: all 12 weeks pass after fixes. Rubric ~18/20 (fork). Playwright: start → week 1, pass
  persists, growth computed, pitch timer, no overflow at 390.

## 8. Measure

- Indexing by Nov 23; impressions for "yc batch", "what happens at yc", "yc demo day pitch" by Dec 7;
  challenge starts and week-page depth once GA4 access arrives.
