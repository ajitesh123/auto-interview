## Reader and job

Sales manager, enablement lead or founder who has to run a roleplay session this week (also an SDR practising alone). After the page they can: pick a scenario built on a real objection, hand out a buyer card and a rep card, run a timed round, score it on a 4-line rubric with defined levels, save the score, and rerun.
