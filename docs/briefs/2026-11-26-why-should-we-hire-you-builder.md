## What the reader can do after the page

1. Paste 2-4 needs from the job post and see which ones the answer names and which it proves (the "need map": Named + proved / Named only / Not in it yet).
2. Write a 30-60 s answer in three parts (Match, Proof, Value), timed out loud at their own measured pace.
3. Catch the 6 mistakes recruiters name, each a rule with the matched text shown: adjectives with no proof, comparing to other candidates, over-claiming, self-focus (learning opportunity, pay, dream company, reputed company), no number, no "what I'd do first".
4. Copy the answer and a short version; load 5 fictional samples (fresher SDE strong + weak, AE, teacher to CS, marketing intern).
