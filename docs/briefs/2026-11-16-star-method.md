# Brief: STAR Answer Builder (/star-method), 16 Nov 2026 (Part 2, job value)

## Gap

- "STAR method" is one of the most-searched interview-prep terms. Our STAR guide (`/blog/behavioral-interview-star-method-guide`) is a draft that 301s to /free-mock-interview, so the site has no STAR owner page. Our 14 behavioural question pages carry an old slider "STAR scorer" (seconds, not your words).
- SERP teardown (16 Nov): top results are articles (MIT CAPD 2023, BuiltIn 2024, Yoodli 2023, Glassdoor 2020) and AI generators that rewrite your text (Resume Worded, aiapply, withresumeai). Closest no-AI rival (techinterview.org) has no examples, FAQ, saving or coverage. No tool checks "I" vs "we", flags a Result with no number, shows the time split against a target, or keeps a story bank with a question coverage map. Length advice on the SERP ranges from "90 s" to "1–4 min" to "no fixed limit".

## What the reader can do after the page

1. Write one story in four boxes and see, live: time out loud at their pace, the S/T/A/R time split against MIT's 20/10/60/10, and 8 rule checks (parts, length, Action share, set-up share, I vs we, number in Result, learning line, hedge words).
2. Read it aloud with a timer to measure their own words per minute.
3. Save 3–5 stories on the device and see which of 12 common questions they cover (with Amazon's principles per question).
4. Copy speaker notes, then practise out loud in a free mock interview (Tough Tongue AI).
