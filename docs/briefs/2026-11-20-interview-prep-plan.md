# Brief: /interview-prep-plan (Part 2, 20 Nov 2026)

**Keyword targets:** interview preparation plan; how to prepare for an interview in 1 week / 3 days / 2 weeks; interview prep schedule; interview tomorrow what to do; day before interview checklist; India phrasing "how to prepare for interview in 7 days".

**What the reader can do after the page:** enter their interview date, role, rounds and real free time, and leave with a dated day-by-day plan they can tick off (saved on the device), a calendar file with one session per day, a list of what to add first if time is short, and day-of + after checklists. Every plan ends in a mock interview on Tough Tongue AI via /free-mock-interview.

**Original asset:** a working no-AI planner (data/prepPlan/engine.ts, pure functions). Ranked triage (pitch → job post → company → STAR stories → round set-up → mock → …), mocks reserved on the last study days, spaced "retell from memory" every other day, a light last day, per-role drill text and links to our role question banks, ?role= preselect from every hub.

**Gap it fills (SERP study 20 Nov 2026):** every ranking article is a fixed 7- or 30-day template (Leverage Edu, SchoolMyKids, Educative, HackingTheCase, TechPrep); none builds from the reader's date, hours and rounds; none is checkable with a calendar export; none cites learning research. Closest tool (scale.jobs) has no saving, ticks or calendar export.

**Sources (opened, quotes verified verbatim 20 Nov 2026):** Cepeda 2006 + 2008 (PubMed), APS page on Dunlosky 2013, Google re:Work structured interviewing, About Amazon interview guide (Apr 2025), Meta Careers SWE prep, McKinsey careers blog (Jenny Phung), NIH NHLBI sleep, Harvard FAS career services, Harvard Law School video interviewing PDF.

**Visuals:**

1. SegmentBar: where the time goes in the 1-week example (computed from the engine).
2. ColumnChart: hours said out loud by days to the interview (3/7/14/28), computed.
3. Rules table: rule → what the plan does → verbatim source quote + link.
4. Flow: the last 48 hours.
5. Ready-made plans (tomorrow / 3 days / 1 week / 2 weeks) rendered server-side from the engine, in `<details>`.

**CTA:** MockInterviewButton in the hero; MockInterviewCTA at the end; every plan includes "Full mock interview, out loud" linking /free-mock-interview.

**Honesty notes:** task minutes are our estimates (said on the page). No claim that the plan raises offer rates. Research cited only for what it says (spacing, practice testing, sleep, structured interviews).
